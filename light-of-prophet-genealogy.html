Hadith of the Light

The Full Genealogy

All forty-two generations from Adam to Muhammad ﷺ, traced through Bihar al-Anwar Volume 15.

The chain of the prophetic light runs through forty-two named generations, from the first human being to the Seal of the Prophets. Six of those names are prophets recognised by name in the Quran: Adam, Idris, Noah, Abraham, Ishmael, and Muhammad himself. The rest are the fathers who carried the light from one age to the next, across the Flood, across the migration to Mecca, across centuries of tribal history, until it reached its final resting place.

Notes on the Genealogy

Where the Named Prophets Fall

Six named prophets sit in the chain before Muhammad ﷺ: Adam, Seth, Idris, Noah, Abraham, and Ishmael. Each one is a moment where God renewed the covenant and the light passed from one chosen bearer to the next.

The Flood as a Turning Point

Noah ﷺ is the only prophet whose entire community was wiped out. The light bearer Sam, his son, carries the trust through the Flood. Every generation after Sam descends from the survivors of the Ark.

From Abraham to the Arabs

The Ishmaelite line is the one the Quran specifically connects to Mecca (14:37), where Abraham settles Hagar and Ishmael in a barren valley. Every name in the Ishmaelite branch is preserved in Arab tribal memory and genealogical record.

Forty-Two Generations Total

From Adam to Muhammad ﷺ, the chain runs to forty-two names across the entire arc of human history. Each name is a link in a chain of designated bearers, each one chosen by God to carry the light one step closer to its destination.
