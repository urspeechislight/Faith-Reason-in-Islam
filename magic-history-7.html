What Sorcery Costs the Sorcerer

The school's rulings on the sorcerer run from a repentance that must undo every knot to execution and the loss of Paradise, and the Qur'an's verse sets the hereafter price at no share at all. From Wasa'il al-Shia, al-Burhan, and Bihar al-Anwar.

The corpus prices the craft on three ledgers. The rulings of the Imams ﵈ price it in this world, in a repentance measured in undone knots and a penalty measured in the sorcerer's own life. The verse of Babylon prices it in the hereafter, at no share for whoever buys it. And the reports price it in the community, where the trade's one recorded product is the separation of a man from his wife.

The Rulings

The chapters of Wasa'il al-Shia open with a sorcerer who came to al-Sadiq ﷺ already repentant.

دخل عيسى بن شقفي ، على أبي عبد الله ﵇ وكان ساحرا يأتيه الناس ويأخذ على ذلك الاجر ، فقال له : جعلت فداك أنا رجل كانت صناعتي السحر وكنت آخذ عليه الاجر ، وكان معاشي وقد حججت منه ومن الله علي بلقائك ، وقد تبت إلى الله ﷿ : فهل لي في شئ من ذلك مخرج ؟ فقال له أبو عبد الله ﵇ : حل ولا تعقد

"Isa ibn Shaqfi came to Abu Abd Allah. He was a magician. People came to him, and he charged them for his work. He said, "May I be your ransom. I was a magician, and I earned money from it. It supported my living, and I performed the pilgrimage with that money. Allah blessed me by letting me meet you. I have repented to Allah. Is there any way out for me?" Abu Abd Allah replied, "Undo spells. Do not cast them.""

- al-Sadiq , Wasa'il al-Shia (al-Islamiyya), vol.12, p.105-106

The rule of repentance was then stated on its own.

قال : وروي : أن توبة الساحر أن يحل ولا يعقد

"It is reported that a magician repents by undoing spells and never casting them."

- Wasa'il al-Shia (al-Islamiyya), vol.12, p.106

The penalty in this world stands in the Prophet's ﷺ own ruling.

ساحر المسلمين يقتل وساحر الكفار لا يقتل ، قيل يا رسول الله لم لا يقتل ساحر الكفار ؟ قال : لان الشرك أعظم من السحر ، لان السحر والشرك مقرونان

"A Muslim sorcerer is put to death. A sorcerer among the disbelievers is not put to death. They asked Allah's Messenger, "Why is a sorcerer among the disbelievers not put to death?" He replied, "Associating others with Allah is worse than sorcery, for sorcery and associating others with Allah are joined together.""

- the Prophet , Wasa'il al-Shia (al-Islamiyya), vol.12, p.106

Ali ﷺ set the boundary of the crime itself.

من تعلم شيئا من السحر قليلا أو كثيرا فقد كفر ، وكان آخر عهده بربه وحده أن يقتل الا أن يتوب

"Whoever learns any amount of magic, little or great, has committed disbelief. His final bond with his Lord is that he be executed, unless he repents."

- Ali , Qurb al-Isnad, carried in Wasa'il al-Shia, vol.12, p.107

And the Prophet ﷺ named the three whom Paradise does not enter.

ثلاثة لا يدخلون الجنة : مدمن خمر ، ومدمن سحر ، وقاطع رحم

"Three people will not enter Paradise: one who is addicted to wine, one who is addicted to magic, and one who cuts family ties."

- the Prophet , al-Khisal, carried in Wasa'il al-Shia, vol.12, p.107

The House of the Prophet ﷺ stands measured against the crime in a narration the corpus preserves for the comparison itself.

نحن أهل بيت عصمنا الله من أن نكون فتانين أو كذا بين أو ساحرين أو زنائين ، فمن كان فيه شئ من هذه الخصال فليس منا ولا نحن منه

"We are a household whom Allah has protected from being tempters, liars, magicians, or adulterers. Whoever has any of these traits does not belong to us, and we do not belong to him."

- Ali , Tafsir Furat al-Kufi, carried in Wasa'il al-Shia, vol.12, p.108

Al-Hurr al-Amili closed the chapter with his own note on what undoing a spell may use.

أقول : خصه بعض علمائنا بالحل بغير السحر كالقرآن والذكر والتعويذ ونحوها ، وهو حسن إذ لا تصريح بجواز الحل بالسحر

"Some of our scholars limited this to undoing spells through means other than magic, such as the Quran, remembrance of Allah, supplications for protection, and similar means. This is sound, because no clear wording permits undoing spells through magic."

- al-Hurr al-Amili, Wasa'il al-Shia (al-Islamiyya), vol.12, p.106

The Wound in the Community

The Qur'an names the trade's product, the separation of spouses, and the corpus explains the mechanism. The Askari tafsir describes the working.

يتعلمون التفريق بضروب من الحيل والتمائم (2) ، والإيهام أنه قد دفن كذا وعمل كذا ، ليغضب (3) قلب المرأة على (4) الرجل ، وقلب الرجل على (5) المرأة ، ويؤدي إلى الفراق بينهما

"They learn to separate people through many tricks and amulets. They make people believe that they buried something or performed some act, so the woman's heart grows angry with the man and the man's heart grows angry with the woman. This leads to their separation."

- Tafsir al-Imam al-Askari , carried in al-Burhan, vol.1, p.354

The limit of every such harm is set in the same tafsir.

بتخلية الله وعلمه ، فإنه لو شاء لمنعهم بالجبر والقهر

"Allah leaves them free to act, while knowing what they do. If He wished, He could stop them by force and power."

- Tafsir al-Imam al-Askari , carried in al-Burhan, vol.1, p.354

Al-Majlisi read the separation verse in two ways.

فيه وجهان : أحدهما أن يكونوا يغوون أحد الزوجين ويحملونه على الشرك بالله تعالى ، فيكون بذلك قد فارق زوجه الآخر المؤمن المقيم على دينه ، ليفرق بينهما اختلاف النحلة والملة ، والوجه الآخر أن يسعوا بين الزوجين بالنميمة والوشاية والاغراء والتمويه بالباطل حتى يؤول أمرهما إلى الفرقة والمباينة

"There are two explanations. First, they led one spouse astray and pushed that person into associating others with God. That spouse then left the other believing spouse, who remained firm in the religion. Their different faiths separated them. Second, they spread gossip, false accusations, incitement, and deceptive lies between the spouses until they separated and went their own ways."

- al-Majlisi, Bihar al-Anwar, vol.56, p.272

And he recorded the practice of Solomon's ﷺ own law on the matter.

ويقوي هذا الوجه ما روي أنه كان من دين سليمان أنه من سحر بانت منه امرأته

"Solomon's religious law held that a sorcerer's wife was separated from him. This supports that interpretation."

- al-Majlisi, Bihar al-Anwar, vol.56, p.274

The Price in the Hereafter

The verse of Babylon set the hereafter price, and the Askari tafsir explains who the buyers of the craft were held to be.

لأن المتعلمين لهذا السحر هم الذين يعتقدون أن لا رسول ، ولا إله ، ولا بعث ، ولا نشور

"Those who learn this kind of magic believe that there is no messenger, no god, no resurrection, and no rising after death."

- Tafsir al-Imam al-Askari , carried in al-Burhan, vol.1, p.354

One further power is granted and bounded in a single ruling already carried in Part 1, that the sorcerers were given power over nothing except the eye, and al-Majlisi's weighing of the harm verse closes the ledger.

قال أبو جعفر الباقر ﵇ : إن السحرة لم يسلطوا على شئ إلا العين

"Abu Jafar al-Baqir said: "Magicians have power over nothing except the evil eye.""

- al-Baqir , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.26

Commentary

A Repentance Measured in Knots

Source: Wasa'il al-Shia vol.12 p.105-108

Claim: the sorcerer's repentance is to undo spells and never cast them, and the sorcerer of the Muslims is executed

Speaker: al-Sadiq ﷺ; the Prophet ﷺ; Ali ﷺ; al-Hurr al-Amili

Isa ibn Shaqafi had made his living from the craft, and his question to al-Sadiq ﷺ was the question of a tradesman, whether a way out remained. The Imam's ﷺ answer made the exit physical. A sorcerer repents by untying, never by binding, which means the repentance is complete only when the last knot of the old work is undone. The chapters around it set the border of the crime, that learning any of it is disbelief with the death penalty unless repented, and the three barred from Paradise stand with sorcery between drunkenness and the severing of kin. Al-Hurr al-Amili's note guards the exit itself. The undoing must use means outside the craft, the Qur'an, the remembrance of God, and the ta'widh, because no wording permits undoing one spell by working another.

The One Product of the Trade

Source: al-Burhan vol.1 p.354; Bihar al-Anwar vol.56 p.272 and p.274

Claim: the craft's worked harm is the separation of spouses, by tricks, amulets, and the illusion of buried work

Speaker: Tafsir al-Imam al-Askari ﵈; al-Majlisi

The corpus is exact about what sorcery sells. The Askari tafsir names the mechanics, tricks and amulets and the suggestion that something was buried and worked, so that anger grows between husband and wife. Al-Majlisi's two readings of the verse widen the wound, that the separation is either the making of an unbeliever out of one spouse or the carrying of tales between the two. Solomon's ﷺ law, that the sorcerer's own wife left him, shows the wound returning to its maker. And the limit stands over all of it, that no harm lands except by God's letting-be, which the tafsir reads as His knowledge, since He could prevent it by force.

No Share in the Hereafter

Source: al-Burhan vol.1 p.354; Bihar al-Anwar vol.60 p.26

Claim: the buyers of sorcery, in the tafsir's reading, are deniers of the messenger, the deity, and the rising, and they hold no share in the Hereafter

Speaker: Tafsir al-Imam al-Askari ﵈; al-Baqir ﷺ

The hereafter price is the verse's own word, no share, and the tafsir identifies the buyers as people whose craft rests on a creed, that there is no messenger, no god, and no rising. The sorcerer's working capital, in this reading, is the denial of the One who audits. The grave has no separate recorded penalty for the sorcerer in this corpus, and this series reports that plainly, that the school's recorded prices are the undoing, the execution, the exclusion from Paradise, and the transformed shapes of the old reports. The eye remains the one granted power, and the verse's own clause bounds every claimed harm, that nothing lands except by God's letting-be. The school's recorded position keeps the prophets and Imams ﵈ outside sorcery's reach, and the believer stands inside that clause with a shield in hand, which is the corpus's whole answer to what the craft can and cannot reach.

What the Ledger Leaves Open

The ledger closes with one standing command, that knots already tied must be untied, and the untying needs instruments. The corpus keeps a whole shelf of them, two suras that came down at a well, a ta'widh taught by Jibril, a sevenfold word of Ali ﷺ, and the practices of the Imams ﵈ against the eye and the seizure. That shelf is the last part.
