Hadith of the Light

Part 1: Before All Things

Bihar al-Anwar, Volume 15, page 27. Narrated from Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇.

Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇ said:

God was, and nothing was with Him. The first thing He created was the light of His beloved Muhammad ﷺ, four hundred and twenty-four thousand years before He created the water, the Throne, the Footstool, the heavens and the earth, the Tablet and the Pen, Paradise and the Fire, the angels, Adam, and Eve.

When God created the light of our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, it stood before Him for a thousand years, glorifying and praising Him. And the Truth, blessed and exalted, looked upon it and said:

"My servant, you are the one sought and the one who seeks. You are My chosen one out of all I have made. By My might and majesty, had it not been for you, I would not have created the heavens. Whoever loves you, I love. Whoever turns against you, I turn against."

Then his light flashed and its rays shot upward, and from it God made twelve veils.

The veils in order: the Veil of Power, then Greatness, then Honour, then Awe, then Sovereignty, then Mercy, then Prophethood, then Grandeur, then Rank, then Loftiness, then Bliss, then Intercession.

Then God commanded the light to pass through each veil in turn, dwelling in each for a set span, glorifying God with a phrase unique to that station:

  • Power: "Glory to the High, the Most High" for 12,000 years.
  • Greatness: "Glory to the One who knows the secret and what is more hidden still" for 11,000 years.
  • Honour: "Glory to the King, the Giver of all gifts" for 10,000 years.
  • Awe: "Glory to the One who is rich and never in need" for 9,000 years.
  • Sovereignty: "Glory to the Generous, the Most Generous" for 8,000 years.
  • Mercy: "Glory to the Lord of the Mighty Throne" for 7,000 years.
  • Prophethood: "Glory to your Lord, the Lord of Honour, far above what they ascribe to Him" for 6,000 years.
  • Grandeur: "Glory to the Great, the Greatest" for 5,000 years.
  • Rank: "Glory to the All-Knowing, the Generous" for 4,000 years.
  • Loftiness: "Glory to the One who makes all things pass away and never passes Himself" for 2,000 years.
  • Intercession: "Glory be to God and praise be to Him. Glory to God the Great" for 1,000 years.

Commentary

"God was, and nothing was with Him"

This opening line is one of the most quoted phrases in Islamic theology. It is a well-known hadith on its own, found in both Sunni and Shi'i sources, used to assert God's absolute pre-existence. Nothing shared eternity with Him. Whatever else exists, exists because He brought it into being. The narration uses this line as the starting point for everything that follows. Before there was anything, there was only God. And then, before anything else, came the light of the Prophet.

The Huge Numbers

The figure of 424,000 years before the rest of creation is not meant to be read as a literal stopwatch count. In hadith literature dealing with what came before time as we know it, large numbers function as a way of conveying immensity, priority, and depth. The point is that the Prophet's reality precedes everything else in creation by an unimaginable stretch.

The Prophet as Goal of Creation

The line "had it not been for you, I would not have created the heavens" appears in several narrations across the tradition, often called the hadith lawlaka. It is theologically loaded. It places the Prophet ﷺ as the reason creation exists at all. In Imami thought this extends to the Prophet's family as well, since their lights are described in other hadiths as drawn from his. The wider doctrine is that the cosmos is a stage built for the appearance of the perfect human, and the perfect human, in his fullest form, is Muhammad ﷺ.

The Twelve Veils

Notice the symbolism in the count. Twelve veils, matching the twelve Imams in Imami theology. Each veil is a name of a divine attribute (Power, Greatness, Honour, and so on), and each one is a stage the Prophet's light passes through, learning to glorify God under that specific aspect. By the end, the Prophet's light has known God by twelve different names, each one for thousands of years.

The Descending Years

The pattern is deliberate. Twelve thousand, eleven thousand, ten thousand, nine thousand, and down. The longest stretch is the first, in the Veil of Power. The shortest is the last, in the Veil of Intercession. One traditional reading is that the descent matches a movement from the most hidden divine attribute (raw Power) to the most relational one (Intercession, which is about the Prophet's role with creation). Another is that the Prophet's light grows more refined as it goes, so each stage takes less time to absorb.

How to Read This Responsibly

This narration sits in a category of hadiths that are theological and cosmological rather than legal. Its chain is not strong by the standards of strict hadith criticism, and it is classified by most scholars among the fada'il and khasa'is literature. Allama Majlisi includes it because his project is to preserve, not to filter. Some scholars take it as literal pre-existential history. Others read it symbolically. Both readings are present in the tradition.
