The Shield of the Believer

The two shelter suras that came down at the well close this history with the instruments of its undoing. They stand with the ta'widhs of Jibril and Ali ﵇, the practices of the Imams ﵈ against the eye and the seizure, and the formula that foiled a king's sorcerer. From Bihar al-Anwar, Man La Yahduruhu al-Faqih, and the two Qisas al-Anbiya books.

The history of magic ends in the hands of the believer. It began with words stolen from heaven, and it ends with words given from heaven. Two suras came down at a knotted cord, and a sheltering invocation Jibril spoke over two children of the Prophet's ﷺ house. A sevenfold word stands against sorcerers and tyrants, with practices the Imams ﵈ used against the eye and the seizure. The corpus also preserves the end of the argument, a prophet under a sorcerer's poison who was kept by the name of God while the sorcerer himself surrendered his craft.

The Two Suras and Their Status

The two shelter suras carry their own origin story, and al-Qummi's narration sets it simply.

1 - تفسير علي بن إبراهيم : أبي ، عن بكر بن محمد ، عن أبي عبد الله ﵇ قال : كان سبب نزول المعوذتين أنه وعك رسول الله ﵌ فنزل عليه جبرئيل بهاتين السورتين فعوذه بهما (2)

"Tafsir Ali ibn Ibrahim relates, from my father, from Bakr ibn Muhammad, from Abu Abd Allah, who said, the reason the two protective surahs were revealed was that the Messenger of Allah fell ill. Gabriel came down with these two surahs and used them to protect him.

- al-Sadiq , Tafsir al-Qummi, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.89, p.365

Their place in the Book had to be defended. Al-Baqir ﵇ was asked about the companion who erased them.

2 - تفسير علي بن إبراهيم : علي بن الحسين ، عن البرقي ، عن علي بن الحكم ، عن ابن عميرة عن الحضرمي قال : قلت لأبي جعفر ﵇ إن ابن مسعود كان يمحو المعوذتين من المصحف ، فقال ﵇ : كان أبي يقول : إنما فعل ذلك ابن مسعود برأيه ، وهما من القرآن (1) .

"Tafsir Ali ibn Ibrahim relates, from Ali ibn al-Husayn, from al-Barqi, from Ali ibn al-Hakam, from Ibn Umayra, from al-Hadrami, who said, I said to Abu Jafar, "Ibn Mas'ud used to erase the two protective surahs from the manuscript." He replied, "My father used to say, Ibn Mas'ud did that on his own opinion, and they are part of the Quran."

- al-Baqir , Tafsir al-Qummi, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.89, p.365-366

Al-Sadiq ﵇ answered the same doubt in his own era.

أخطأ ابن مسعود أو قال : كذب ابن مسعود ، هما من القرآن . قال الرجل : فأقرأ بهما يا ابن رسول الله في المكتوبة ؟ قال : نعم ، وهل تدري ما معنى المعوذتين وفي أي شئ نزلتا ؟ إن رسول الله ﵌ سحره لبيد بن أعصم اليهودي

"Ibn Masud was mistaken, or he lied. Both are part of the Qur'an. The man asked, "May I recite them in the obligatory prayer?" Al-Sadiq said, "Yes. And do you know what the two protective surahs mean, and on what occasion they came down? Labid ibn al-Asam the Jew cast a spell on the Messenger of Allah."

- al-Sadiq , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.89, p.368

And the promise attached to them stands in the reward books.

3 - ثواب الأعمال : أبي ، عن أحمد بن إدريس ، عن الأشعري ، عن محمد بن حسان ، عن ابن مهران ، عن ابن البطائني ، عن ابن أبي العلاء ، عن أبي عبيدة الحذاء ، عن أبي جعفر ﵇ قال : من أوتر بالمعوذتين وقل هو الله أحد قيل له : يا عبد الله أبشر فقد قبل الله وترك (2)

"Thawab al-A'mal relates, through the chain from Abu Ubayda al-Hadha', from Abu Jafar, whoever performs the witr prayer with the two protective surahs and the sura of Unity will be told, "Servant of Allah, rejoice. Allah has accepted your prayer and forgiven you."

- al-Baqir , Thawab al-A'mal, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.89, p.366

The Practices of the House

The Tibb of the Imams carries the Prophet's ﷺ own use of the opening chapter and the two suras.

4 - طب الأئمة : أحمد بن زياد ، عن فضالة ، عن إسماعيل بن أبي زياد ، عن الصادق ﵇ قال : كان رسول الله ﵌ إذا كسل أو أصابته عين أو صداع بسط يديه فقرأ فاتحة الكتاب والمعوذتين ثم يمسح بهما وجهه ، فيذهب عنه ما كان يجد (3)

"Tibb al-A'imma relates, from Ahmad ibn Ziyad, from Fadala, from Ismail ibn Abi Ziyad, from al-Sadiq, who said, when the Messenger of Allah felt weak, suffered from the evil eye, or had a headache, he spread out his hands, recited the Opening of the Book and the two protective surahs, then wiped his face with them, and what he felt would leave him.

- al-Sadiq , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.89, p.366

Al-Rida ﷺ met a seizure with recited water, and promised what he promised.

5 - طب الأئمة : عن أبي الحسن الرضا ﵇ أنه رأى مصروعا فدعا له بقدح فيه ماء ثم قرأ عليه الحمد والمعوذتين ، ونفث في القدح ثم أمر فصب الماء على رأسه ووجهه فأفاق وقال له : لا يعود إليك أبدا (4)

"Tibb al-A'imma relates that Abu al-Hasan al-Rida saw a man having a seizure. He called for a cup of water, recited the Opening and the two protective surahs over it, blew into the cup, then ordered the water poured over the man's head and face. The man recovered, and al-Rida said to him, it will never return to you."

- al-Rida , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.89, p.366

The night practice is recorded in al-Saduq's legal collection.

ومن أصابه فزع عند منامه فليقرأ إذا أوى إلى فراشه المعوذتين وآية الكرسي

"Whoever feels fear when going to sleep should recite the two protective surahs and the Throne Verse after lying down."

- al-Sadiq , Man La Yahduruhu al-Faqih, vol.1, p.502

The sheltering invocation beneath it is the one Jibril spoke over the Prophet's ﷺ grandsons.

أعيذ نفسي وذريتي وأهل بيتي ومالي بكلمات الله التامات من كل شيطان وهامة ومن كل عين لامة " (1) فذلك الذي عوذ به جبرئيل ﵇ الحسن والحسين ﵉

"I seek Allah's protection for myself, my children, my household, and my wealth through Allah's perfect words, from every devil, every harmful creature, and every evil eye. Jibril used these words to protect al-Hasan and al-Husayn."

- al-Sadiq , Man La Yahduruhu al-Faqih, vol.1, p.502

Ali ﵇ left a sevenfold word aimed at the sorcerer and the tyrant together.

هذه عوذة السحر والخوف من السلطان ، تقولها سبع مرات : " بسم الله وبالله ، سنشد عضدك بأخيك ونجعل لكما سلطانا فلا يصلون إليكما بآياتنا أنتما ومن اتبعكما الغالبون " وتقوله في وجه الساحر (1) إذا فرغت من صلاة الليل قبل أن تبدأ بصلاة النهار سبع مرات فإنه لا يضرك إنشاء الله تعالى

"This is a protection against sorcery and fear of the ruler. Say it seven times, "In the name of Allah and through Allah. We will strengthen you through your brother and give both of you authority. They will not reach either of you through Our signs. You and those who follow you will prevail." Say it facing the sorcerer seven times after finishing the night prayer and before beginning the daytime prayer. Allah willing, it will not harm you."

- Ali , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.92, p.127

Commentary

A Sura Fought Over

Source: Bihar al-Anwar vol.89 p.365-366, vol.92 p.128

Claim: the two suras are of the Qur'an by the words of two Imams, against a companion's erasure of them

Speaker: al-Sadiq ﵇; al-Baqir ﵇

The shield had to be defended before it could be used. Ibn Mas'ud had removed the two suras from his copy, and the corpus preserves both Imams' ﵈ answers across two books, that he acted on his own opinion, that he erred, and, in one recension, the blunter word. The fight matters practically. A shelter recited at a well only shields if it is revelation, and the Imams ﵈ staked its status in the strongest terms, with al-Sadiq ﵇ ordering its recitation in the obligatory prayers themselves. The fever origin and the witr promise then round the suras' story, given from heaven for a Prophet's ﷺ need, and promised acceptance for everyone who prays them.

The House at Work

Source: Tibb al-A'imma and Man La Yahduruhu al-Faqih, in Bihar al-Anwar vol.89-92

Claim: the recorded protections are recitation, water, the wiping of the face, and spoken shelter, aimed at the eye, fear, and the seizure

Speaker: al-Sadiq ﵇; al-Rida ﵇

The practices share one shape. None of them is a counter-spell, and none is worked as the craft works. The Prophet ﷺ recited and wiped his face, al-Rida ﵇ recited over water and poured it, and the believer at bedtime recites the two suras with the Throne Verse and speaks the sheltering words over his children and his wealth.

The one aggressive instrument in the shelf is Ali's ﵇ sevenfold word, and even that is speech, said to the face of the sorcerer, after the night prayer. The eye remains the standing danger, and the standing answer is God's own words. Counter-work has no place in it, exactly as the repentance rule of Part 7 requires, that knots are untied, never retied.

The Name That Undid the Craft

Source: Qisas al-Anbiya p.239-240; al-Nur al-Mubin p.451-452

Claim: sorcery employed to its full against a prophet failed before the name of God, and the sorcerer converted

Speaker: al-Rawandi; al-Jaza'iri

Jurayj's story is the series in miniature. The king sends every sorcerer he has, the craft is worked to its limit, and the poison is neutralized by a sentence of the name of God, the formula the story itself preserves. The sorcerer, a professional who knew exactly what failure meant, then pronounces the verdict of Part 2 again from his own side, testifying that the power in front of him is real and his own is spent.

The king answers with the old word, sorcerer, and the story closes with the prophet's prayer that his name become a recourse in every later terror. That prayer is why the folk invocations of the centuries after him carry a saint's name inside them, and the corpus shows where the custom began. The two story-books named at the head of this history stand here at its end, carrying the same text almost word for word, the older author held inside the younger.

Where the Shield Stands

Magic began as a theft of words above the sky, and it ends as given words in a believer's mouth. Between the two stand the contests and the verdicts. They carry the cleared name of Solomon ﵇, the weighed story of two angels at Babylon, the split moon and the knotted well, and the ledger of what the craft costs the one who practices it. One heirloom of the contests remains in the House's keeping, the staff prepared for the Qa'im, as the part on Egypt recorded.

The recited word shields the believer now, and the waiting staff waits with the Imam, and those two are the endings the corpus holds open. The history closes the way it opened, with heaven's speech, this time handed over, recited, and kept. The believer who sleeps behind the two suras holds the shield, and the shield holds until the last trial, which is the final part of this series.
