The Second Expedition of Badr

Abu Sufyan set a rematch appointment at Badr, Badr al-Maw'id, as he left Uhud, and when the year turned the Muslims kept the appointment while Quraysh stayed home in a drought year and earned the name army of sawiq, the barley drink of the road. The Muslims kept the word and won the ground by showing up. Part 14 of 28 in The Battles of the Prophet.

The Appointment Kept

As Abu Sufyan left the field of Uhud, he called a rematch across the valley, an appointment at Badr al-Safra, the fair ground of Badr, at the turn of the year. The Prophet ﷺ ordered Umar to accept it. The year turned, the Muslims marched to the appointment, and Quraysh, the ruling tribe of Mecca, came to the edge of marching and then used a drought year to talk itself home. The Muslims waited eight nights at the fair ground and came back without a battle.▼ commentary▼ commentary

Al-Waqidi's entry opens with the dating and the deputy, and then carries the full chain, with the market at the heart of the story.

غزوة بدر الموعد وكانت لهلال ذي القعدة على رأس خمسةٍ وأربعين شهراً وغاب رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلّم فيها ست عشرة ليلة ورجع إلى المدينة لأربع عشرة بقيت من ذي القعدة واستخلف على المدينة ابن رواحة

حدثني الضحاك بن عثمان ومحمد بن عمرو الأنصاري وموسى بن محمد بن إبراهيم بن الحارث وأبو بكر بن عبد الله بن محمد بن أبي سبرة ومعمر بن راشد وأبو معشر وعبد الله بن جعفر ومحمد بن عبد الله بن مسلم وعبد الحميد بن جعفر وابن أبي حبيبة ومحمد بن يحيى بن سهل وكل قد حدثني بطائفة من هذا الحديث وغيرهم ممن لم أسم قالوا : لما أراد أبو سفيان أن ينصرف يوم أحد نادى : موعدٌ بيننا وبينكم بدر الصفراء رأس الحول نلتقي فيه فنقتتل . فقال رسول الله صلى الله عليه وسلّم لعمر بن الخطاب ﵁ : قل نعم إن شاء الله .

ويقال قال أبو سفيان يومئذ : موعدكم بدر الصفراء بعد شهرين . قال ابن واقد : والأول أثبت عندنا . فافترق الناس على ذلك ورجعت قريش فخبروا من قبلهم بالموعد وتهيئوا للخروج وأجلبوا وكان هذا عندهم أعظم الأيام لأنهم رجعوا من أحد والدولة لهم طمعوا في بدر الموعد أيضاً بمثل ذلك من الظفر . وكان بدر الصفراء مجمعاً يجتمع فيه العرب وسوقاً تقوم لهلال ذي القعدة إلى ثمان ليالٍ خلون منه فإذا مضت ثماني ليال منه تفرق الناس إلى بلادهم .

"The expedition of Badr al-Maw'id. It fell at the crescent of Dhu al-Qa'da, forty five months on. The Messenger of Allah was away in it sixteen nights, and he returned to Medina with fourteen nights of Dhu al-Qa'da left, having put Ibn Rawaha in charge of Medina. Al-Dahhak ibn Uthman, Muhammad ibn Amr al-Ansari, Musa ibn Muhammad ibn Ibrahim ibn al-Harith, Abu Bakr ibn Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Abi Sabrah, Ma'mar ibn Rashid, Abu Mu'shar, Abd Allah ibn Ja'far, Muhammad ibn Abd Allah ibn Muslim, Abd al-Hamid ibn Ja'far, Ibn Abi Habibah, Muhammad ibn Yahya ibn Sahl, and others whose names I have not mentioned, all narrated parts of this tradition to me. They said. When Abu Sufyan wanted to leave on the day of Uhud, he called out, 'An appointment between us and you at Badr al-Safra, at the turn of the year, where we will meet and fight.' The Messenger of Allah said to Umar ibn al-Khattab, 'Say, yes, if Allah wills.' It is also said that Abu Sufyan said that day, 'Your appointment is Badr al-Safra after two months.' Al-Waqidi said. The first report is more confirmed with us. So the people dispersed on that basis. Quraysh returned and informed those behind them of the appointment. They prepared to march out and gathered allies. This was the greatest day for them because they had returned from Uhud with the upper hand, and they hoped for a similar victory at the appointed Badr. Badr al-Safra was a gathering place where Arabs met. It was a fair that stood for the crescent of Dhu al-Qa'dah until eight nights had passed. When eight nights passed, the people dispersed to their lands."

- al-Waqidi, al-Maghazi, vol.1, p.388, with the full chain

Al-Maqrizi fixes the cause and the answer.▼ commentary

غزوة بدر الموعد ثم كانت غزوة بدر الموعد لهلال ذي القعدة على رأس خمسة وأربعين شهرا . وسببها أن أبا سفيان بن حرب لما أراد أن ينصرف يوم أحد نادى : موعد بيننا وبينكم بدر الصفراء رأس الحول نلتقي فيه فنقتتل . فقال عمر بن الخطاب ﵁ - وقد أمره رسول الله ﵌ - : نعم ، إن شاء الله .

"The expedition of Badr al-Maw'id came at the crescent of Dhu al-Qa'da, forty five months on. Its cause was that when Abu Sufyan ibn Harb made to leave the field on the day of Uhud, he called out, 'An appointment between us and you at Badr al-Safra, at the turn of the year, where we meet and fight.' Umar ibn al-Khattab said, and the Messenger of Allah had ordered him to answer, 'Yes, if Allah wills.'"

- al-Maqrizi, Imta' al-Asma', vol.1, p.238

The Shia encyclopedist carries Abu Sufyan's disinformation campaign, and the scene where the excuse was staged.▼ commentary

ولما دنا الموعد كره أبو سفيان الخروج إلى رسول اللّه وأحبّ أن يقيم رسول اللّه وأصحابه بالمدينة لا يوافون الموعد ، فكان كل من يرد عليه مكة يريد المدينة يظهر له : أنا نريد أن نغزو محمدا في جمع كثيف !

وقدم نعيم بن مسعود الأشجعي مكة ، فجاءه أبو سفيان بن حرب في رجال من قريش وقال له : يا نعيم ، إنّي وعدت محمدا وأصحابه يوم أحد أن نلتقي نحن وهو ببدر الصفراء على رأس الحوس ، وقد جاء ذلك . فقال نعيم : ما أقدمني إلّا ما رأيت محمدا وأصحابه يصنعون من إعداد السلاح والكراع ، قد تجلّب إليه حلفاء الأوس من بليّ وجهينة وغيرهم ، فتركت المدينة أمس وهي كالرمّانة ! فقال أبو سفيان : أسمعك تذكر ما تذكر وما قد أعدّوا ، وهذا عام جدب ، وإنّما يصلحنا عام خصب غيداق ترعى فيه الظهر والخيل ونشرب اللبن ، وأنا أكره أن يخرج محمد وأصحابه ولا أخرج فيجترءون علينا

"When the appointed time drew near, Abu Sufyan hated going out against the Messenger of Allah, and he would have preferred that the Messenger of Allah and his companions stay in Medina and miss the appointment. So everyone leaving Mecca for Medina would show himself saying, 'We mean to raid Muhammad with a great host.' Nu'aym ibn Mas'ud al-Ashja'i came to Mecca, and Abu Sufyan ibn Harb came to him with men of Quraysh and said, 'Nu'aym, I promised Muhammad and his companions on the day of Uhud that we would meet, they and we, at Badr al-Safra at the turn of the year, and it has come.' Nu'aym said, 'Nothing brought me here except what I saw Muhammad and his companions doing to ready weapons and mounts. The allies of the Aws, of Bali and Juhayna and others, have streamed to him, and I left Medina yesterday and it was like a pomegranate packed full.' Abu Sufyan said, 'I hear what you say of what they have made ready, but this is a year of drought. What suits us is a year of rich pasture where the beasts and the horses graze and we drink milk, and I hate that Muhammad and his companions should come out while I stay home, for then they would grow bold against us.'"

- al-Yusufi al-Gharawi, Mawsu'at al-Tarikh al-Islami, vol.2, p.459, quoting al-Waqidi

خروج الرسول قال ابن إسحاق : ثم خرج في شعبان إلى بدر ، لميعاد أبي سفيان ، حتى نزله .

استعماله ابن أبىّ على المدينة قال ابن هشام : واستعمل على المدينة عبد اللّه بن عبد اللّه بن أبىّ بن سلول الأنصارىّ .

رجوع أبي سفيان في رجاله قال ابن إسحاق : فأقام عليه ثماني ليال ينتظر أبا سفيان ، وخرج أبو سفيان في أهل مكّة حتى نزل مجنّة ، من ناحية الظّهران ، وبعض الناس يقول : قد بلغ عسفان ، ثم بدا له في الرجوع ، فقال : يا معشر قريش ، إنه لا يصلحكم إلّا عام خصيب ترعون فيه الشّجر ، وتشربون فيه اللين ، وإنّ عامكم هذا عام جدب ، وإني راجع ، فارجعوا فرجع الناس . فسماهم أهل مكة جيش السّويق ، يقولون : إنما خرجتم تشربون السّويق .

"Ibn Ishaq said. Then he went out in Sha'ban to Badr, for the appointment of Abu Sufyan, and he camped there. Ibn Hisham said. He put Abd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Abi ibn Salul of the Ansar in charge of Medina. Ibn Ishaq said. He stayed on it eight nights, waiting for Abu Sufyan. Abu Sufyan went out with the people of Mecca until he camped at Majanna, on the side of Dharan, and some say he reached Usfan, and then the thought of turning back came to him. He said, 'Company of Quraysh, nothing sets you right but a fertile year, in which you graze on the trees and drink the fresh milk, and this year of yours is a year of drought. I am turning back, so turn back.' And the people turned back. The people of Mecca named them the army of sawiq, saying, 'You only went out to drink sawiq.'"

- Ibn Ishaq via al-Suhayli, Rawd al-Unaf, vol.6, p.228, with Ibn Hisham's deputy note

The outcome, in the Shia line.▼ commentary

خرج رسول اللّه في سبعين راكبا ، وأتى موسم بدر ، فكفاهم اللّه بأس العدو ، ولم يوافهم أبو سفيان ، ولم يكن قتال يومئذ ، وانصرف رسول اللّه بمن معه سالمين (1) .

"The Messenger of Allah went out with seventy riders. He reached the Badr season. Allah spared them the enemy's harm. Abu Sufyan did not meet them. There was no fighting that day. The Messenger of Allah returned with his companions safe."

Commentary

The Sources for the Second Badr

Source spine: al-Waqidi, al-Maghazi, vol.1, p.388, with the full chain

Other carriers: al-Maqrizi, Imta' al-Asma', vol.1, p.238. al-Suhayli, Rawd al-Unaf, vol.6, p.228, carrying Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Hisham

Shia line: al-Yusufi al-Gharawi, Mawsu'at al-Tarikh al-Islami, vol.2, p.459, quoting al-Waqidi

Dating note: Ibn Ishaq's line puts the march in Sha'ban, al-Waqidi's at the crescent of Dhu al-Qa'da, and one carrier gives a two month variant of the challenge itself

The carriers agree on the frame, the challenge issued at Uhud, the Muslims keeping it, Quraysh abandoning it, and Mecca laughing at its own army. The month splits, Sha'ban against Dhu al-Qa'da. The deputy splits with it, Ibn Rawaha in al-Waqidi and Ibn Ubayy's son Abd Allah in Ibn Hisham, and both splits stay in view. Al-Waqidi's standing as a transmitter is weighed on the series index.

The Appointment and the Disinformation

The challenge: an appointment at Badr al-Safra at the turn of the year, issued by Abu Sufyan at Uhud

The answer: yes, if Allah wills, said on the Prophet's ﷺ order

The fair: Badr al-Safra hosted an Arab market for the first eight nights of Dhu al-Qa'da

The campaign: travelers leaving Mecca were staged to cry that a great host was coming against Muhammad

The messenger: Nu'aym ibn Mas'ud brought Abu Sufyan the picture of Medina packed like a pomegranate

The appointment was Abu Sufyan's own, and the record of its undoing belongs to him. When the year turned he hated the march, worked the road with rumors, and heard Nu'aym ibn Mas'ud's report of a Medina packed like a pomegranate.

The carriers quoted here do not say whose man Nu'aym was, sent, hired, or come on his own. They carry only what he told Abu Sufyan. Abu Sufyan then stood before Quraysh with a speech about drought and pasture, and Mecca's own mockery named the result, the army of sawiq, an army that marched to drink.

The Keeping and the Outcome

Commander: the Prophet ﷺ, with seventy riders per the Shia line's outcome. Deputy over Medina, Ibn Rawaha per al-Waqidi, Abd Allah ibn Abd Allah ibn Ubayy per Ibn Hisham

Stay: eight nights at the fair ground, and sixteen nights away in all, per al-Waqidi

Contact: none. No fighting, no dead on either side

Result: the Muslims attended the fair and came home safe

Tactically the day was a forfeit won by attendance. The side that bragged of a rematch stayed home, and the side it had beaten kept the appointment with the market open. What the record carries of the wider reading is Mecca's own mockery, the army of sawiq, and Abu Sufyan's stated fear that staying home would let the Muslims grow bold against his people. No carrier reports how the tribes at large read the exchange, and no one was killed.

Was the Second Expedition of Badr Justified

The march answered a standing challenge the enemy had set in the hour of its victory. Abu Sufyan's own stated fear was that staying home would let the Muslims grow bold against his people. That fear shows what the appointment meant to the side that set it.

The rumors Abu Sufyan seeded on the road show how much he wanted the Muslims to miss the appointment. The Muslims marched to the appointed ground, waited the agreed term, and came home.

The confrontation could have been prevented. Prevention lay with Abu Sufyan twice over. He set the challenge, and he abandoned the march he had promised. The Muslims' only alternative to keeping the appointment was breaking their word, and the sources record no counsel to break it.
