  • Flipped negation (all 5 advisors): the c4 fact card read "Quraysh: their dead mutilated by their own women", reversing the page's own evidence. Fixed to "their women mutilated the Muslim dead".

  • 3:155 pardon tension (unanimous fatal flaw): the page leaned on a verse ending "Allah has pardoned them" while indicting the flight. Fixed with the pardon/record distinction in c2.
