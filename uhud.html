Battle Narration

Who Stood at Uhud

Mecca brought three thousand men under Abu Sufyan to avenge Badr, and the Muslim line broke at the mountain pass. The day turned on the few who refused to run. From al-Kharkushi's Sharaf al-Mustafa and the hadith of al-Sadiq ﵇ in al-Rawandi's Qisas al-Anbiya.

The Day the Pass Was Left

Mecca came for revenge. After Badr every house in Mecca held a mourner, and Hind, who had lost her father, her uncle, and her brother, pressed Abu Sufyan until he marched with three thousand men.▼ commentary Al-Kharkushi opens the story with the mourning that drove it.

فإن أهل بدر لما رجع من سلم منهم منهزما ، وقتل سادتهم ، لم يبق منزل بمكة إلّا دخله الصراخ والعويل على قتلاهم ، وحرّض القوم بعضهم بعضا ، وحرّضت أمّ معاوية أبا سفيان ليخرج بهم ليأخذ بثأر قتلاها ، وكان منهم : أبوها عتبة بن ربيعة ، وعمها شيبة ، وأخوها الوليد بن عتبة ، وبنو عمها .

فخرج أبو سفيان في ثلاثة آلاف من قريش ومن تبعهم ، ومعهم هند وصواحبها ينشدن

"When the survivors of Badr came back to Mecca beaten, with their leaders killed, there was no house in Mecca that wailing did not enter for its dead."

"The people stirred one another up, and Hind, the mother of Mu'awiya, pressed Abu Sufyan to march out and take blood for her dead. Among them were her father Utba ibn Rabi'a, her uncle Shayba, her brother al-Walid ibn Utba, and cousins of theirs."

"Abu Sufyan marched out with three thousand of Quraysh and those who followed them, and with them came Hind and her women, chanting."

- Al-Kharkushi, Sharaf al-Mustafa, vol.3, p.20

The Prophet ﷺ met them at the mountain of Uhud, a year after Badr. The battle turned twice, once when the Muslims held the pass, and once when its archers left it against orders for the spoils. The Qur'an itself named the second turn.▼ commentary

إِنَّ الَّذِينَ تَوَلَّوْا مِنْكُمْ يَوْمَ الْتَقَى الْجَمْعَانِ إِنَّمَا اسْتَزَلَّهُمُ الشَّيْطَانُ بِبَعْضِ مَا كَسَبُوا وَلَقَدْ عَفَا اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ إِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ حَلِيمٌ

inna lladhīna tawallaw minkum yawma l-taqā l-jamʿāni innamā stazallahumu l-shayṭānu bi-baʿḍi mā kasabū wa-laqad ʿafā llāhu ʿanhum inna llāha ghafūrun ḥalīm

"Those of you who turned back on the day the two hosts met, only Satan made them slip, because of some of what they had earned. And Allah has pardoned them. Allah is forgiving, forbearing."

- Qur'an 3:155, Uthmani text as carried in the corpus

The army broke at the pass. The cry went up that the Prophet ﷺ had been killed, and the field emptied around him. The hadith of al-Sadiq ﵇ on the hour names the one man who did not run, and carries Jibril as witness.▼ commentary

قال الصادق ﵇ : انهزم الناس عن رسول الله ﵌ ، فغضب غضبا شديدا ، وكان إذا غضب انحدر من وجهه وجبهته مثل اللؤلؤ من العرق ، فنظر فإذا على إلى جنبه ، فقال : مالك لم تلحق ببني أبيك ؟

فقال علي ﵇ : يا رسول الله أكفر بعد ايمان ؟ ان لي بك أسوة فقال : اما فاكفني هؤلاء فحمل على فضرب أول من لقي منهم ، فقال جبرئيل ﵇ : ان هذه لهي المواساة يا محمد ، فقال إنه منى وانا منه ، قال جبرئيل ﵇ : وانا منكما .

"Al-Sadiq, peace be upon him, said, 'The people fled from the Messenger of Allah on the day of Uhud, and anger rose in him fiercely, and when anger rose in him sweat rolled down his face and forehead like pearls. He looked, and Ali was at his side. He said to him, What keeps you from joining your father's sons?'"

"Ali said, 'Messenger of Allah, shall I disbelieve after faith? In you I have my example.' He said, 'Then hold these off me.' So he charged and struck the first of them he met."

"Jibril said, 'Muhammad, this is true standing by.' And he said, 'He is of me and I am of him.' Jibril said, 'And I am of you both.'"

- al-Rawandi, Qisas al-Anbiya, p.339, carrying al-Sadiq

Al-Ansari's al-Musnad al-Sahih, a catalog of reports arranged by companion, carries one of the men who did run.

فر عثمان يوم أحد ورجع بعد ثلاثا فقال النبي : لقد ذهبتم فيها عريضة

"Uthman fled on the day of Uhud and returned after three days, and the Prophet said, You have ranged far in it indeed."

- al-Ansari, al-Musnad al-Sahih, p.263, on Uthman at Uhud

The men who fled were gone from the field, and on that field the Prophet ﷺ himself was wounded. A tooth broken, his face gashed, the blood pouring over it, and the verse that came down on his wound, Qur'an 3:128, put revenge outside his authority.

أخبرنا حامد بن محمد بن شعيب البلخي حدثنا سريج بن يونس حدثنا هشيم ويزيد بن هارون قالا حدثنا حميد عن أنس بن مالك أن النبي ﵌ كسرت رباعيته يوم أحد وشج وجهه حتى سأل الدم على وجهه فقال كيف يفلح قوم فعلوا هذا بنبيهم ﵌ وهو يدعوهم إلى ربهم فنزلت ليس لك من الامر شئ أو يتوب عليهم أو يعذبهم فإنهم ظالمون

"Hamid ibn Muhammad ibn Shu'ayb al-Balkhi informed us. Sarij ibn Yunus told us. Hushaym and Yazid ibn Harun said. Hamid told us, from Anas ibn Malik, that the Prophet's tooth was broken on the day of Uhud and his face was gashed until the blood poured over it."

"He said, 'How can a people prosper who did this to their Prophet while he calls them to their Lord?' Then the verse came down, 'It is not yours to decide in the matter, whether He turns to them in mercy or punishes them, for they are wrongdoers.'"

- Ibn Hibban, Sahih, vol.14, p.536, from Anas

Ali ﵇ held the ground around him. The sources the two communities share carry Hamza's day as well. Hamza fought with two swords, killed thirty of the enemy, and was named in heaven before Wahshi's spear found him.▼ commentary

اشترك في معركة بدر ، وقتل بعض صناديد المشركين ؛ كعتبة بن ربيعة ، فامتلأت قلوبهم غيظا عليه . وفي معركة أحد كان يقاتل بسيفين ، ويغوص في الصفوف حتّى قتل منهم ثلاثين شخصا ، وقال فيه رسول اللّه صلّى اللّه عليه واله : « والذي نفسي بيده ، إنّه مكتوب عند اللّه عز وجل في السماء السابعة : حمزة بن عبد المطلب أسد اللّه وأسد رسوله

"He fought at Badr and killed some of the champions of the idolaters, among them Utba ibn Rabi'a, and their hearts filled with rage against him. At Uhud he fought with two swords and plunged into the ranks until he had killed thirty of them."

"The Messenger of Allah said, 'By Him in whose hand is my soul, it is written with Allah Almighty in the seventh heaven, Hamza ibn Abd al-Muttalib, the lion of Allah and the lion of His Messenger.'"

- Al-Ruwat al-Mushtaraka bayn al-Shia wa-l-Sunna, vol.1, p.275

The false cry that broke the army had a name. Ibn Qami'a killed Mus'ab ibn Umayr, took him for the Prophet ﷺ, and cried that Muhammad was dead. The exchange the sira keeps shows the confusion reaching Abu Sufyan himself at the day's end.

ثم قال أبو سفيان لعمر أي بعد ان قال له هلم يا عمر فقال له رسول الله ﵌ ائته انظر ما شأنه فجاءه فقال له أبو سفيان أشدك الله يا عمر اقتلنا محمد قال عمر رضي الله تعالى عنه لا وإنه ليسمع كلامك الآن قال أنت أصدق عندي من ابن قمئة وابر أي لأنه لما قتل مصعب بن عمير ظنه النبي ﵌ فقال قتلت محمد كما تقدم

"Then Abu Sufyan called to Umar, after he had called him to come, and the Messenger of Allah told him to go and see what he wanted. He came to him, and Abu Sufyan said, 'May your arm be strong, Umar. Have we killed Muhammad?' Umar said, 'No, and he can hear your words right now.'"

"He said, 'You are more truthful to me than Ibn Qami'a,' because when Ibn Qami'a killed Mus'ab ibn Umayr he had taken him for the Prophet and cried that he had killed Muhammad."

- Al-Halabi, al-Sira al-Halabiyya, vol.2, p.529

What was done to the Muslim dead, the Shia scholar al-Askari carries without softening.▼ commentary

يجد عن الآذان والأنف حتى اتخذت هند من آذان الرجال وأنفهم خدما وقلائد وأعطت خدمها وقلائدها وقرطتها وحشيا ، وبقرت عن كبد حمزة فلاكتها فلم تسطع أن تسيغها فلفظتها

"They cut away at the ears and noses until Hind had made anklets and necklaces from the ears and noses of the men, and she gave her anklets and necklaces and her earrings to Wahshi. She tore open the liver of Hamza and chewed it, and she could not swallow it, so she spat it out."

- Al-Askari, Ahadith Umm al-Mu'minin Aisha, vol.1, p.281

What was done to the dead ended at burial. The poverty of the mourners, in a hadith of Ibn Hibban, closed the burials. The count of the dead closed the day.

أتي عبد الرحمن بن عوف وكان صائما بطعام فجعل يبكي فقال قتل حمزة فلم يوجد ما يكفن فيه إلا ثوب واحد وقتل مصعب بن عمير فلم يوجد ما يكفن فيه إلا ثوب واحد ولقد خشيت أن تكون قد عجلت طيباتنا في حياتنا الدنيا قال وجعل يبكي

"Food was brought to Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf while he was fasting, and he began to weep. He said, 'Hamza was killed, and nothing was found to shroud him in except a single garment. Mus'ab ibn Umayr was killed, and nothing was found to shroud him in except a single garment. I fear our good things have been hurried to us in the life of this world.' And he kept weeping."

- Ibn Hibban, Sahih, vol.15, p.485, from Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf

روى الإمام أحمد . والشيخان والنسائي عن البراء ﵁ ، قال : أصابوا - أي المشركون - منا يوم أحد سبعين ، وكان رسول الله ﵌ وأصحابه أصابوا من المشركين يوم بدر مائة وأربعة وسبعين قتيلا .

وروى سعيد بن منصور عن أبي الضحى مرسلا قال : قتل يوم أحد سبعون : أربعة من المهاجرين : حمزة ، ومصعب ، وعبد الله بن جحش ، وشماس بن عثمان ، وسائرهم من الأنصار .

"Imam Ahmad, the two Sahihs, and al-Nasa'i relate from al-Bara that the idolaters struck down seventy of us on the day of Uhud, while the Messenger of Allah and his companions had struck down one hundred and seventy-four of the idolaters on the day of Badr."

"Sa'id ibn Mansur relates from Abu al-Duha, as an unconnected report, that seventy were killed on the day of Uhud, four of them Emigrants, Hamza, Mus'ab, Abd Allah ibn Jahsh, and Shammas ibn Uthman, and the rest of the Ansar."

- Al-Shami, Sabil al-Huda, vol.4, p.256, on the seventy

At the day's end, the two armies called to each other across the valley. Abu Sufyan cried his gods, and the answers came back from the ridge.

وقال أبو سفيان اعل هبل أي أظهر دينك أو ازدد علو فقال رسول الله ﵌ قم يا عمرفأجبه فقل الله أعلى وأجل لا سواء قتلانا في الجنة وقتلاكم في النار فقال أبو سفيان إنكم تزعمون ذلك لقد خبنا إذن وخسرنا

"Abu Sufyan said, 'Exalt Hubal,' meaning, show your religion and rise higher. The Messenger of Allah said, 'Rise, Umar, and answer him. Say, Allah is higher and greater. They are not the same. Our dead are in Paradise, and your dead are in the Fire.' Abu Sufyan said, 'You claim it. Then we have failed and lost.'"

- Al-Halabi, al-Sira al-Halabiyya, vol.2, p.529, the closing exchange

ثم قال أبو سفيان إن لنا العزى ولا عزى لكم فقال رسول الله ﵌ الله مولانا ولا مولى لكم

"Then Abu Sufyan said, 'We have al-Uzza, and you have no Uzza.' The Messenger of Allah said, 'Allah is our protector, and you have no protector.'"

- Al-Halabi, al-Sira al-Halabiyya, vol.2, p.529, on al-Uzza

Commentary

The Sources for Uhud

Source spine: al-Kharkushi, Sharaf al-Mustafa, vol.3, p.20, on the cause

Hadith lines: Ibn Hibban, Sahih, vol.14, p.536, on the wound, and vol.15, p.485, on the shrouds. Al-Shami, Sabil al-Huda, vol.4, p.256, on the seventy

Sira line: al-Halabi, al-Sira al-Halabiyya, vol.2, p.529, on the closing exchange

Shia lines: al-Askari, Ahadith Umm al-Mu'minin Aisha, vol.1, p.281, on the mutilation. al-Rawandi, Qisas al-Anbiya, p.339, on the hour of the rout

Shared line: al-Ruwat al-Mushtaraka bayn al-Shia wa-l-Sunna, vol.1, p.275, on Hamza at Uhud. al-Ansari, al-Musnad al-Sahih, p.263, the catalog on Uthman at Uhud

Scripture: Qur'an 3:155 and Qur'an 3:128, Uthmani text as carried in the corpus

Uhud is carried by every layer of the record, hadith and sira, Sunni and Shia, and the Qur'an itself, and the layers agree on the shape. A revenge army of three thousand, a battle the Muslims first dominated, the fatal leaving of the pass, the wounding of the Prophet ﷺ, seventy dead, and the mutilation of the dead by Hind's women.

The Day the Pass Was Left

Enemy: three thousand of Quraysh and their followers, with Hind and her women chanting

The turn: the archers left the mountain pass against orders, for the spoils

The verse: Satan made them slip because of some of what they had earned, and Allah pardoned them

The wounded: the Prophet ﷺ, tooth broken, face gashed

The dead of the Muslims: seventy, among them Hamza, Mus'ab ibn Umayr, Abd Allah ibn Jahsh, and Shammas ibn Uthman

The Qur'an's verse does the work no polemic can. It names the turning of the day as a fault of the Muslims themselves, it names its mechanism, a slip earned by their own conduct, and it closes with pardon.

The pardon and the record are two different things. The verse pardons the men who turned back, and the same verse fixes the fact that they turned. The school's question is the record of who stood, and the pardon belongs to Allah alone.

The death of Hamza decided the shape of the rout. With him and Mus'ab down, and the cry going up that the Prophet ﷺ had been killed, the army broke. The killing of Mus'ab mattered twice, for the army lost the bearer of its banner, and Ibn Qami'a had mistaken him for the Prophet ﷺ and cried that Muhammad was dead. The verse that came down over the Prophet's own wound, Qur'an 3:128, closed the matter of revenge in the Qur'an's own voice.

Who Stood When the Army Ran

The rout: the army broke at the pass, the cry went up that the Prophet ﷺ had been killed, and the field emptied around him

The one who did not run: Ali ﵇, named in the hadith of al-Sadiq ﵇, found at the Prophet's ﷺ side, offered leave to join his kin who fled, and answering, shall I disbelieve after faith

The divine witness: Jibril's word on the hour, this is true standing by, and the answer, he is of me and I am of him, and I am of you both

Uthman's three days: the catalog carries his flight from Uhud and his return after three days, with the Prophet's ﷺ word on it, you have ranged far in it indeed

The filter the school reads this day through is exact. The Qur'an named the slipping and the pardon, and the hadith of al-Sadiq ﵇ names the man who did not slip, with Jibril as witness. The catalog of al-Ansari names the man whose three-day absence the Prophet's ﷺ own word marked.

The duels of the morning fell to Hamza and Ali ﵇, and the stand of the afternoon fell to Ali ﵇ alone. The sources the two communities share give Hamza the two swords, the thirty dead, and the naming in heaven. The hadith of al-Sadiq ﵇ gives Ali ﵇ the stand at the Prophet's ﷺ side, with Jibril as witness.

What the Two Sides Carried Home

Muslims: seventy dead, the wounded Prophet ﷺ, and a verse of pardon

Quraysh: their women mutilated the Muslim dead, and a claim of victory they could not hold

The exchange: Abu Sufyan's cry to Hubal answered from the ridge, our dead in Paradise, yours in the Fire

Hind tore open the liver of Hamza and could not swallow it. The exchange Abu Sufyan called across the valley was answered by the Prophet's ﷺ words through Umar, and by the answer that closed the account, Allah is our protector.

Medina held the strategic ground within the year. The Prophet ﷺ marched out after them to Hamra al-Asad, and Abu Sufyan's army went home without pressing its advantage. The rematch the two sides appointed for the following year, Badr al-Maw'id, ended without battle. Uhud broke none of Medina's institutions and killed none of its chiefs beyond Hamza.

Was the Battle of Uhud Justified

The battle was forced on Medina. A revenge army of three thousand marched on the city a year after Badr, and the choice was the mountain's ground or the city's streets. The Muslims met the army at Uhud.

The conduct in the field stands against the enemy's ledger. The Prophet ﷺ, wounded and bled, asked only how a people could prosper who had done this to their Prophet, and the verse that came down on his wound put revenge outside his authority. Quraysh's women mutilated the Muslim dead for jewelry. The Muslim dead were shrouded one garment to a man, and the two communities remember it as the mark of a generation that had given everything.

Could it have been prevented? The door was on Mecca's side, an army it raised for blood after Badr and marched on a city that had not attacked it. Within the battle, the day was lost by the leaving of the pass against orders, a fault the Qur'an assigns to the Muslims alone, and pardons.
