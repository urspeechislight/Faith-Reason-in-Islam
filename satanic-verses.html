Qur'anic Objection · Debate

Satan Never Authored a Verse of the Qur'an

The claim that Satan authored the Qur'an's abrogated verses collapses on the words of Qur'an 22:52, because the lexicons fix tamanna as a wish formed in the soul, the verse abolishes what Satan casts before Allah establishes His signs, 2:106 and 16:101 name one Author for both sides of every exchange, the refuge command of 16:98 addresses every reciter while 16:99-100 deny Satan power over believers, and the cranes story is the other school's report, filed by the school as such and rejected under ismah. From al-Mufradat, Tafsir al-Qummi, al-Burhan, Nur al-Thaqalayn, Mukhtasar Tafsir al-Qummi, Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi, Wadh al-Burhan, Haqa'iq al-Ma'rifa, al-Nass wa-l-Ijtihad, and al-Qur'an wa Riwayat al-Madrasatayn.

The Argument Under Examination

Background

The attack states its case in one sentence. The Qur'an never says that Allah sent down the verses that were later abrogated. One passage says that Allah abolishes what Satan proposes in the prophets' recitation. The passage about exchanging verses commands seeking refuge from Satan while reciting. So Satan, the only author the Qur'an names for an abolished text, wrote at least some of the abrogated verses. And verses the Sunni canon counts as abrogated may still sit in the Book. The sentence has a structure, a lexicon, a grammar, a passage, a story, and a doctrine. Each layer fails on the sources the attack itself leans on, on five premises.

Premise 1: The Words Do Not Mean What the Attack Needs

The attack hangs on two words it never opens a dictionary for. Tamanna, the verse's verb, is a wish formed in the soul, and umniyya is the image that wish leaves behind, in the definition of Raghib's al-Mufradat. Naskh, the verse's second verb, is effacement, what the sun does to a shadow, in the lexical paragraph of Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi. A translator who renders the first word as recitation and the second as scheduled expiry has chosen a reading. The dictionaries refuse to certify it.

Premise 2: The Verse Separates Three Things the Attack Melts Into One

The verse separates three things the attack melts into one. Qur'an 22:52 names the prophet's wish, Satan's casting at it, and Allah's signs, and it orders them abolish, then establish. What Satan casts is the object of the abolition, the signs are what remain after it, and the prophet's wish is the target, never the text. Nothing of the casting survives into the signs on the verse's own grammar, and the attack's authorship transfer has no clause to stand on.

Premise 3: The Abrogation Verses Name One Author

The abrogation verses name one Author for both sides of every exchange. Qur'an 2:106 has one We who abrogates and the same We who brings the better. Qur'an 16:101 answers the forger charge in its own breath with the Holy Spirit. The school's tafsir tradition reads the exchange verses on the qibla, one revealed direction replacing another from the same Revealer. No verse anywhere names a third author.

Premise 4: The Refuge Command Is Etiquette, Not Confession

The refuge command is etiquette, not confession. Qur'an 16:98 addresses whoever recites, and 16:99-100 state that Satan has no authority over those who believe, only over those who take him as patron. The Imams of the House read the casting as Satan's whisper to the audience, and 75:16-19 makes the gathering and the reciting of revelation Allah's own task. The magic reports the attack appends are about imagined household acts, and their own text says the acts never happened.

Premise 5: The Cranes Story Is the Other School's Report

The cranes story is the other school's report, and the corpus files it as such. Tafsir al-Qummi relays it under the label of what the common tradition reported. It then gives the Imams' reading of the same verse, a wish to share food with his Family, two intruders, and Ali's ﵇ arrival as the abolition. Al-Ata'iqi's summary renders the categorical verdict, this is impermissible for any prophet because they are protected. And the school transmits from Ali ﵇ that Quraysh invented the story in defense of their idols.

Conclusion: The Satanic Verses Are the Attack's Invention

The attack hangs Satan's authorship on a verb the verse itself abolishes before the word stands, on a reading rule the same passage answers with a denial of Satan's power, and on a story the chains discard and the school files under the other camp's reports. Every verse the attack lists as a surviving satanic text is a ruling change the two schools debate in their books of jurisprudence. The author of both terms is named as Allah in the very verses quoted.

1. The Words the Verse Actually Uses

The Words the Verse Actually Uses

What the Lexicon Fixes

The attack renders tamanna as recited and builds Satan's authorship on the rendering. The classical lexicon says what the word carries, and it says it at the verse itself. Raghib's al-Mufradat works through the whole family of the word, from wishing, to lying, to the Qur'an's own verses, and it closes with 22:52.

والأمنية الصورة الحاصلة في النفس من تمنى الشئ ، ولما كان الكذب تصور ما لا حقيقة له وإيراده باللفظ صار التمني كالمبدأ للكذب فصح أن يعبر عن الكذب بالتمني ، وعلى ذلك ما روى عن عثمان ﵁ : ما تغنيت ولا تمنيت منذ أسلمت وقوله (ومنهم أميون لا يعلمون الكتاب إلا أماني) قال مجاهد : معناه إلا كذبا ، وقال غيره إلا تلاوة مجردة عن المعرفة من حيث إن التلاوة بلا معرفة المعنى تجرى عند صاحبها مجرى أمنية تمنيتها على التخمين ، وقوله (وما أرسلنا من قبلك من رسول ولا نبي إلا إذا تمنى ألقى الشيطان في أمنيته) أي في تلاوته ، فقد تقدم أن التمني كما يكون عن تخمين وظن فقد يكون عن روية وبناء على أصل ، ولما كان النبي صلى الله عليه وسلم كثيرا ما كان يبادر إلى ما نزل به الروح الأمين على قلبه حتى قيل له (لا تعجل بالقرآن) الآية و (لا تحرك به لسانك لتعجل به) سمى تلاوته على ذلك تمنيا ونبه أن للشيطان تسلطا على مثله في أمنيته وذلك من حيث بين أن العجلة من الشيطان . ومنيتني كذا : جعلت لي أمنية بما شبهت لي ، قال تعالى مخبرا عنه : (ولأضلنهم ولأمنينهم) .

wa-l-umniyyatu aṣ-ṣūratu al-ḥāṣilatu fī an-nafsi min tamannā ash-shayʾ, wa-li-mā kāna al-kadhibu taṣawwura mā lā ḥaqīqata lahū wa-īrāduhu bi-l-lafẓ ṣāra at-tamannī ka-l-mabdaʾi li-l-kadhib...

"A wish is the image that forms in the soul when someone desires something. A lie imagines something with no reality and puts it into words. For this reason, wishing can serve as the beginning of lying, and the word for wishing can describe a lie. Uthman said, "Since I became Muslim, I have neither sung nor lied." In the verse, "Among them are unlettered people who know nothing of the Scripture except wishes," Mujahid explained "wishes" as lies. Others explained it as recitation without understanding. A person who recites without knowing the meaning treats it like a wish formed through guesswork. In the verse, "We sent no messenger or prophet before you without Satan casting something into his recitation when he recited," recitation is meant. Wishing may arise from guesswork and assumption. It may also arise from reflection and a sound basis. The Prophet often hastened to repeat what the trustworthy Spirit brought to his heart. He was therefore told, "Do not hurry with the Quran," and, "Do not move your tongue quickly with it." His recitation in this state was called wishing. Satan can affect such recitation because haste comes from Satan. The phrase, "You made me wish for such a thing," means, "You gave me a wish by making something seem so to me." Allah reports Satan as saying, "I will lead them astray, and I will fill them with wishes.""

- al-Raghib al-Isfahani, al-Mufradat fi Gharib al-Qur'an, p. 476, entry tamanna

How the Qur'an Uses the Verb

The Qur'an uses the verb where a wish means a wish, and it does so inside the same sura the cranes story calls home. Surat al-Najm reaches the three goddesses the story names, then runs on to its own question.

أَ فَرَأَيْتُمُ اللاَّتَ وَالْعُزَّى (19) وَمَناةَ الثَّالِثَةَ الْأُخْرى (20) أَ لَكُمُ الذَّكَرُ وَلَهُ الْأُنْثى (21) تِلْكَ إِذاً قِسْمَةٌ ضِيزى (22) إِنْ هِيَ إِلاَّ أَسْماءٌ سَمَّيْتُمُوها أَنْتُمْ وَآباؤُكُمْ ما أَنْزَلَ اللَّهُ بِها مِنْ سُلْطانٍ إِنْ يَتَّبِعُونَ إِلاَّ الظَّنَّ وَما تَهْوَى الْأَنْفُسُ وَلَقَدْ جاءَهُمْ مِنْ رَبِّهِمُ الْهُدى (23) أَمْ لِلْإِنْسانِ ما تَمَنَّى (24) فَلِلَّهِ الْآخِرَةُ وَالْأُولى (25) وَكَمْ مِنْ مَلَكٍ فِي السَّماواتِ لا تُغْنِي شَفاعَتُهُمْ شَيْئاً إِلاَّ مِنْ بَعْدِ أَنْ يَأْذَنَ اللَّهُ لِمَنْ يَشاءُ وَيَرْضى (26)

a-fa-raʾaytumu llāta wa-l-ʿuzzā wa-manāta th-thālithata al-ukhrā... am li-l-insāni mā tamannā

"Have you considered al-Lat and al-Uzza, and Manat, the third and last? Are sons for you and daughters for Him? That would be an unfair division. These are only names that you and your fathers gave them. Allah sent down no authority for them. They follow only guesswork and what their souls desire, though guidance has already come to them from their Lord. Does man receive whatever he wishes? The Hereafter and this life belong to Allah. How many angels are in the heavens whose intercession has no effect until Allah permits it for whomever He wills and approves."

- Qur'an 53:19-26, as embedded in Wahba al-Zuhayli, al-Tafsir al-Munir, vol. 27, p. 107

The Verse With Its Three Objects

The verse itself, from its corpus text.

وَما أَرْسَلْنا مِنْ قَبْلِكَ مِنْ رَسُولٍ وَلا نَبِيٍّ إِلاَّ إِذا تَمَنَّى أَلْقَى الشَّيْطانُ فِي أُمْنِيَّتِهِ فَيَنْسَخُ اللَّهُ ما يُلْقِي الشَّيْطانُ ثُمَّ يُحْكِمُ اللَّهُ آياتِهِ وَاللَّهُ عَلِيمٌ حَكِيمٌ (52) لِيَجْعَلَ ما يُلْقِي الشَّيْطانُ فِتْنَةً لِلَّذِينَ فِي قُلُوبِهِمْ مَرَضٌ وَالْقاسِيَةِ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَإِنَّ الظَّالِمِينَ لَفِي شِقاقٍ بَعِيدٍ (53) وَلِيَعْلَمَ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْعِلْمَ أَنَّهُ الْحَقُّ مِنْ رَبِّكَ فَيُؤْمِنُوا بِهِ فَتُخْبِتَ لَهُ قُلُوبُهُمْ وَإِنَّ اللَّهَ لَهادِ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِلى صِراطٍ مُسْتَقِيمٍ (54)

wa-mā arsalnā min qablika min rasūlin wa-lā nabiyyin illā idhā tamannā alqa sh-shayṭānu fī umniyyatihī fa-yan sakhu llāhu mā yulqī sh-shayṭānu thumma yuḥkimu llāhu āyātihī...

"We sent no messenger or a prophet before you, but when he formed a wish, Satan cast something into his wish. Allah then abolishes what Satan casts. Then Allah establishes His verses, and Allah is All-Knowing and Wise. He lets what Satan cast become a trial for those with sickness in their hearts and for hardened hearts, and the wrongdoers are in deep conflict. Those given knowledge may know that it is the truth from your Lord, believe in it, and let their hearts grow humble before it. Allah guides those who believe to a straight path."

- Qur'an 22:52-54, as embedded in Muhammad Hasan Qubaysi al-Amili, Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi, vol. 3, p. 410

What Naskh Does to What It Reaches

The second contested word is the verse's own verb, naskh, which the attack renders as a standing verse's scheduled expiry. The lexicons say otherwise.

النسخ لغة عبارة عن الإزالة والابطال والاعدام ، تقول : نسخت الشمس الظل ، يعني ازالته ، وتقول : نسخت الرياح آثار القدم ، يعني أزالتها ، ويراد من النسخ أيضا النقل والتحويل ، تقول : نسخت الكتاب أي نقلت كلما فيه ( وتطبيق النقل على كتابة المثل مجاز عقلي ) ، ونسخت النحل من خلية إلى أخرى يعني حولتها من مكان إلى آخر

an-naskhu fī l-lughati ʿibāratun ʿani l-izālati wa-l-ibṭāli wa-l-iʿdām, taqūlu naskhati sh-shamsu ẓ-ẓilla, yaʿnī azālatuhū...

"In language, naskh means removal, cancellation, and bringing something to an end. One says, "The sun removed the shadow," meaning that it made the shadow disappear. One also says, "The winds erased the footprints," meaning that they removed them. Naskh can also mean transfer and change. One says, "I copied the book," meaning that I transferred everything in it. Applying transfer to writing a copy is a figurative use based on cause and effect. One says, "I moved the bees from one hive to another," meaning that I transferred them from one place to another."

- Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi (al-Qubaysi al-Amili), vol. 1, p. 348, the lexical definition of naskh

فإنّ النسخ قريب المعنى من المحو ؛ يقال : نسخت الشمس الظلّ إذا أزالته وذهبت به ، ومنه أيضاً قولهم : نسخت الكتاب إذا نقل من نسخة إلى أخرى ، فكأنّ الكتاب أُذهب به وأُبدل مكانه ، ولذلك أبدل لفظ النسخ بالتبديل في قوله تعالى : وَإِذَا بَدَّلْنَا آيَةً مَكَانَ آيَةٍ ( النحل : 101 )

"Naskh is close in meaning to erasure. People say, 'The sun effaced the shadow,' when it removed it and took it away. They also say, 'He copied the book,' when it was transferred from one copy to another, as though the first book were taken away and another put in its place. For this reason the word replacement stands in for naskh in His words, 'When We replace one verse with another' (Qur'an 16:101)."

- Al-Kamal al-Haydari, al-Arsh wa-l-Kursi, vol. 2, p. 62, on naskh and tabdil

2. The Abrogation Verses Name One Author

The Abrogation Verses Name One Author

The We Who Abrogates and the We Who Brings

The attack's central claim is an argument from silence, that the abrogation verses never say Allah sent down the abrogated term. The verse's own sentence structure says it for them.

مَا نَنْسَخْ مِنْ آيَةٍ أَوْ نُنْسِهَا نَأْتِ بِخَيْرٍ مِنْهَا أَوْ مِثْلِهَا ( البقرة : 106 )

mā nansakh min āyatin aw nunsihā naʾti bi-khayrin minhā aw mithlihā

"Whatever verse We abrogate or cause to be forgotten, We bring one better than it or like it."

- Qur'an 2:106, as embedded in al-Kamal al-Haydari, al-Arsh wa-l-Kursi, vol. 2, p. 62

وهذا ومثله ، وما كان نظيرا له ، فإذا ألقي في تنزيل اللّه ووحيه ، أو أمر اللّه ونهيه ، نسخه اللّه فنفاه ، وأبطله ونحّاه ، واللّه سبحانه لا يبطل ولا ينفي وحيه بنسخه وتبديله ، وإن صرفه فزاد أو نقض من الفرض في تنزيله ، كقوله سبحانه : وَإِذا بَدَّلْنا آيَةً مَكانَ آيَةٍ وَاللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِما يُنَزِّلُ قالُوا إِنَّما أَنْتَ مُفْتَرٍ بَلْ أَكْثَرُهُمْ لا يَعْلَمُونَ (101) قُلْ نَزَّلَهُ رُوحُ الْقُدُسِ مِنْ رَبِّكَ بِالْحَقِّ لِيُثَبِّتَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَهُدىً وَبُشْرى لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ (102) [ النحل : 101 - 102 ] . فكل أمر اللّه ونهيه هدى ورحمة ، ومنّ من اللّه على خلقه ونعمة ، فكذلك أمر النسخ والتبديل ، وما ذكر منهما جميعا في التنزيل .

"This and anything like it, when it is cast into Allah's revelation and inspiration, or into Allah's command and prohibition, Allah abrogates it, rejects it, cancels it, and sets it aside. Allah, exalted is He, does not cancel or remove His revelation through abrogation and replacement. He may direct it, increasing an obligation in His revelation or easing part of it. He says, 'When We replace one verse with another, and Allah knows best what He sends down, they say, you have invented this. Most of them do not know. Say, the Holy Spirit brought it down from your Lord in truth, to strengthen those who believe, and as guidance and good news for those who submit to Allah.' Every command and prohibition from Allah is guidance and mercy, grace from Allah to His creation and a blessing. The same is true of abrogation and replacement, and of all that the revelation mentions of the two."

- Al-Qasim al-Rassi, Majmu' Kutub wa Rasa'il, vol. 2, p. 49, on 16:101-102

The Exchange the Passage Names

The attack quotes 16:101 without its context, and the context answers it. The passage carries the pagans' charge in its own words, you are a mere forger, and returns the Holy Spirit as the answer.

وَإِذا بَدَّلْنا آيَةً مَكانَ آيَةٍ وَاللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ بِما يُنَزِّلُ قالُوا إِنَّما أَنْتَ مُفْتَرٍ بَلْ أَكْثَرُهُمْ لا يَعْلَمُونَ (101) قُلْ نَزَّلَهُ رُوحُ الْقُدُسِ مِنْ رَبِّكَ بِالْحَقِّ لِيُثَبِّتَ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَهُدىً وَبُشْرى لِلْمُسْلِمِينَ (102) وَلَقَدْ نَعْلَمُ أَنَّهُمْ يَقُولُونَ إِنَّما يُعَلِّمُهُ بَشَرٌ لِسانُ الَّذِي يُلْحِدُونَ إِلَيْهِ أَعْجَمِيٌّ وَهذا لِسانٌ عَرَبِيٌّ مُبِينٌ (103)

wa-idhā baddalnā āyatan makāna āyatin wa-llāhu aʿlamu bi-mā yunazzilu...

"When We replace one verse with another, and Allah knows best what He sends down, they say, "You have only invented this." Most of them do not know. Say, "The Holy Spirit brought it down from your Lord in truth, to make the believers firm and as guidance and good news for those who submit to Allah." We know that they say, "A human being teaches him." The language of the person they point to is foreign, while this is clear Arabic speech."

- Qur'an 16:101-103, as embedded in Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi, vol. 3, p. 290

ومنه تحويل القبلة من بيت المقدس إلى الكعبة المكرمة ، قال اللّه تعالى (3) : قَدْ نَرى تَقَلُّبَ وَجْهِكَ فِي السَّماءِ فَلَنُوَلِّيَنَّكَ قِبْلَةً تَرْضاها فَوَلِّ وَجْهَكَ شَطْرَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرامِ ، وقال اللّه تعالى (4) : وَما جَعَلْنَا الْقِبْلَةَ الَّتِي كُنْتَ عَلَيْها إِلَّا لِنَعْلَمَ مَنْ يَتَّبِعُ الرَّسُولَ مِمَّنْ يَنْقَلِبُ عَلى عَقِبَيْه

"An example is the change of the direction of prayer from Jerusalem to the honored Kaaba. Allah says, 'We have seen your face turning toward the sky. We will turn you toward a direction of prayer that pleases you. Turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque.' Allah also says, 'We made the direction of prayer that you followed only so that We might know who follows the Messenger and who turns back on his heels.'"

- Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi (al-Qubaysi al-Amili), vol. 1, p. 375, on the instances of naskh

3. The Refuge Rule Is Etiquette for Readers

The Refuge Rule Is Etiquette for Readers

What the Passage Says About Satan's Reach

The attack reads 16:98 as Muhammad's private confession of satanic interference in his recitation. The passage says the opposite in its own words.

فَإِذا قَرَأْتَ الْقُرْآنَ فَاسْتَعِذْ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطانِ الرَّجِيمِ (98) إِنَّهُ لَيْسَ لَهُ سُلْطانٌ عَلَى الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَلى رَبِّهِمْ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ (99) إِنَّما سُلْطانُهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ يَتَوَلَّوْنَهُ وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ بِهِ مُشْرِكُونَ (100)

fa-idhā qaraʾta l-qurʾāna fastaʿidh bi-llāhi mina sh-shayṭāni r-rajīm...

"When you recite the Quran, seek Allah's protection from Satan, the rejected. He has no power over those who believe and rely on their Lord. His power reaches only those who take him as an ally and join others with Allah through him."

- Qur'an 16:98-100, as embedded in Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi, vol. 3, p. 289

فصح أن الشيطان ليس له سلطان على المؤمنين قال اللّه تعالى : فَإِذا قَرَأْتَ الْقُرْآنَ فَاسْتَعِذْ بِاللَّهِ مِنَ الشَّيْطانِ الرَّجِيمِ * إِنَّهُ لَيْسَ لَهُ سُلْطانٌ عَلَى الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَلى رَبِّهِمْ يَتَوَكَّلُونَ * إِنَّما سُلْطانُهُ عَلَى الَّذِينَ يَتَوَلَّوْنَهُ وَالَّذِينَ هُمْ بِهِ مُشْرِكُونَ [ النحل : 98 - 100 ] ، فصحّ أنه ليس له إحسان (1) في الدخول في صدر الإنسان

"It is established that Satan has no authority over believers. Allah says, 'When you recite the Qur'an, seek Allah's protection from Satan, the accursed. He has no power over those who believe and place their trust in their Lord. His power is only over those who take him as an ally and those who join partners with Allah through him.' It is therefore established that he has no way of entering a person's heart."

- Haqa'iq al-Ma'rifa fi Ilm al-Kalam, p. 241, on Qur'an 16:98-100

Where the Casting Lands

If 22:52 is still pressed, the question is where Satan's casting lands. The school's Imams ﵈ answered it, and their answer runs through the audience, never through the channel of revelation.

قال جعفر بن محمد (1) : كلّ نبيّ يتمنى إيمان قومه ، فيلقي الشيطان في أمنيته بما يوسوس إلى قومه (2) . ثُمَّ يُحْكِمُ اللَّهُ آياتِهِ . وقيل على هذا القول : إنّ وسوسة الشيطان يجوز أن يكون للنبيّ ، بما يلقي في أمنيته من اعتراض الهموم والخطرات المزعجة ، عند تباطؤ القوم عن الإيمان ، وتسارعهم إلى الصد والعدوان ، أو عند تأخر نصر اللّه له على قومه . - وإن حملت الأمنية على التلاوة ، فيجوز أن يكون الشيطان الملقي في التلاوة من شياطين الإنس ، فإنه كان من المشركين من يلغو في القرآن . فَيَنْسَخُ اللَّهُ ما يُلْقِي الشَّيْطانُ . ويبيّن إبطاله

"Ja'far ibn Muhammad said, every prophet hopes for his people's faith, and Satan then casts into that hope by whispering to the people. Then Allah establishes His verses. On this reading, Satan's whispering may reach the prophet through distressing worries and thoughts when his people are slow to believe and quick to reject and attack, or when Allah's victory over his people is delayed. If the wish is taken to mean recitation, the satan who casts into the recitation may be a human satan, for some of the idolaters used to shout over the Qur'an. Allah then abolishes what Satan casts and shows its failure."

- Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq ﵇, on Qur'an 22:52, in Wadh al-Burhan fi Masha'il al-Qur'an, vol. 2, p. 91

ومعنى تَمَنَّى قرأ . وتأويل أَلْقَى الشَّيْطانُ فِي أُمْنِيَّتِهِ المراد به في قراءته ، وليس المراد به أنه يلقي في قلب الرسول ولا على لسان الرسول ، ولكن المراد ( به أنه ) (1) يلقي في قراءة بعض من يقرأ ما يأتي به الرسول ، وذلك الإلقاء مثل الغلط والنّسيان ، والزيادة والنقصان . وقد سئل القاسم بن إبراهيم عليه السّلام عن تأويل هذه الآية فقال : تأويل تَمَنَّى هو قرأ ، وتأويل أَلْقَى الشَّيْطانُ فِي أُمْنِيَّتِهِ تأويله ألقى الشيطان في قراءته . وقراءته عليه السّلام هو إلقاء من الشيطان في أمنيته وقراءته . والإلقاء في القراءة من الشيطان ليس إلقاء (2) في قلب الرسول ، ولا فيما جعله اللّه له من اللسان ، ولكنه إلقاء في القراءة من الشيطان ، بزيادة منه في القراءة أو نقصان (3)

"The meaning of tamanna is recited, and the meaning of 'Satan cast something into his wish' is into his recitation. It does not mean that he casts into the Messenger's heart or onto the Messenger's tongue. It means that he casts into the recitation of someone who recites what the Messenger brought, and that casting is like error and forgetfulness, addition and omission. Al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim was asked about the meaning of this verse and said, 'The meaning of tamanna is recited, and the meaning of "Satan cast something into his wish" is that Satan cast into his recitation.' His recitation, peace be upon him, was a casting from Satan into his wish and his recitation. Satan's casting into recitation is not a casting into the Messenger's heart, nor into the tongue Allah gave him. It is a casting into the recitation, by an addition or an omission."

- Haqa'iq al-Ma'rifa fi Ilm al-Kalam, p. 243, relaying the ta'wil of al-Qasim ibn Ibrahim on Qur'an 22:52

The Reception Is Allah's Own Task

The deeper reason the school rejects any satanic entry into the revealed text is the Qur'an's own account of how reception works. A modern authority of the school collects the verses.

والأنبياء كلهم صلوات الله وسلامه عليهم لا يؤامرون أممهم فيما يشترعون (بل عباد مكرمون * لا يسبقونه بالقول وهم بأمره يعملون (3)) وحسبنا قوله ﷿ لعبده وخاتم رسله : (قل إنما أتبع ما يوحى إلي من ربي هذا بصائر من ربكم وهدى ورحمة لقوم يؤمنون (4)) (قل ما يكون لي أن أبدله من تلقاء نفسي إن أتبع إلا ما يوحى إلي إني أخاف إن عصيت ربي عذاب يوم عظيم (5)) . (قل ما كنت بدعا من الرسل وما أدري ما يفعل بي ولا بكم إن أتبع إلا ما يوحى إلي وما أنا إلا نذير مبين (6)) . وقد حظر ، عز سلطانه ، عليه العجل ولو بحركة اللسان فقال جل وعلا : (لا تحرك به لسانك لتعجل به * إن علينا جمعه وقرآنه * فإذا قرأناه فاتبع قرآنه * ثم إن علينا بيانه (7))

"All the prophets, may Allah's blessings and peace be upon them, do not take counsel with their peoples about the laws they establish. 'They are honored servants. They do not speak before Him, and they act by His command.' Allah's word to His servant and the seal of His messengers suffices us. 'Say, I follow only what my Lord reveals to me. This is clear insight from your Lord, guidance, and mercy for people who believe.' 'Say, it is not for me to change it by myself. I follow only what is revealed to me. I fear the punishment of a tremendous day if I disobey my Lord.' 'Say, I am no new thing among the messengers. I do not know what will be done with me or with you. I follow only what is revealed to me. I am only a clear warner.' In His might, Allah forbade him haste, even by the movement of the tongue, and said, 'Do not move your tongue with it to hasten it. It is for Us to gather it and recite it. When We recite it, follow its recitation. Then it is for Us to explain it.'"

- Al-Sharaf al-Musawi, al-Nass wa-l-Ijtihad, p. 248, on the reception of revelation

The Magic Reports Prove the Wrong Thing

The attack appends Aisha's reports of the Prophet's ﷺ bewitchment as evidence that Satan gained power over him. Al-Kurani carries the text.

"Al-Bukhari related this fable from Aishah in five places, one of them at 4/91. 'From Aishah, she said, the Prophet was bewitched. Al-Layth said that Hisham wrote to him that he had heard and retained it from his father, from Aishah. - Aisha's report in Sahih al-Bukhari, relayed in Ali al-Kurani, Alef Su'al wa Ishkal, vol. 3, p. 293

4. The Cranes Story Is the Other School's Report

The Cranes Story Is the Other School's Report

How the School Files the Story

The earliest tafsir of the school introduces the story with a filing label, and the label is the argument's half.

فان العامة رووا ان رسول الله ﵌ كان في الصلاة فقرأ سورة النجم في مسجد الحرام وقريش يستمعون لقراءته فلما انتهى إلى هذه الآية " أفرأيتم اللات والعزى ومنات الثالثة الأخرى " اجرى إبليس على لسانه " فإنها للغرانيق الأولى وان شفاعتهن لترتجي " ففرحت قريش وسجدوا وكان في القوم الوليد بن المغيرة المخزومي وهو شيخ كبير فأخذ كفا من حصى فسجد عليه وهو قاعد ، وقالت قريش قد أقر محمد بشفاعة اللات والعزى ، قال فنزل جبرئيل فقال له جبرئيل قد قرأت ما لم انزل عليك وانزل عليه " وما أرسلنا من قبلك من رسول ولا نبي إلا إذا تمنى ألقى الشيطان في أمنيته فينسخ الله ما يلقى الشيطان " .

"The common people reported that the Messenger of Allah was praying when he recited Surat al-Najm in the Sacred Mosque, and Quraysh listened to his recitation. When he reached the verse, 'Have you considered al-Lat and al-Uzza, and Manat, the third and last,' Iblis made him say, 'They are the high cranes, and their intercession is hoped for.' Quraysh rejoiced and prostrated. Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah al-Makhzumi was among them. He was an old man. He took a handful of pebbles and prostrated on them while seated. Quraysh said, 'Muhammad has accepted the intercession of al-Lat and al-Uzza.' Jibril then came down and said to him, 'You have recited what I did not send down to you.' Allah then sent down, 'We sent no messenger or a prophet before you, but when he formed a wish, Satan cast something into his wish. Allah then abolishes what Satan casts.'"

- Ali ibn Ibrahim al-Qummi, Tafsir al-Qummi, vol. 2, p. 85, on Qur'an 22:52

The Imam's Reading of the Same Verse

The same page of the corpus then gives the school's own reading of 22:52, and it has no cranes in it at all.

وأما الخاصة فإنهم رووا عن أبي عبد الله (﵇) : «أن رسول الله (﵌) أصابته خصاصة ، فجاء إلى رجل من الأنصار ، فقال له : هل عندك من طعام ؟ فقال : نعم ، يا رسول الله . وذبح له عناقا (2) ، وشواه ، فلما أدناه منه تمنى رسول الله (﵌) أن يكون معه علي وفاطمة والحسن ، والحسين (﵈) . فجاء أبو بكر وعمر ، ثم جاء علي (﵇) بعدهما ، فأنزل الله في ذلك : * (وما أَرْسَلْنا مِنْ قَبْلِكَ مِنْ رَسُولٍ ولا نَبِيٍّ) * ولا محدث * (إِلَّا إِذا تَمَنَّى أَلْقَى الشَّيْطانُ فِي أُمْنِيَّتِه) * يعني فلانا وفلانا * (فَيَنْسَخُ اللَّه ما يُلْقِي الشَّيْطانُ) * يعني لما جاء علي (﵇) بعدهما * (ثُمَّ يُحْكِمُ اللَّه آياتِه) * يعني بنصرة أمير المؤمنين (﵇)»

"The select group reported from Abu Abd Allah, 'The Messenger of Allah suffered hunger. He came to a man of the Ansar and asked him, do you have any food. The man answered, yes, Messenger of Allah. He slaughtered a young goat for him and roasted it. When he brought it near him, the Messenger of Allah wished that Ali, Fatimah, al-Hasan, and al-Husayn were with him. Abu Bakr and Umar came, and then Ali came after them. Allah then sent down, "We sent no messenger or a prophet before you" or one addressed by angels, "but when he formed a wish, Satan cast something into his wish," meaning so-and-so and so-and-so. "Allah then abolishes what Satan casts," meaning when Ali came after them. "Then Allah establishes His verses," meaning by supporting the Commander of the Faithful.'"

- Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq ﵇, on Qur'an 22:52, in al-Bahrani, al-Burhan fi Tafsir al-Qur'an, vol. 3, p. 897, from Tafsir al-Qummi

The Verdict the School Renders

The school's summaries do not weigh the story, they dismiss it, in the categorical language of ismah.

أقول : إنّ هذا لا يجوز على أحد من الأنبياء ؛ لأنّهم معصومون صلوات اللَّه عليهم . (8)

"I say, this can never happen to any of the prophets, because they are protected from error. May Allah's blessings be upon them."

- Al-Ata'iqi al-Hilli, Mukhtasar Tafsir al-Qummi, p. 322, the verdict on the cranes report

أقول : إمّا أن يريد بالتمنّي التلاوة ، أو تمنّي القلب . فإن أراد التلاوة ، كان المعنى : أنّ من ارسل قبلك كان إذا تلا ما يؤدّيه إلى قوم حرّفوه ، كما فعلت اليهود . وأضاف ذلك إلى الشيطان ؛ لأنّه بوسوسته وغروره . وإن أراد (6) تمنّي القلب ، كان : متى تمنّى بقلبه بعض ماتمنّاه ، يوسوس إليه بالباطل ويحدّثه بالمعاصي ، فإنّه تعالى ينسخ ذلك ويبطله . والأحاديث المروية في هذا الباب كثيرة (7) ، فلا يلتفت إليها في الباب ؛ لأنّها مخالفة للعقل والنقل . (8)

"I say, tamanna may mean recitation, or the wish of the heart. If it means recitation, the meaning is that whoever was sent before you, when he recited what he delivered to his people, they corrupted it, as the Jews did, and this was attributed to Satan because of his whispering and deceit. If it means the wish of the heart, the meaning is that whenever he wished for something in his heart, Satan would whisper falsehood to him and speak to him of sins, and Allah abolishes that and cancels it. Many reports are transmitted in this chapter, and none of them deserves attention here, because they conflict with reason and with transmission."

- Al-Ata'iqi al-Hilli, Mukhtasar Tafsir al-Qummi, p. 323, weighing the two readings of tamanna

A Report From Ali Himself

The corpus also carries the school's own account of where the story came from, attributed to the first Imam.

Will you dispute with him over what he sees?'"

- Imam Ali ﵇, in al-Aqa'id al-Islamiyya, vol. 3, p. 54, from Hilal al-Abrar, vol. 2, p. 125

- Al-Suyuti's chains for the cranes report, in Abha al-Midad, vol. 2, p. 62

ومن التدليس ما فعله الفخر الرازي عندما قال في تفسيره : 23 / 49 : (وأيضاً فقد روى البخاري في صحيحه أن النبي (ص) قرأ سورة النجم وسجد فيها المسلمون والمشركون والإنس والجن ، وليس فيه حديث الغرانيق ! وروي هذا الحديث من طرق كثيرة وليس فيها البتة حديث الغرانيق) ! !

"One example of concealment is what Fakhr al-Razi did when he said in his commentary at 23/49, 'Al-Bukhari also related in his Sahih that the Prophet recited Surat al-Najm, and the Muslims, the idolaters, mankind, and the jinn prostrated in it, and it contains no report of the cranes. This report came through many chains, and not one of them contains any report of the cranes.'"

- Ali al-Kurani, Alef Su'al wa Ishkal, vol. 2, p. 206, on Fakhr al-Razi and the cranes report

5. Naskh Is Ruling History, Not a Change of Author

Naskh Is Ruling History, Not a Change of Author

What Naskh Names

The school's own definition of naskh carries no authorship transfer in it anywhere.

ثامنا - النسخ : النسخ في اللغة : إزالة شيء بشيء يتعقبه ، يقال : نسخت الشمس الظلّ (1) . وفي المصطلح الاسلامي : نسخ أحكام في شريعة بأحكام في شريعة أخرى (2) . مثل نسخ بعض أحكام الشرائع السابقة بأحكام في شريعة خاتم الأنبياء ( ص ) (3) . وكذلك نسخ حكم مؤقت بحكم أبديّ في شريعة خاتم الأنبياء ( ص ) ، مثل نسخ حكم توارث المتآخيين من المهاجرين والأنصار في المدينة قبل فتح مكّة بحكم توارث ذوي الأرحام بعد فتح مكّة .

"Eighth, abrogation. In language, abrogation means the removal of one thing by another that follows it. People say the sun abrogated the shadow, meaning it removed it. In Islamic terminology, it means the replacement of rulings in one religious law by rulings in another, such as the replacement of some rulings of the earlier laws by rulings in the law of the seal of the prophets. It also includes the replacement of a time-bound ruling by a lasting ruling in the law of the seal of the prophets, such as the replacement of the ruling by which the paired Emigrants and Helpers inherited from one another in Medina before the conquest of Mecca by the ruling of inheritance by kinship after the conquest of Mecca."

- Al-Sayyid Murtada al-Askari, al-Qur'an wa Riwayat al-Madrasatayn, vol. 1, p. 325, on naskh

نعم قد استدل على عدم وقوعه في الخارج بقوله تعالى (1) : لا يَأْتِيهِ الْباطِلُ مِنْ بَيْنِ يَدَيْهِ وَلا مِنْ خَلْفِهِ ، بتوهم أن النسخ ابطال للقرآن . والجواب ان بيان الغاية ممن بيده البيان ليس ابطالا ، كيف والقرآن ينص على جواز النسخ في قوله تعالى (2) : ما نَنْسَخْ مِنْ آيَةٍ أَوْ نُنْسِها نَأْتِ بِخَيْرٍ مِنْها أَوْ مِثْلِها فلا مانع عقلا من النسخ ولا دليل سمعا على عدم وقوعه . المطلب الثالث [ البحث في موارد النسخ ] قد أطال العلماء البحث في موارد النسخ ولا سيما علماء العامة ، ففي - الاتقان - للسيوطي في المسألة السابعة من النوع السابع والأربعين في ناسخه ومنسوخه : النسخ في القرآن على ثلاثة أضرب :

"Some have argued that it never occurred by using Allah's words, 'Falsehood does not come to it from before it or from behind it,' assuming that abrogation cancels the Qur'an. The answer is that stating the end of a term, by the One who owns the statement, is not cancellation. The Qur'an itself permits abrogation in His words, 'Whatever verse We abrogate or cause to be forgotten, We bring one better than it or like it.' Reason therefore allows abrogation, and no transmitted proof shows it never occurred. Third topic, the cases of abrogation. Scholars have long examined the cases of abrogation, especially the scholars of the common people. In al-Itqan, al-Suyuti treats it in the seventh question of the forty-seventh type, on what abrogates and what is abrogated. Abrogation in the Qur'an falls into three kinds,"

- Tafsir al-Bayan al-Safi (al-Qubaysi al-Amili), vol. 1, p. 359, on whether naskh touches preservation

The Bound the Other School Draws

The attack asks how a Muslim knows that no satanic verse survived in the Book. The other school's own scholars answer with a rule that closes the door.

الجواب : إن علماء السنة الذين قالوا بجواز نسخ التلاوة أجمعوا على أن هذا النسخ لا يتحقق بلا بدل ، فما ينسخه الله ويرفعه يجب ولازم أن ينزل بدلا عنه ما يقوم مقامه من آيات القرآن ، وهو المستفاد من قوله تعالى ( ما ننسخ من آية أو ننسها نأت بخير منها أو مثلها ) فيجب أن يأتي بخير منها أو بمثلها ، فالبدل لازم وإلا لا يكون هذا المورد من المنسوخ تلاوة .

"Answer. Sunni scholars who permit abrogating recitation agree that this abrogation cannot occur without a replacement. Whatever Allah abrogates and removes must be replaced by Qur'anic verses that take its place. This follows from His words, 'Whatever verse We abrogate or cause to be forgotten, We bring one better than it or like it.' He must bring one better or like it. A replacement is therefore necessary. Otherwise the case cannot count as abrogated recitation."

- Al-Amili, al-Intisar, vol. 3, p. 53, on the replacement rule in naskh of recitation

The Two Verses the Attack Pairs

The attack's showcase is the pairing of 2:178 with 5:32, retaliation scaled by status against the sanctity of every soul, and its charge that 2:178 was worded so badly that it had to be abrogated. A reader of the third century had both verses on one page, and he read them as one law.

وقال سبحانه : مِنْ أَجْلِ ذلِكَ كَتَبْنا عَلى بَنِي إِسْرائِيلَ أَنَّهُ مَنْ قَتَلَ نَفْساً بِغَيْرِ نَفْسٍ أَوْ فَسادٍ فِي الْأَرْضِ فَكَأَنَّما قَتَلَ النَّاسَ جَمِيعاً [المائدة : 32] . فأحل سبحانه من قتل الأنفس بفسادها واعتدائها ، مثل الذي أحل من القتل بالقصاص بينها في دمائها . وقال أيضا ﷾ ، فيما جعل من القصاص بين القتلى يا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْقِصاصُ فِي الْقَتْلى الْحُرُّ بِالْحُرِّ وَالْعَبْدُ بِالْعَبْدِ وَالْأُنْثى بِالْأُنْثى فَمَنْ عُفِيَ لَهُ مِنْ أَخِيهِ شَيْءٌ فَاتِّباعٌ بِالْمَعْرُوفِ وَأَداءٌ إِلَيْهِ بِإِحْسانٍ ذلِكَ تَخْفِيفٌ مِنْ رَبِّكُمْ وَرَحْمَةٌ فَمَنِ اعْتَدى . . . [البقرة : 178] الآية فهذه وجوه ما أحل اللّه به الدماء

"Allah said, 'For this reason We decreed for the Children of Israel that whoever kills a soul, unless for another soul or for spreading corruption in the land, is as if he killed all people' (Qur'an 5:32). Allah made lawful the killing of souls whose corruption and aggression deserved it, just as He made lawful killing in retaliation for blood among them. He also said, about the retaliation He set for those killed, 'Believers, retaliation for those killed has been prescribed for you, the free person for the free person, the slave for the slave, and the woman for the woman. If someone receives pardon from his brother for anything, then let the claim be pursued fairly and let payment be made kindly. This is a relief from your Lord and a mercy. Whoever then transgresses' (Qur'an 2:178), to the end of the verse. These are the ways in which Allah has made the shedding of blood lawful."

- Al-Qasim al-Rassi, Majmu' Kutub wa Rasa'il, vol. 2, p. 236, on the blood verses

The Rule That Ends the List

The school's rule of thumb closes the chapter.

وقال الإمامية : إن الحديث يفسر ويخصص آيات القرآن ، ولا يجوز أن ينسخ آية من آياته ، وإنما تنسخ الآية بآية مثلها .

"The Imamis said, hadith explains and specifies the verses of the Qur'an, and hadith may never abrogate any of its verses. A verse is abrogated only by another verse like it."

- Muhammad Jawad Mughniyya, al-Shi'a fi al-Mizan, p. 316, on the school's rule of specification

6. The Concession the Attack Signs

The Concession the Attack Signs

The attack ends by conceding the case. Its authors write that their conclusion is most likely not what the author of the Qur'an intended to say, and that they advanced it by taking the statements at face value anyway. A reading its own authors withdraw before the argument ends is not a finding about the Qur'an, and the note has shown what the face value of the verses actually is, a wish, a casting at it, an abolition, and signs established after.

The afterthought that maybe Satan authored the abrogating verses, or abrogated the good ones, reverses the one attribution the verses make. The We who abrogates in 2:106 is the We who brings the better, and the One who abolishes in 22:52 is Allah by name, fa-yan-sakhu llah, while Satan appears only as the one whose casting is abolished. No verse, chain, or lexicon entry anywhere in the record names Satan as an abolisher. The reversal is not a reading of the text, it is the attack's own doctrine dressed in the text's vocabulary, and it dies for want of a clause.

The Gospel comparison concedes more than it asks. The attack's own premises hold that the Gospel's crucifixion account stands written in the book Christians keep, and the Qur'an's answer is that a substitution was made to seem to them, which is the Qur'an asserting that the error is in what people did with a book, with Allah stating the correction when He sent it. The six hundred years the attack counts are the age of the keeping, the length of time a community held a changed account, and the comparison testifies that Allah corrects accounts He has a messenger to correct through. The Qur'an claims exactly that correction for itself, falsehood does not come to it, and the claim was grounded above on the other school's own rules. A person who accepts the attack's comparison has accepted a God who lets a kept book drift, and the Muslim case, on both schools, is a Book whose recited text is a closed set from one Author.

The closing questions, how do you know all the satanic interjections were removed before the Prophet's ﷺ death, and how do you know the abrogated verses are not satanic, were both answered inside the verses the attack quotes. The abolition of 22:52 precedes the establishment of the signs, with thumma leaving no interval, so nothing of Satan's casting stands as a sign at any moment. The replacement rule of 2:106, on the other school's own consensus, brings a better or like verse from the same Author for every withdrawn term. The school adds its chain, hadith never abrogates the Book, and only a like verse abrogates a verse. The satanic interjection the attack needs is a text standing in the Book with Satan's name on it, and every rule in the record, both schools' rules, refuses the possibility before it is asked.

Final Verdict

The charge that Satan authored the Qur'an's abrogated verses fails on five grounds, each carried by the sources the attack itself quotes or files under.

First, the words. Tamanna is a wish formed in the soul in the lexicon the tradition uses, and the Qur'an's own use of the verb, five verses after the cranes passage, means wish plainly. Naskh is effacement, the sun and the shadow, a removal that leaves nothing standing. The attack's fixed meanings, recitation and scheduled expiry, are readings argued from tafsir, and the dictionaries refuse to certify them as the words' content.

Second, the grammar. 22:52 separates the prophet's wish, Satan's casting, and Allah's signs, and orders them abolish then establish. What Satan casts is the object of the abolition, never a sign, and no clause in the verse moves it into the Book. 2:106 gives one We for both terms of every exchange, 16:101 answers the forger charge with the Holy Spirit in its own breath, and the qibla, one revealed direction for another, is the tradition's canonical exchange.

Third, the passage the attack leans on. 16:98 addresses whoever recites, and 16:99-100 deny Satan authority over believers in the passage's own words. The Imams of the House ﵈ locate the casting in the whisper to the audience, the reception of revelation is Allah's own task in 75:16-19, and the magic reports the attack appends describe imagined acts that never happened, healed by Allah, with no recitation in them.

Fourth, the story. The cranes report is filed by the school under the other camp's transmissions, answered with the Imams' ﵈ reading of a wish, two intruders, and Ali's ﵇ arrival as the abolition, and dismissed in the categorical language of ismah. The corpus transmits from Ali ﵇ that Quraysh invented it. The other school's own house is divided over it, no cranes text in Bukhari on al-Razi's word, Ibn Abbas reading the umniyya as a wish for his people's Islam inside the Sunni relay itself, and al-Razi defending the Prophet ﷺ before accusing him, with his best defense borrowed from this school's Tanzih al-Anbiya.

Fifth, the doctrine. Naskh is the history of rulings, the earlier laws ending in the final one, time-bound rulings ending in lasting ones, and every verse remains Allah's word. The other school's own rule requires a replacement for every withdrawn recitation, the school's rule forbids hadith from abrogating the Book at all, and the attack's showcase pairing of 2:178 with 5:32 was read as one law of blood a thousand years ago, sanctity with its exception clause and procedure with its remission.

The Qur'an names Satan as the author of no verse. It names him as the caster at the prophets' hopes, whose every cast is abolished by the God who then establishes His signs, and it names the Author of every term of every exchange as itself. The attack's satanic verses are a doctrine its own authors withdraw in their closing paragraphs, built on words the dictionaries widen, grammar the sentence walls off, etiquette the passage answers, and a story the chains discard. The Qur'an the attack indicts says, about the whispering it describes, that Allah abolishes it, and the record of both schools keeps that promise.
