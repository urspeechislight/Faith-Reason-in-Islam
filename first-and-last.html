He Was, and Nothing Else Was

The Verse and the Question

هُوَ ٱلْأَوَّلُ وَٱلْءَاخِرُ وَٱلظَّٰهِرُ وَٱلْبَاطِنُ ۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ شَىْءٍ عَلِيمٌ

Huwa al-awwalu wa-al-akhiru wa-al-zahiru wa-al-batin, wa-huwa bi-kulli shay'in 'alim.

"He is the First and the Last, the Outward and the Inward, and He has knowledge of every thing."

- Qur'an 57:3, Uthmani text
The Question at Its Strongest

Nothing comes from nothing. Every made thing answers to something before it, and the maker himself needs material to work on. A first creation therefore looks impossible from both sides. If God made the first thing from nothing, then something appeared with nothing behind it. If He made it from something, that something either came from God, and a piece of the Maker has entered the world, or the chain of somethings never began at all.

The Imams ﵈ were asked this question by their own people and by challengers who traveled to find them. Their answers survive in the hadith corpus, and the commentators built on those answers for a thousand years. The road below follows the whole exchange.

The Shape of the Reading

The four names of Qur'an 57:3 order the road. The First stands before everything, alone, and the corpus begins there with its strongest chains. The Outward answers the question of the first thing, because the first thing is how the hidden God shows without being divided. The Inward keeps the journey honest, since the showing never hands the seeker God as He is in Himself. The Last gathers the road back to where it started, because the tradition says the world was made for the sake of the first light, and the first light is where the world returns.

Each report and each lexicon entry along the road is a pearl. The reading strings them on one thread, and the thread runs from the first name to the last.

1. The Four Names on the Prophet's Tongue

Contested words. al-Zahir (ٱلظَّٰهِرُ), the third name of Qur'an 57:3. Common renderings give "the Outward" or "the Manifest", and a reader can take these to mean plain visibility, like an object set out in view. The gloss taught by the Imams ﵈ and the entries of the classical lexicons carry a stronger sense, the strength that overtops everything beneath it. The root, the Qur'an's own usage, and the poets decide between the two readings.

A Prayer Placed Before Every Need

Source: al-Kulayni, al-Kafi, vol. 2, Book of Supplication, bab al-tahmid wa-al-tamjid

Speaker: Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq ﵇

Chain: Ali ibn Ibrahim, from his father, from Ali ibn Hassun, from one of the companions of the Imam ﵇

Claim: the praise that opens every prayer names the four names of Qur'an 57:3, one negation to each name

The Report

A companion asked Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq ﵇ what praise suffices before a supplication, and the answer fixed the four names of the verse into a prayer that a believer says before he asks for anything.

عن أبي عبد الله (﵇) قال : كل دعاء لا يكون قبله تحميد فهو أبتر ، إنما التحميد ثم الثناء ، قلت : ما أدري ما يجزي من التحميد والتمجيد ،

قال : يقول : " اللهم أنت الأول فليس قبلك شئ ، وأنت الآخر فليس بعدك شئ ، وأنت الظاهر فليس فوقك شئ ، وأنت الباطن فليس دونك شئ ، وأنت العزيز الحكيم "

"Abu Abd Allah ﵇ said, every prayer that has no praise before it is cut short. Praise comes first, then the declaration of glory. I said, I do not know what suffices of the praise and the glorifying."

"He said, you say, O God, You are the First, and nothing is before You. You are the Last, and nothing is after You. You are al-Zahir, and nothing is above You. You are al-Batin, and nothing is nearer than You. You are the Mighty, the Wise."

- al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, vol. 2, p. 504

The same chain carries a shorter praise for the one who cannot manage more, and its verbs put the four names into motion.

وبهذا الإسناد قال : سألت أبا عبد الله (﵇) ما أدنى ما يجزي من التحميد ؟

قال : تقول : " الحمد لله الذي علا فقهر ، والحمد لله الذي ملك فقدر ، والحمد لله الذي بطن فخبر ، والحمد لله الذي [يميت الاحياء و] يحيي الموتى وهو على كل شئ قدير "

"By the same chain he said, I asked Abu Abd Allah ﵇, what is the least that suffices of the praise?"

"He said, you say, praise belongs to God, who rose high and overpowered. Praise belongs to God, who owns and measures. Praise belongs to God, who is hidden and knows. Praise belongs to God, who makes the living die and gives the dead life, and He is powerful over every thing."

- al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, vol. 2, pp. 504-505
One Negation to Each Name

The prayer gives each name its own fence. The First owns the side before everything, the Last owns the side after everything, al-Zahir owns the height above everything, and al-Batin owns the nearness beneath everything and within it. Two of these fences are easy to say. A child can hear that nothing came before God and nothing outlasts Him. The middle two hold the whole mystery of the question, because height and depth are the two directions in which a created world stands.

The Imam ﵇ set one exact word against al-Zahir. He fenced it with height. God is the one above whom nothing stands, and visibility itself went unmentioned. The name carries highness with its showing. Whether that is the word's real range is a question for the dictionaries, and the dictionaries are the next pearl.

So the prayer already sketches the whole journey. The First is where the road starts, alone. The Outward is the road itself, the showing that fills every height. The Inward is the warning that the showing never hands over the depths of God. And the Last is the end of the road, waiting where the road ends.

What the Root Actually Carries

Source: Ibn Faris, Maqayis al-Lugha, root z-h-r; Ibn Manzur, Lisan al-Arab, root z-h-r

Claim: the root carries one sense, strength joined to coming out into the open, and its noun zuhur means prevailing

The Lexicons

Ibn Faris built his dictionary on the theory that each root holds one core sense, and his entry for this root states it in four words.

ظهر الظاء والهاء والراء أصلٌ صحيح واحدٌ يدلُّ على قوّة وبروز

من ذلك ظَهَر الشىءُ يظهر ظهورا فهو ظاهر ، إِذا انكشف وبرز . ولذلك سمِّى وقت الظُّهر والظَّهيرة ، وهو أظهر أوقات النَّهار وأضوؤُها . والأصل فيه كلّه ظهر الإنسان ، وهو خلافُ بطنه ، وهو يجمع البُروزَ والقوّة

Zahara al-zah wa-al-ha wa-al-ra asl sahih wahid yadullu 'ala quwwa wa-buruz. Zahara al-shay yazharu zhurran fa-huwa zahir idha inkashafa wa-baraz.

"Zah, ha, ra is one sound root that points to strength and protrusion, to coming out into the open."

"From it is zahara al-shay, the thing came out into the open, so it is zahir, when it was uncovered and stood forth. For this reason the time of noon is named Zuhr, the most out-standing of the hours of day and their brightest. The origin of the whole root is the back of the human being, which is the opposite of his front, and the back gathers strength and standing forth."

- Ibn Faris (d.395 AH), Maqayis al-Lugha, vol. 3, p. 471

The same entry then names the branch that decides the question.

والظُّهور : الغَلبة . قال اللَّه تعالى : فَأَصْبَحُوا ظاهِرِينَ

Wa-al-zuhur al-ghalaba.

"And z-hur means prevailing, overcoming. God the Exalted said, and they became zahirin, overtoppers, prevailers."

- Ibn Faris (d.395 AH), Maqayis al-Lugha, vol. 3, p. 471

Ibn Manzur, in the largest of the classical dictionaries, gives the usage at full length. The verb zahara with the particle 'ala means to prevail over, and the overtaken thing is the one underneath.

والظُّهور : الظَّفَرُ بالشيء والإِطلاع عليه

يقال : ظَهَر فلانٌ على فلان أَي قَوِيَ عليه . وفلان ظاهِرٌ على فلان أَي غالب عليه . وظَهَرْتُ على الرجل : غلبته

وظَهَرَ على الشيء إِذا غلبه وعلاه . ويقال : ظَهَرَ على الحائط وعلى السَّطْح صار فوقه

Yugal zahara fulanun 'ala fulan ay qawiya 'alayh, wa-zahartu 'ala al-rajul ghalabtuh, wa-zahara 'ala al-shay idha ghalabahu wa-'alahu.

"Zuhur is victory over a thing and gaining access to it. It is said, a man zahara over another, meaning he had the strength against him. Such a one is zahir over him, meaning prevailing over him. And I zahartu over the man, I overcame him."

"And he zahara over a thing when he overcame it and rose above it. It is said, he zahara over the wall and over the roof, he came to be on top of it."

- Ibn Manzur (d.711 AH), Lisan al-Arab, vol. 4, p. 526, root z-h-r
The Qur'an and the Poets

The Qur'an itself uses the verb for the victory of the believers over their enemies, and Ibn Faris quoted this very verse inside his entry.

فَأَيَّدْنَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ عَلَىٰ عَدُوِّهِمْ فَأَصْبَحُوا۟ ظَٰهِرِينَ

Fa-ayyadna alladhina amanu 'ala 'aduwwihim fa-asbahu zahirin.

"So We strengthened the ones who believed against their enemy, and they became the overtoppers."

- Qur'an 61:14, Uthmani text

The poets show the root's daily life. The line Ibn Faris set at the head of his entry uses the back in its other sense, a thing left behind the back and abandoned.

تميمَ بنَ بدر لا تكوننَّ حاجتي * بِظهرٍ فلا يَخْفى عليك جوابُها

Tamima bna Badrin la takunanna hajati bi-zahrin fa-la yakhfa 'alayka jawabuha.

"Tamim son of Badr, let my need never be a thing cast behind the back, and its answer will not be hidden from you."

- al-Farazdaq (d.110 AH), quoted by Ibn Faris, Maqayis al-Lugha, vol. 3, p. 472

Neither lexicon cites a line of poetry for plain visibility. Their cited lines carry prevailing, the strength of the back, the noon that overtops the hours, and the thing left behind. Showing comes joined to strength in this root, as the back of a man joins the two.

The Name Read Through Its Root

The lexicons settle the gloss of the prayer. Al-Zahir is the one whose showing overtops everything, as noon overtops the hours of the day, and nothing stands above Him. He stands out over all that stands, as noon stands over the hours.

The gloss of the Imam ﵇ and the dictionaries meet exactly, fence against fence. He said nothing is above God, and the dictionaries say the verb means to come out over a thing. The name carries the strength of noon over the day. The world itself is the showing of this name, and that showing is the next question.

So the four names form one day, first light to last light, and the reading now walks from the First, who owned the day before the day began.

2. He Was, and Nothing Else Was

The Sentence with the Strongest Chain

Source: al-Saduq, al-Tawhid, chapter on God's knowledge before creation

Speaker: Imam Muhammad al-Baqir ﵇

Chain: Saduq's father, from Muhammad ibn Yahya al-Attar, from Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Abi al-Khattab, from Ibn Abi Umayr, from Hisham ibn Salim, from Muhammad ibn Muslim

Claim: God was when there was nothing other than Him, and His knowledge of a thing before its existence equals His knowledge of it after

The Report

The fence of the First, planted by the prayer in the first section, stands on one short sentence that the corpus repeats more than any other.

عن أبي جعفر (﵇) قال : سمعته يقول : كان الله ولا شيء غيره ، ولم يزل عالماً بما يكون ، فعِلْمُهُ به قبلَ كونِهِ كعِلْمِهِ به بعدَ ما كَوَّنَهُ

"From Abu Ja'far ﵇, who said, I heard him say, God was, and there was nothing other than Him."

"And He never ceased knowing what would come to be. His knowledge of a thing before its coming to be is like His knowledge of it after He brought it to be."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 145, no. 12

Every narrator of this chain is graded trustworthy in the Shia science of narrators. The sentence it carries closes the first door of the journey. Before the world there was no material lying about, no timeless mold, no second thing of any kind. The Qur'an shuts the same door from the other side.

لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِۦ شَىْءٌۭ

Laysa ka-mithlihi shay'.

"Nothing is like Him."

- Qur'an 42:11, Uthmani text

The Arabic builds a double wall. No thing matches His likeness, and no thing even matches the likeness of His likeness. Whatever the first creation came from, it did not come from a part of Him, because a part would be a thing like Him, and the verse leaves no room for one.

So the First stands alone in a way no oneness of the world can copy, and the world's first problem is solved before it is asked. Nothing shares His before, and nothing matches His what. The next question is how anything stood up at all.

Attributes That Waited for Objects

Source: al-Kulayni, al-Kafi, vol. 1, bab sifat al-dhat

Speaker: Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq ﵇

Chain: Ali ibn Ibrahim, from Muhammad ibn Khalid al-Tayalisi, from Safwan ibn Yahya, from Ibn Muskun, from Abu Basir

Claim: God's attributes are His essence without objects, and when He brought things into being, the attributes fell upon their objects and nothing in God changed

Knowledge with Nothing to Know

The objector could still press the fence of the First from inside. A knower, he would say, needs a known, and a hearer needs something heard. If God never ceased knowing and hearing, then objects of His knowledge stood with Him from the start, and the solitude of the First is broken by the back door. Abu Basir brought that problem to the Imam ﵇, and the answer is one of the most precise sentences in the corpus.

سَمِعْتُ أَبا عَبْدِ اللّهِ (﵇) يَقُولُ : لَمْ يَزَلِ اللّهُ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ رَبَّنَا ، وَالْعِلْمُ ذَاتُهُ وَلَا مَعْلُومَ ، وَالسَّمْعُ ذَاتُهُ وَلَا مَسْمُوعَ ، وَالْبَصَرُ ذَاتُهُ وَلَا مُبْصَرَ ، وَالْقُدْرَةُ ذَاتُهُ وَلَا مَقْدُورَ

فَلَمَّا أَحْدَثَ الْأَشْيَاءَ وَكَانَ الْمَعْلُومُ ، وَقَعَ الْعِلْمُ مِنْهُ عَلَى الْمَعْلُومِ ، وَالسَّمْعُ عَلَى الْمَسْمُوعِ ، وَالْبَصَرُ عَلَى الْمُبْصَرِ ، وَالْقُدْرَةُ عَلَى الْمَقْدُورِ

"I heard Abu Abd Allah ﵇ say, God, mighty and majestic, never ceased to be our Lord, and knowledge was His very essence with nothing known, hearing His essence with nothing heard, sight His essence with nothing seen, power His essence with nothing over which power was held."

"When He brought the things into being and the known came to be, knowledge fell from Him upon the known, hearing upon the heard, sight upon the seen, power upon the empowered."

- al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, vol. 1, p. 503, no. 293

God did not begin to know when He created. Knowing is what He is, and it needed no object, the way the sun needs no viewer to be bright. Creation did not grow anything in Him. The river of His attributes simply began to fall on ground that had not been there, and the falling is called the world.

So the solitude of the First survives its hardest test. The objection from eternal objects fails, because the attributes of God are God, and God waited for nothing. What remains is the maker's side of the riddle, and a scholar from Syria carried that side of it to Medina in person.

Even the Names Were Made

Source: al-Saduq, al-Tawhid, chapter on the Names

Speaker: Imam Muhammad al-Jawad ﵇

Claim: the Names of God were not with Him from eternity; He created them as a means between Himself and His creation

No Eternal Companion of Any Kind

The fence holds against one more claimant. Perhaps, someone might say, the divine Names at least, the all-merciful, the holy, were carved in His presence from the start. A questioner put this to the young Imam Muhammad al-Jawad ﵇, and his written answer left no companion standing.

إِنْ قُلْتَ : إِنَّهَا لَمْ تَزَلْ فِي عِلْمِ اللّهِ ، وَهُوَ مُسْتَحِقُّهَا ، فَنَعَمْ . وَإِنْ كُنْتَ تَقُولُ : لَمْ تَزَلْ تَصْوِيرُهَا وَهِجَاؤُهَا ، وَتَقْطِيعُ حُرُوفِهَا ، فَمَعَاذَ اللّهِ أَنْ يَكُونَ مَعَهُ شَيْءٌ غَيْرُهُ

بَلْ كَانَ اللّهُ ، وَلَا مَعَهُ خَلْقٌ ، ثُمَّ خَلَقَهَا وَسِيلَةً بَيْنَهُ وَبَيْنَ خَلْقِهِ ، يَتَضَرَّعُونَ بِهَا إِلَيْهِ

"If you say that the Names never ceased to be in the knowledge of God, and that He deserved them, then yes. But if you say that their forming and their spelling and the cutting of their letters never ceased with Him, then God forbid that anything be with Him."

"Rather, God was, and no creation was with Him. Then He created them as a means between Himself and His creation, by which they implore Him."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 138

Even the ladder the world climbs to God is a created ladder. The corpus then sings the same solitude as praise, in a report from Jabir.

عَنْ جَابِرٍ عَنْ أَبِي جَعْفَرٍ (﵇) قَالَ : إِنَّ اللّهَ تَبَارَكَ وَتَعَالَى كَانَ وَلَا شَيْءَ غَيْرُهُ نُوراً لَا ظَلَامَ فِيهِ ، وَصَادِقاً لَا كَذِبَ فِيهِ ، وَعَالِماً لَا جَهْلَ فِيهِ ، وَحَيّاً لَا مَوْتَ فِيهِ

وَكَذَلِكَ هُوَ الْيَوْمَ وَكَذَلِكَ لَا يَزَالُ أَبَداً

"From Jabir, from Abu Ja'far ﵇, who said, God, blessed and exalted, was, and there was nothing other than Him, light with no darkness in it, true with no lie in it, knowing with no ignorance in it, living with no death in it."

"So He is today, and so He shall forever be."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 141, no. 5

Here light names God Himself, defined by what it never holds, darkness and lie and ignorance and death. This is the light of the Inward, and no eye meets it. The journey has now circled its starting point. The First is alone, needing nothing, complete in Himself, with even His Names created after Him.

So the fence is absolute, and the cost of the fence is now visible. If nothing stood with God, and nothing can come out of God, the first created thing has no place to come from. The Syrian scholar arrived in Medina holding exactly that knot, and the next section hands it to him.

3. The Question the Syrian Brought

Three Answers Refused

Source: al-Kulayni, al-Kafi, vol. 8, the report of the Syrian scholar

Speaker: Imam Muhammad al-Baqir ﵇

Chain: through al-Husayn ibn Sa'id, from Muhammad ibn Dawud, from Muhammad ibn Atiyya

Claim: the first created thing cannot itself be made from a thing, or the series would never begin, and the first thing is the water, from which all things are made while the water is attributed to nothing before it

The Regress Named and Refused

A scholar of Syria wore himself out on one question. Every teacher he asked gave a different answer, and he came to Medina to ask Imam Muhammad al-Baqir ﵇. His question was the question of the whole journey, what was the first thing God created. The candidates he had collected were the best of the day, the Decree, the Pen, and the Spirit, and the Imam refused all three before answering.

فَإِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عَنْ أَوَّلِ مَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ مِنْ خَلْقِهِ ؟ فَإِنَّ بَعْضَ مَنْ سَأَلْتُهُ قَالَ : الْقَدَرُ ، وَقَالَ بَعْضُهُمْ : الْقَلَمُ ، وَقَالَ بَعْضُهُمْ الرُّوحُ

فَقَالَ أَبُو جَعْفَرٍ (﵇) : مَا قَالُوا شَيْئاً ، أُخْبِرُكَ أَنَّ اللّهَ ﵎ كَانَ وَلَا شَيْءَ غَيْرَهُ ، وَكَانَ عَزِيزاً ، وَلَا أَحَدَ كَانَ قَبْلَ عِزِّهِ ، وَذَلِكَ قَوْلُهُ : سُبْحَانَ رَبِّكَ رَبِّ الْعِزَّةِ عَمَّا يَصِفُونَ ، وَكَانَ الْخَالِقُ قَبْلَ الْمَخْلُوقِ

"I ask you about the first thing God created of His creation. Some of those I asked said the Decree, some said the Pen, and some said the Spirit."

"Abu Ja'far ﵇ said, they have said nothing. I will tell you that God ﷻ was, and there was nothing other than Him. And He was Mighty, and no one was before His might, and that is His word, glory to your Lord, the Lord of Might, above what they describe. And the Creator was before the created."

- al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, vol. 8, p. 118

Then comes the pivot of the whole journey.

وَلَوْ كَانَ أَوَّلَ مَا خَلَقَ مِنْ خَلْقِهِ الشَّيْءَ مِنَ الشَّيْءِ إِذَاً لَمْ يَكُنْ لَهُ انْقِطَاعٌ أَبَداً ، وَلَمْ يَزَلِ اللّهُ إِذَاً وَمَعَهُ شَيْءٌ لَيْسَ هُوَ يَتَقَدَّمُهُ

وَلَكِنَّهُ كَانَ إِذْ لَا شَيْءَ غَيْرُهُ ، وَخَلَقَ الشَّيْءَ الَّذِي جَمِيعَ الْأَشْيَاءِ مِنْهُ وَهُوَ الْمَاءُ الَّذِي خَلَقَ الْأَشْيَاءَ مِنْهُ ، فَجَعَلَ نَسَبَ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ إِلَى الْمَاءِ ، وَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ لِلْمَاءِ نَسَباً يُضَافُ إِلَيْهِ

"Had the first thing He created of His creation been a thing from a thing, then the series would have no ending ever, and God would never cease to have with Him a thing that He did not precede."

"But He was when there was nothing other than Him, and He created the thing from which all things are, and that is the water, from which He created all things. So He traced the lineage of every thing back to the water, and He gave the water no lineage to which it could be traced."

- al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, vol. 8, p. 118

The tradition has a name for this kind of making. Ibdā' means to bring something out with no material and no template before it, an innovation. A shaping works on existing stock, and this act had none. Exactly one act of ibdā' stands at the head of the world. After it, everything else has a lineage, back and back to the water, like sons to a father. The water alone has no lineage behind it.

The Imam then traced the lineage down. The wind was created from the water, and the wind churned the water until foam rose, and earth was made from the foam. Fire was created from the water, and fire boiled it until smoke rose, and heaven was made from the smoke.

When the Syrian asked about the verse that says the heavens and the earth were closed up and split apart, the Imam refused the picture of two stuck sheets being torn from each other. The heaven was closed up with no rain, he said, and the earth was closed up with no growth, and the splitting was rain and harvest. The Syrian ended on his face in recognition, saying the knowledge of this man is the knowledge of the prophets ﵈.

So the fence built in the second section now has a gate, one gate, and the gate is the water. Nothing made the water, and the water is the mother of every made thing. The thread still needs the twin report that says the same thing in the language of the book, with a chain the scholars grade higher.

No Template Before the World

Source: al-Kulayni, al-Kafi, vol. 1, the report of Humran ibn A'yan

Speaker: Imam Muhammad al-Baqir ﵇

Chain: Muhammad ibn Yahya, from Abd Allah ibn Muhammad ibn Isa, from al-Hasan ibn Mahbub, from Ali ibn Ri'ab, from Sadir al-Sayrafi

Claim: God innovated all things by His knowledge upon no exemplar that existed before Him

The Report of Humran

Humran ibn A'yan asked about a word of the Qur'an, the Originator of the heavens and the earth, and the answer restates the gate of the Syrian's report as a doctrine.

إِنَّ اللّهَ عَزَّ وَجَلَّ ابْتَدَعَ الْأَشْيَاءَ كُلَّهَا بِعِلْمِهِ عَلى غَيْرِ مِثَالٍ كَانَ قَبْلَهُ

فَابْتَدَعَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرَضِينَ ، وَلَمْ يَكُنْ قَبْلَهُنَّ سَمَاوَاتٌ وَلَا أَرَضُونَ ، أَ مَا تَسْمَعُ لِقَوْلِهِ تَعَالى : وَكانَ عَرْشُهُ عَلَى الْماءِ

"God, mighty and majestic, innovated all things by His knowledge, upon no exemplar that had been before Him."

"He innovated the heavens and the earths, and before them there were no heavens and no earths. Do you not hear His saying, and His Throne was upon the water?"

- al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, vol. 1, p. 879, no. 666

The chain of this report runs through narrators the school grades trustworthy without dispute. Together with the Syrian's report it closes both doors at once. The Syrian's Imam refused a first thing made from a thing, which refuses the material. Humran's Imam refused a pattern standing before the making, which refuses the template. There was no eternal pattern in the mind of God that creation copied, no timeless blueprint standing beside Him. Both reports quote the verse of the Throne on the water, and the Qur'an carries that verse in full.

وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضَ فِى سِتَّةِ أَيَّامٍۢ وَكَانَ عَرْشُهُۥ عَلَى ٱلْمَآءِ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًۭا

Wa-huwa alladhi khalaqa al-samawati wa-al-arda fi sittati ayyam, wa-kana 'arshuhu 'ala al-ma', li-yabluwakum ayyukum ahsanu 'amala.

"And it is He who created the heavens and the earth in six days, and His Throne was upon the water, to test which of you is finest in deed."

- Qur'an 11:7, Uthmani text

The throne of a king rests on what his kingdom already stands on. God's Throne rested on water when nothing else existed, because the water was all there was to rest on. The first made thing was already the floor under the greatest made thing to come.

So the Syrian's gate now stands on two pillars. Nothing made the water, and no template guided the making. What still hangs is the act itself, the joining of a God who changes nothing to a water that begins. The next pearl answers with the first instrument, and the report after that reaches for a lamp.

The Will Created by Itself

Source: al-Saduq, al-Tawhid, the report of Umar ibn Udhayna

Speaker: Imam Ja'far al-Sadiq ﵇

Claim: God created the Will by means of itself, then created all things by means of the Will

The First Instrument

One more pearl answers the last part of the knot. If everything after the first thing is made through something, what did the very first making use, when nothing existed to be used?

خَلَقَ اللّهُ الْمَشِيَّةَ بِنَفْسِهَا ثُمَّ خَلَقَ الْأَشْيَاءَ بِالْمَشِيَّةِ

"God created the Will by means of itself. Then He created the things by means of the Will."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 148

The Will is the first instrument, and its own making used no instrument, because there was none. Al-Saduq set this report at the head of his doctrine of the attributes, and drew the line that guards the fence of the First from the inside.

وَلَيْسَتِ الْإِرَادَةُ وَالْمَشِيَّةُ وَالرِّضَا وَالْغَضَبُ وَمَا يُشْبِهُ ذَلِكَ مِنْ صِفَاتِ الْأَفْعَالِ بِمَثَابَةِ صِفَاتِ الذَّاتِ ، لِأَنَّهُ لَا يَجُوزُ أَنْ يُقَالَ لَمْ يَزَلِ اللّهُ مُرِيداً شَائِياً كَمَا يَجُوزُ أَنْ يُقَالَ لَمْ يَزَلِ اللّهُ قَادِراً عَالِماً

"The willing, and the wanting, and the being pleased, and the being angry, and what resembles these of the attributes of action, stand apart from the attributes of essence, because it is not permitted to say that God never ceased willing and wanting, as it is permitted to say that God never ceased powerful and knowing."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 148

Power and knowledge are what God is, and they waited for nothing, as the second section showed. Willing is something God does, and doing begins. The Will stands at the head of all doing, and through it runs the whole lineage of the water and everything after the water.

A reader can press the report the way the Syrian pressed his teachers. If the Will was created by means of itself, did the Will make itself? The teaching answers with the fence already built. The Will belongs to the side of doing, the side that begins. God made the first doing with no prior instrument, because no instrument existed, and the making cost Him nothing of His essence, because the lamp loses no light. The phrase marks the single point where the series starts, and it leaves no second start behind the first.

So the three reports now hold one doctrine. The water is the first thing, made from nothing, on no template, through a Will that was itself the first instrument made. Nothing in this doctrine lets any thing come out of God. How the light of the Maker reaches a world it never entered is the work of the next pearl, and that pearl is a lamp.

4. The Lamp and the Mirror

A Theologian at the Door

Source: al-Saduq, al-Tawhid, the disputation with Imran the Sabian

Speaker: Imam Ali al-Rida ﵇

Setting: a public session of theologians, where Imran the Sabian, a master debater of his age, asked his questions

Claim: God created from no need, the first showing is like light from a lamp, and God is neither inside the world nor the world inside Him

The Question of the First Being

Imran the Sabian told Imam Ali al-Rida ﵇ that he had crossed Iraq and Syria meeting the theologians of every school, and none of them could establish for him one God, single, standing alone. Then he asked the question of this journey in his own words, tell me about the First Being, and from what He created. The Imam answered with the fence of the First, that the One never ceased one, with nothing with Him, without limits and without accidents, and then described the first creation.

فَجَعَلَ مِنْ بَعْدِ ذَلِكَ الْخَلْقِ صَفْوَةً وَغَيْرَ صَفْوَةٍ ، وَاخْتِلَافاً وَائْتِلَافاً ، وَأَلْوَاناً وَذَوْقاً وَطَعْماً

لَا لِحَاجَةٍ كَانَتْ مِنْهُ إِلَى ذَلِكَ ، وَلَا لِفَضْلِ مَنْزِلَةٍ لَمْ يَبْلُغْهَا إِلَّا بِهِ ، وَلَا رَأَى لِنَفْسِهِ فِيمَا خَلَقَ زِيَادَةً وَلَا نُقْصَاناً ، تَعْقِلُ هَذَا يَا عِمْرَانُ ؟ قَالَ : نَعَمْ وَاللّهِ يَا سَيِّدِي

"After that creation He made a chosen portion and an unchosen, a differing and a coming together, colors and a taste and a flavor."

"Not from any need of Him for it, and not for a rank of excellence He could reach only by it, and He saw for Himself no increase and no decrease in what He created. Do you grasp this, Imran? He said, yes, by God, my master."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 431

The Imam then named the reason a need can never stand behind the world.

أَنَّهُ لَوْ كَانَ خَلَقَ مَا خَلَقَ لِحَاجَةٍ لَمْ يَخْلُقْ إِلَّا مَنْ يَسْتَعِينُ بِهِ عَلَى حَاجَتِهِ ، وَكَانَ يَنْبَغِي أَنْ يَخْلُقَ أَضْعَافَ مَا خَلَقَ ، لِأَنَّ الْأَعْوَانَ كُلَّمَا كَثُرُوا كَانَ صَاحِبُهُمْ أَقْوَى

وَلِذَلِكَ أَقُولُ لَمْ يَخْلُقِ الْخَلْقَ لِحَاجَةٍ ، وَلَكِنْ نَقَلَ بِالْخَلْقِ الْحَوَائِجَ بَعْضَهُمْ إِلَى بَعْضٍ

"Had He created what He created from a need, He would have created only those who could aid Him in His need, and it would have been right to create many times more than He did, for the more the helpers, the stronger their master."

"For that reason I say He did not create creation from a need. But by creating He transferred needs from one to another."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 431

A needy maker needs his helpers, and a truly needy one needs more help than the world contains. The need the world runs on was placed inside the world, wiring creature to creature, while its Maker stayed outside the wiring. This completes the fence of the First from the side of the act. The second section showed God gaining nothing when the known came to be. This pearl shows God lacking nothing when the world came to be.

So the Maker is complete before and after, and the world begins with no debt on His side. Imran then asked the question the whole journey hangs on, and the answer was a household object.

How Light Comes from a Lamp

Source: al-Saduq, al-Tawhid, the disputation with Imran the Sabian

Speaker: Imam Ali al-Rida ﵇

Claim: the light of a lamp is neither an act of the lamp nor a thing other than the lamp, and this is the likeness of the world's coming from God

The Likeness of the Lamp

Imran pressed the obvious objection. Was God silent before creation and speaking after, changed by what He made? The Imam refused the very shape of the question, and gave the likeness that carries the rest of the road.

وَالْمَثَلُ فِي ذَلِكَ أَنَّهُ لَا يُقَالُ لِلسِّرَاجِ هُوَ سَاكِتٌ لَا يَنْطِقُ ، وَلَا يُقَالُ إِنَّ السِّرَاجَ لَيُضِيئُ فِيمَا يُرِيدُ أَنْ يَفْعَلَ بِنَا

لِأَنَّ الضَّوْءَ مِنَ السِّرَاجِ لَيْسَ بِفِعْلٍ مِنْهُ وَلَا كَوْنٍ ، وَإِنَّمَا هُوَ لَيْسَ شَيْءٌ غَيْرُهُ ، فَلَمَّا اسْتَضَاءَ لَنَا قُلْنَا قَدْ أَضَاءَ لَنَا حَتَّى اسْتَضَأْنَا بِهِ ، فَبِهَذَا تَسْتَبْصِرُ أَمْرَكَ

"The likeness of it is that no one says of a lamp that it is silent and does not speak, and no one says that the lamp shines in order to do something to us."

"For the light of the lamp is neither an act of it nor a production, and it is simply nothing other than the lamp. When it lit us, we said the lamp has shone upon us, until we ourselves shone by it. By this, see your matter clearly."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 434

Every word of the likeness is load-bearing. The light does not act, so the lamp does nothing when it lights a room. The light is not a thing other than the lamp, so nothing left the lamp to travel to the wall. And the room can say truly that the lamp has shone upon it, because the ones in the room really did catch the light. The shining is real on the receiving side and costless on the giving side.

That is the shape of the whole answer. The world comes from God the way light comes from a lamp, as an overflow of what the source is, with no cutting and no carrying and no loss. The fence of the First stays whole, because light is not a piece of the lamp. And the coming is real, because the lit room truly stands in light.

So the knot of the journey is cut by a lamp. Nothing left God, and the world still came from Him. The Imam then showed the same truth from a second direction, with a mirror and a warning.

The Mirror and the Standing Pairs

Source: al-Saduq, al-Tawhid, the disputation with Imran the Sabian

Speaker: Imam Ali al-Rida ﵇

Claim: God is neither in the creation nor the creation in Him, every created thing leans on another, and God alone stands without a supporter

Neither Inside Nor Containing

Imran asked whether God is in the creation or the creation in Him. The Imam refused both sides of the question and offered a test Imran could run on himself.

قَالَ عِمْرَانُ : أَ هُوَ فِي الْخَلْقِ أَمِ الْخَلْقُ فِيهِ ؟ قَالَ الرِّضَا (﵇) : جَلَّ يَا عِمْرَانُ عَنْ ذَلِكَ ، لَيْسَ هُوَ فِي الْخَلْقِ وَلَا الْخَلْقُ فِيهِ ، تَعَالَى عَنْ ذَلِكَ

وَسَأُعَلِّمُكَ مَا تَعْرِفُهُ بِهِ وَلَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ أَخْبِرْنِي عَنِ الْمِرْآةِ ، أَنْتَ فِيهَا أَمْ هِيَ فِيكَ ؟

"Imran said, is He in the creation, or the creation in Him? Al-Rida ﵇ said, glory beyond that, Imran. He is not in the creation, nor the creation in Him, exalted is He above it."

"And I will teach you that by which you may know it. Tell me about the mirror, are you in it, or is it in you?"

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 434

A face is in a mirror and not in it. Nothing of the face entered the glass, and the face lost nothing, and the image truly stands there for every eye. The world holds God the way the mirror holds the face, by showing alone.

Then the Imam gave the deepest reason of all for the world's design.

وَلَمْ يَخْلُقْ شَيْئاً فَرْداً قَائِماً بِنَفْسِهِ دُونَ غَيْرِهِ ، لِلَّذِي أَرَادَ مِنَ الدَّلَالَةِ عَلَى نَفْسِهِ وَإِثْبَاتِ وُجُودِهِ

وَاللّهُ تَبَارَكَ وَتَعَالَى فَرْدٌ وَاحِدٌ لَا ثَانِيَ مَعَهُ يُقِيمُهُ وَلَا يَعْضُدُهُ وَلَا يُمْسِكُهُ ، وَالْخَلْقُ يُمْسِكُ بَعْضُهُ بَعْضاً بِإِذْنِ اللّهِ وَمَشِيَّتِهِ

"And He created nothing as a solitary standing by itself apart from the rest, because of what He willed of pointing to Himself and establishing His existence."

"And God, blessed and exalted, is the single One, with no second beside Him upholding Him, or supporting Him, or holding Him. And creation holds up one another, by God's leave and His will."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-Tawhid, p. 439

Nothing in the world stands alone, and that is the point of the world. Every creature leans, air on sun, root on rain, soul on body. The leaning of each on the next is the loudest sermon creation preaches. A universe of mutual debt needs someone outside the debt to pay for the whole of it. God alone is the One with no second to hold Him up, and the pairs are the proof that everything else is held.

So the lamp says how the world comes, shining with no loss, and the pairs say why the world is needy, made to lean. The likeness of the lamp named the first showing as light, and the corpus goes further. It says the first thing God made was in fact a light, and that light had a name.

5. The First Light and the Water

The Report of the First Light

Source: al-Majlisi, Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 54, the reports of the first creation

Speaker: the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, from Jabir ibn Abd Allah al-Ansari

Claim: the first thing God created was the light of the Prophet ﷺ, from it He split the light of Ali ﵇, and from the two came the Throne, the Tablet, the sun, the light of day, sight, intellect, and knowledge

The Report

The lamp of the fourth section was a likeness. The corpus now gives the likeness a name, and the name is a person.

وَعَنْ جَابِرٍ ، قَالَ : قَالَ رَسُولُ اللّهِ (﵌) أَوَّلَ مَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ نُورِي ، فَفَتَقَ مِنْهُ نُورَ عَلِيٍّ (﵇) ، ثُمَّ خَلَقَ الْعَرْشَ وَاللَّوْحَ وَالشَّمْسَ وَضَوْءَ النَّهَارِ وَنُورَ الْأَبْصَارِ وَالْعَقْلَ وَالْمَعْرِفَةَ

"From Jabir, who said, the Messenger of God ﷺ said, the first thing God created was my light. From it He split open the light of Ali ﵇."

"Then He created the Throne, and the Tablet, and the sun, and the light of day, and the light of the eyes, and the intellect, and knowledge."

- al-Majlisi (d.1111 AH), Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 54, p. 172

The order of the list carries the doctrine. Every light a reader can name comes after the one first light and comes out of it. The sun over his head is its borrower, and so are the day that wakes him, the sight in his eyes, and the intellect he thinks with. Prophecy in this report is older than the human race, because the light of the Prophet ﷺ stood before the Throne, and the Throne stood before Adam. The Prophet ﷺ said it in his own words elsewhere in the corpus, that he was a prophet while Adam was still between water and clay.

The tradition grades the chains of this cluster strictly. The reports of the first light travel through narrators the scholars of the school dispute. The chain question stands open around this report, as it will stand open around the creed cluster of the sixth section. The teaching is the shape of the showing. The lamp analogy of the fourth section explains how a light can come from a source with no loss, and this report says what the lamp of the world is.

So the Outward of the first section now has a face. The showing of God into creation runs through one created light, first of all made things, and every later light is its borrower.

Water and Light, One Beginning

Source: al-Kulayni, al-Kafi, vol. 15, report of Muhammad ibn Muslim; Sadr al-Din al-Shirazi, Sharh Usul al-Kafi, vol. 1

Speakers: Imam Muhammad al-Baqir ﵇; the commentator Mulla Sadra

Claim: all things were water, commanded into fire and smoke and ash, and the many names of the first creation name one thing under different aspects

The Commanded Water

The third section planted the water at the head of creation, from the Syrian's report. A second report, whose chain runs through narrators the school grades trustworthy, shows the water under command, turning into the world by orders.

قَالَ لِي أَبُو جَعْفَرٌ (﵇) : كَانَ كُلُّ شَيْءٍ مَاءً ، وَكَانَ عَرْشُهُ عَلَى الْمَاءِ ، فَأَمَرَ اللّهُ الْمَاءَ فَاضْطَرَمَ نَاراً ، ثُمَّ أَمَرَ النَّارَ فَخَمَدَتْ ، فَارْتَفَعَ مِنْ خُمُودِهَا دُخَانٌ

فَخَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ مِنْ ذَلِكَ الدُّخَانِ ، وَخَلَقَ الْأَرْضَ مِنَ الرَّمَادِ

"Abu Ja'far ﵇ said to me, everything was water, and His Throne was upon the water. God commanded the water, and it blazed into fire. Then He commanded the fire, and it went out, and from its going out smoke rose."

"He created the heavens from that smoke, and He created the earth from the ash."

- al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, vol. 15, p. 235, no. 14883

The passage shows one material, one chain of commands, and the world as their history. The water was never shaped from outside by a craftsman's tools. It heard, and it became.

So the two great report families of the first creation now stand side by side, the family of the water and the family of the light, and a reader can ask whether they compete. The commentator Mulla Sadra answered with the reports themselves in hand.

وَهُوَ الْمُرَادُ فِيمَا وَرَدَ فِي الْأَحَادِيثِ عَنْهُ صَلَّى اللّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَآلِهِ مِنْ قَوْلِهِ فِي رِوَايَةٍ : أَوَّلُ مَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ الْعَقْلُ ، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ : أَوَّلُ مَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ نُورِي ، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ : أَوَّلُ مَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ رُوحِي ، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ : أَوَّلُ مَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ الْقَلَمُ ، وَفِي رِوَايَةٍ : أَوَّلُ مَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ مَلَكٌ كَرُوبِيٌّ

وَهذِهِ كُلُّهَا أَوْصَافٌ وَنُعُوتٌ لِشَيْءٍ وَاحِدٍ بَاعْتِبَارَاتٍ مُخْتَلِفَةٍ ، فَبِحَسَبِ كُلِّ صِفَةٍ يُسَمَّى بِاسْمٍ آخَرَ ، فَقَدْ كَثُرَتِ الْأَسْمَاءُ وَالْمُسَمَّى وَاحِدٌ ذَاتاً وَوُجُوداً

"And this is what is intended in what has come in the hadiths from him, may God bless him and his family, where he says in one report, the first thing God created was the Intellect, and in one report, the first thing God created was my light, and in one report, my spirit, and in one report, the Pen, and in one report, a cherubic angel."

"All of these are descriptions of one single thing under different considerations. According to each aspect it is called by another name, so the names have multiplied while the named is one in essence and in existence."

- Sadr al-Din al-Shirazi, Mulla Sadra (d.1050 AH), Sharh Usul al-Kafi, vol. 1, p. 231

The names differ by the angle of sight, as one mountain wears a different face from every road. Call the first creature Intellect when it is known as the first knowing light. Call it Pen when it is the first writer of destiny, Spirit when it is the first living breath, and light when it is the first showing. The reading of this journey sets the water of the Syrian's report at the head of the same series in its own aspect, as the first material under the first form.

So water and light are two windows on one dawn, and the quarrel between the report families dissolves. The first thing is one, first in line and first in rank, and the Qur'an has a name for how it stands before God.

Light upon Light

Source: Qur'an 24:35; Qadi Sa'id al-Qummi, Sharh Tawhid al-Saduq, vol. 1

Claim: the lamp of God is lit from a blessed tree, light upon light, and the commentator reads the great book of the world as opened by the Muhammadan light

The Verse of the Lamp

The Qur'an itself joins the lamp of the fourth section to the light of this one.

ٱللَّهُ نُورُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۚ مَثَلُ نُورِهِۦ كَمِشْكَوٰةٍۢ فِيهَا مِصْبَاحٌ ۖ ٱلْمِصْبَاحُ فِى زُجَاجَةٍ ۖ ٱلزُّجَاجَةُ كَأَنَّهَا كَوْكَبٌۭ دُرِّىٌّۭ يُوقَدُ مِن شَجَرَةٍۢ مُّبَٰرَكَةٍۢ زَيْتُونَةٍۢ لَّا شَرْقِيَّةٍۢ وَلَا غَرْبِيَّةٍۢ يَكَادُ زَيْتُهَا يُضِىٓءُ وَلَوْ لَمْ تَمْسَسْهُ نَارٌۭ ۚ نُّورٌ عَلَىٰ نُورٍۢ

Allahu nuru al-samawati wa-al-ard, mathalu nurihi ka-mishkatin fiha misbah, al-misbahu fi zajaja, al-zajajatu ka-annaha kawkabun durriyun yuqadu min shajaratin mubarakatin zaytunatin la sharqiyyatin wa-la gharbiyya, yakadu zaytuha yudu'u wa-law lam tamsashu nar, nurun 'ala nur.

"God is the light of the heavens and the earth. The likeness of His light is a niche, and in the niche a lamp, and the lamp in a glass, and the glass as a star of brilliance, lit from a blessed olive tree that is neither of the east nor of the west, whose oil almost shines though no fire touch it. Light upon light."

- Qur'an 24:35, Uthmani text

The verse sets a lamp in a glass in a niche, fed by a tree that belongs to no one horizon, with oil that almost lights itself. The verse stacks vessels inside vessels, and every vessel is transparent. The commentator Qadi Sa'id al-Qummi, reading the Imran dialogue of the fourth section, says what the stack is.

وَلَعَلَّ تَأْوِيلَهُ انَّهُ تَعَالَى افْتَتَحَ كِتَابَهُ الْكَبِيرَ الْمُسَمَّى بِالْعَالَمِ الْأَكْبَرِ وَالْإِنْسَانِ الْكَبِيرِ بِالْحَمْدِ الْجَامِعِ ، وَالنُّورِ السَّاطِعِ ، الْبُرْهَانِ الْقَاطِعِ ، الْمُسَمَّى بِالْقَلَمِ وَالْعَقْلِ الْأَوَّلِ وَالرُّوحِ الْأَعْظَمِ وَالنُّورِ الْمُحَمَّدِيِّ وَالْحَقِيقَةِ الْمُحَمَّدِيَّةِ

"And perhaps its meaning is that He, exalted, opened His great book, named the greater world and the greater human, with the gathering praise and the blazing light, the decisive proof, named the Pen, and the First Intellect, and the greatest Spirit, and the Muhammadan light, and the Muhammadan reality."

- Qadi Sa'id al-Qummi (d.1103 AH), Sharh Tawhid al-Saduq, vol. 1, p. 774

The world is a book, and the first word of the book is a light, and the names of that first word are the names of the first section's dawn. Light upon light reads then as the whole architecture of the journey. God is the light of the heavens and the earth, the way the sun is the light of a hundred lit rooms, by shining, and the shining never enters. The first created light is the first of the lenses. Every later lamp of the world is a smaller lens in the stack.

The reader of the fifth section now holds every strand. The First was alone. His showing is the name al-Zahir, the out-topping noon. The showing began as one light that is also water, intellect, pen, and spirit. What no strand has answered yet is the question a child asks at a fountain, who turned it on, and for whom it pours.

So the world now has a first word, and a first word begs a reason for the sentence. The next pearl is the reason, and the reason is love.

6. Why the Worlds Were Made

The Hidden Treasure

Source: Sadr al-Din al-Shirazi, Sharh Usul al-Kafi, vol. 3, on the sacred saying of the hidden treasure

Speaker: God ﷻ, in the sacred saying, as read by Mulla Sadra

Claim: creation is the mirror in which the hidden treasure is known, and God is veiled by the intensity of His showing

The Sacred Saying

The fourth section met a mirror, and it met a lamp. The tradition joins both images in one sacred saying, and Mulla Sadra set it in a single breath with the sun and the cloud.

فَالشَّمْسُ آيَةٌ مِنْ آيَاتِ نُورِهِ وَالسَّحَابُ الْحِجَابُ مِرْآةٌ مِنْ مَرَايَا ظُهُورِهِ ، لَوْلَا احْتِجَابُهُ بِنَا لَمَا ظَهَرَ لَنَا ، فَالْخَلْقُ مَرَايَا نُورِهِ وَالْحُجُبُ الْعَالَمِيَّةُ وَسَائِطُ ظُهُورِهِ

كُنْتُ كَنْزاً مَخْفِياً لِفَرْطِ ظُهُورِي فَأَحْبَبْتُ أَنْ أُعْرَفَ فَخَلَقْتُ الْخَلْقَ وَاحْتَجَبْتُ بِهَا لِيَظْهَرَ نُورِي

"The sun is a sign of His light, and the cloud the veil, a mirror of His showings. Were He not veiled by us, He would not appear to us. So creation is the mirrors of His light, and the cosmic veils are the go-betweens of His showing."

"I was a hidden treasure, and I loved to be known, so I created creation, and veiled Myself by it, that My light might appear."

- Sadr al-Din al-Shirazi, Mulla Sadra (d.1050 AH), Sharh Usul al-Kafi, vol. 3, p. 445

The saying reverses the obvious picture of hiding. God is hidden by the very strength of His showing, the way the noon sun blinds the naked eye that looks for it. So He made mirrors that can bear a ray at a time, and the making of the mirrors is what creation is, and the reason for the making is love of being known.

The wording of this saying returns to the first section. Sadra says mirrors of His showings, and the word for showings is the plural of the very z-h-r the lexicons fixed in section one. The Outward does His outwardness through a world of lenses, and the hidden treasure is loved and known precisely in the stack of light upon light.

So the fountain has its why. The worlds were made to be mirrors, and the mirrors were made for love of being known. The next pearl names the first and dearest mirror of all.

For the Sake of a Light

Source: al-Saduq, al-I'tiqadat, the creed of the school; al-Namazi, Mustadrak Safinat al-Bihar, the collected lawla reports

Claim: God created all things for the sake of the Prophet ﷺ and his House ﵈, and the reports say it in God's own voice

The Creed and the Reports

Al-Saduq wrote the creed of the Imami school in plain numbered articles, and one article states the final cause of creation without decoration.

وَأَنَّ اللّهَ تَعَالَى خَلَقَ جَمِيعَ مَا خَلَقَ لَهُ وَلِأَهْلِ بَيْتِهِ ، وَأَنَّهُ لَوْلَاهُمْ لَمَا خَلَقَ اللّهُ السَّمَاءَ وَالْأَرْضَ ، وَلَا الْجَنَّةَ وَلَا النَّارَ ، وَلَا آدَمَ وَلَا حَوَّاءَ ، وَلَا الْمَلَائِكَةَ وَلَا شَيْئاً مِمَّا خَلَقَ

"And that God created everything He created for his sake and for the sake of the people of his house, and that were it not for them, God would not have created the heaven and the earth, nor the Garden, nor the Fire, nor Adam, nor Eve, nor the angels, nor any thing He created."

- al-Saduq (d.381 AH), al-I'tiqadat, p. 95

The reports behind the creed speak in God's own voice, and the collectors gathered them under the particle lawla, were it not.

وَعِزَّتِي وَجَلَالِي لَوْلَاكَ لَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْأَفْلَاكَ . مَنْ أَحَبَّكَ أَحْبَبْتُهُ . وَمَنْ أَبْغَضَكَ أَبْغَضْتُهُ

فِي حَدِيثِ الْمِعْرَاجِ ... قَالَ تَعَالَى : فَلَوْلَاكُمْ مَا خَلَقْتُ الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةَ وَلَا الْجَنَّةَ وَالنَّارَ

فِي رِوَايَةِ الْمُفَضَّلِ عَنِ الصَّادِقِ (﵇) ... بَعْدَ مَا رَأَى آدَمُ أَسْمَاءَ النَّبِيِّ وَالْأَئِمَّةِ عَلَى سَاقِ الْعَرْشِ قَالَ تَعَالَى : لَوْلَاهُمْ مَا خَلَقْتُكُمَا

"By My might and My majesty, were it not for you, I would not have created the spheres. Whoever loves you, I love him, and whoever hates you, I hate him."

"In the report of the night journey, the Exalted said, were it not for you all, I would not have created this world and the next, nor the Garden, nor the Fire."

"In the report of al-Mufaddal from al-Sadiq ﵇, after Adam saw the names of the Prophet and the Imams ﵈ on the leg of the Throne, the Exalted said, were it not for them, I would not have created the two of you."

- al-Namazi al-Shahru-di (d.1406 AH), Mustadrak Safinat al-Bihar, vol. 3, p. 167

Adam himself, in the last of these reports, is told he exists for the sake of others. The first light of the fifth section stands at the beginning of the world, and these reports stand at its heart, and both say the same thing from two ends. The world is the mirror, the first light is the first and clearest mirror, and every other mirror exists so that the beloved might be known in a fullness one mirror alone could not carry.

The scholar of hadith weighs chains, and this cluster is famous and its chains are debated in the books of the school. The creed states it as belief, and the corpus transmits it in many report families, and the reader holds it with both hands, receiving it as devotion and weighing its chains.

So the showing has a why, and the why has a face. The last pearl of the descent asks how the showing is guarded, so that the treasure stays hidden while the light is shown.

The Veils and the Standing Proof

Source: al-Majlisi, Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 18, the night journey reports

Claim: between God and His creation stand thousands of veils, and the first veil is light

The First Veil Is Light

The night journey reports describe what the Prophet ﷺ passed as he rose, and one of them counts the coverings.

أَنَّ بَيْنَ اللّهِ وَبَيْنَ خَلْقِهِ تِسْعِينَ أَلْفَ حِجَابٍ ، وَأَقْرَبُ الْخَلْقِ إِلَى اللّهِ أَنَا وَإِسْرَافِيلُ ، وَبَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَهُ أَرْبَعَةُ حُجُبٍ : حِجَابٌ مِنْ نُورٍ ، وَحِجَابٌ مِنْ ظُلْمَةٍ ، وَحِجَابٌ مِنْ الْغَمَامِ ، وَحِجَابٌ مِنْ الْمَاءِ

"Between God and His creation are ninety thousand veils. The nearest of creation to God are I and Israfil, and between us and Him are four veils, a veil of light, a veil of darkness, a veil of cloud, and a veil of water."

- al-Majlisi (d.1111 AH), Bihar al-Anwar, vol. 18, p. 329

The stack of the veils repeats the stack of the niche. Light, darkness, cloud, and water are themselves creatures, and the first of them is light, which returns the journey to its own first pearl. The light that was first created is also the first veil, because a showing that overtopps everything is by that same strength a covering. The noon that fills the sky blinds the eye that looks for the sun itself.

And the nearest of creation, standing inside the four veils, is a person. The corpus says in many reports that the earth is never left without a proof of God, a hujja, and that were the proof to depart, the earth would sink with its people. The showing of God into the world runs through the first light, and the first light is carried by persons, prophet to Imam ﵈ down the generations. The mirror of the world never goes dark in any age.

So the descent is complete from its top to its floor. Alone, then a will, then a light that is water, then the mirrors and the veils, then the persons who carry the light, and under it all the ground that stands because they stand. The last section walks the road back upward.

7. The Ladder of Unveiling

How the Unseen Is Shown

Source: Sadr al-Din al-Shirazi, Sharh Usul al-Kafi, vol. 2, on the ranks of unveiling

Claim: unveiling has ranks, some by meaning and some by form, and the forms reach the senses of the one granted them

The Two Orders of Unveiling

The road down has now been walked from the solitude of the First to the veils of light. The road up is called kashf, unveiling, and Mulla Sadra set out its ranks.

أَنَّ الْكَشْفَ مِنْهُ مَعْنَوِيٌّ وَمِنْهُ صُورِيٌّ ، وَعَيَّنُوا بِالصُّورِيِّ مَا يَحْصُلُ فِي عَالَمِ الْمِثَالِ مِنْ طَرِيقِ الْحَوَاسِّ الْخَمْسِ

وَذَلِكَ إِمَّا عَلى طَرِيقِ الْمُشَاهَدَةِ كَرُؤْيَةِ الْمُكَاشَفِ صُورَ الْأَرْوَاحِ الْمُتَجَسِّدَةِ وَالْأَنْوَارِ الرُّوحَانِيَّةِ ، وَإِمَّا عَلى طَرِيقِ السَّمَاعِ ، كَسَمَاعِ النَّبِيِّ صَلَّى اللّهُ عَلَيْهِ وَآلِهِ الْوَحْيَ النَّازِلَ عَلَيْهِ كَلَاماً مَنْظُوماً أَوْ مِثْلَ صَلْصَلَةِ الْجَرَسِ وَدَوِيِّ النَّحْلِ

"Unveiling is of two kinds, some of meaning and some of form. By the formal they mean what comes about in the world of images through the five senses."

"This is either by way of direct sight, as when the one granted unveiling sees the forms of embodied spirits and the lights of the world of spirit, or by way of hearing, as when the Prophet ﷺ heard the revelation descending upon him as ordered speech, or as the ringing of a bell and the humming of bees."

- Sadr al-Din al-Shirazi, Mulla Sadra (d.1050 AH), Sharh Usul al-Kafi, vol. 2, p. 477

The unseen does not stay unseen for everyone. It wears forms, the same forms dreams wear, and the senses of the one granted unveiling receive those forms the way waking eyes receive light. The commentator adds smell and touch, citing the report that God has breaths in the days of a lifetime, and the touch of light upon light. Every sense can become a door.

So unveiling is the road up the same staircase the world came down. The descent ran from meaning into matter, and the unveiling runs the ladder backward, sense by sense, image by image, until meaning stands bare of form.

The Door in the Chest

Source: a maxim carried by the commentators of both communities; recorded in Ibn Abi al-Hadid, Sharh Nahj al-Balagha, vol. 20

Claim: the tradition compresses the whole ascent into one line, whoever knows himself knows his Lord

The Shortest Road

The fourth section showed every creature leaning on another, with God alone standing. The ascent begins the moment a reader turns that teaching on himself, and the corpus has compressed the turn into one line, carried by the commentators of both communities.

مَنْ عَرَفَ نَفْسَهُ فَقَدْ عَرَفَ رَبَّهُ

"Whoever knows himself knows his Lord."

- Ibn Abi al-Hadid (d.656 AH), Sharh Nahj al-Balagha, vol. 20, p. 292

A library of treatises grew around this one line. Self-knowledge here is deeper than inspection. To know the self is to find it leaning, needing, held in being at every breath by what it is not. That finding is already knowledge of the Lord. The self, honestly known, is a pointer from first to last. The commentary tradition around this line fills volumes, Persian and Arabic, century upon century, each one unfolding the same fold.

So the mirror of the fourth section turns around. The world mirrors God to the eye that looks outward, and the self mirrors Him to the eye that looks in, and both roads meet inside one chest.

Present in Every Place

Source: Muhammad Sadiq al-Ruhani, al-Tawhid, on the four names, citing a report of the Commander of the Faithful ﵇

Claim: oneness has its showing in its hiddenness and its hiddenness in its showing, sought in every place and never absent from one

The Report of the Turning Names

The corpus holds one more pearl that turns the four names of the anchor verse into a single wheel, and it comes from the Commander of the Faithful ﵇.

التَّوْحِيدُ ظَاهِرُهُ فِي بَاطِنِهِ ، وَبَاطِنُهُ فِي ظَاهِرِهِ ، ظَاهِرُهُ مَوْصُوفٌ لَا يُرَى ، وَبَاطِنُهُ مَوْجُودٌ لَا يَخْفَى ، يُطْلَبُ بِكُلِّ مَكَانٍ ، وَلَمْ يَخْلُ عَنْهُ مَكَانٌ طَرْفَةَ عَيْنٍ ، حَاضِرٌ غَيْرُ مَحْدُودٍ ، وَغَائِبٌ غَيْرُ مَفْقُودٍ

"Oneness has its outward in its inward and its inward in its outward. Its outward is described and never seen, and its inward exists and never hides."

"It is sought in every place, and no place is empty of it for the blink of an eye. Present and unlimited, absent and never lost."

- Muhammad Sadiq al-Ruhani, al-Tawhid, vol. 1, p. 84, citing a prophetic report by way of the Commander of the Faithful ﵇

The wheel turns both ways at once. The showing of God never shows Him as He is, because the outward is described and never seen. The hiding of God never truly hides Him, because the inward exists and never hides. The seeker who looks for God in the world finds Him sought in every place, and the seeker who despairs of finding Him in the world finds no place without Him. The Imam Ali ﵇ said the same in one line that the corpus preserves beside these, that he never saw a thing without seeing God before it.

That is the unveiling the whole journey was walking toward. The Outward without the Inward becomes an idol of light, a God reduced to his nearest mirror. The Inward without the Outward becomes an empty sky, a God so hidden that no road reaches Him. Held together, the names give what the prayer of the first section asked for, a God who fills every height and every depth and is never contained by either.

The wheel of the names has a last turn, and the corpus gives the turn a scene. On the day when every borrowed light goes out, one light keeps burning, and it is the light this journey met first.

وقد روى أنه تطفأ سائر الأنوار إلا نور محمد (﵌) ، ثم يعطى المخلصون من أصحابه أنواراً يسيرة يبصرون بها مواطئ الاقدام ، فيدعون إلى الله تعالى بزيادة تلك الأنوار وإتمامها

ثم إن الله تعالى يتم نور محمد (﵌) ، فيستطيل حتى يملأ الآفاق ، فذلك هو إتمام نوره (﵌)

"It has been related that on that day every light is put out except the light of Muhammad ﷺ. Then the sincere among his companions are given small lights, enough to see where they set their feet, and they call upon God the Exalted to increase those lights and complete them."

"Then God the Exalted completes the light of Muhammad ﷺ, and it stretches out until it fills the horizons, and that is the completion of His light."

- Ibn Abi al-Hadid (d.656 AH), Sharh Nahj al-Balagha, vol. 6, p. 142, a commentator of the other school

The first light of the fifth section returns here as the last light standing. The report carrying it reaches this journey through a commentator of the Sunni school, and a witness from the far side is the hardest kind to dismiss. The radiance that was the first thing made is the thing still burning when every other lamp goes out, and the faithful cross the darkness of that day inside it.

Creation opens in his light, the world unfolds beneath it, and the return runs back along it. That is the exhibit the fourth name needed, and the fourth fence of the prayer now has its proof. The Last is the one after whom nothing comes, and the last light belongs to the first of creatures.

So the ladder stands complete. The names that opened the journey close it, and one step remains, the gathering of all four into a single reading.

Glossary of Key Terms

isnad: the chain of named narrators that carries a report from its source to its recorder, the object of the Shia and Sunni grading sciences.

ibdā': making something with no material and no template before it, an innovation, the single act by which the first creature came from nothing.

mashī'a: the Will, the first instrument of creation, which God created by means of itself before creating all things through it.

hujja: the Proof of God upon the earth, the bearer of the light in every generation, whose presence the reports tie to the earth's continuance.

kashf: unveiling, the opening of the unseen to a servant's sight or hearing, ranked by the commentators into kinds and degrees.

hadith qudsi: a sacred saying, words God ﷻ speaks in the first person outside the Qur'an, carried by a prophetic report.

The Reading

He is the First. Before every thing He was, alone, with nothing beside Him and nothing missing in Him. His knowledge needed no object and His power no field, and even the Names the world would call Him by were created after Him. The fence around that firstness is absolute, and the Qur'an seals it with the words that nothing is even as His likeness.

He is the Outward. One thing was made first, from nothing, on no template, through a Will that was itself the first instrument. That first thing is called light and water, intellect and pen and spirit, one dawn under many names. From it the world descends like light from a lamp, real on the side of the lit room and costless on the side of the flame.

The world is the mirror of a hidden treasure who loved to be known. The dearest mirror is the light of the Prophet ﷺ and his House ﵈, for whose sake the reports say the spheres were set turning. Every creature leans on another and proclaims by its leaning the One who leans on nothing, and in every age a bearer of the light stands inside the veils, the first veil being light itself.

He is the Inward. The showing never hands over the shown. Outward without inward becomes an idol of light, and inward without outward becomes an empty sky. The wheel of the Commander of the Faithful ﵇ turns them together. He is described and never seen, existing and never hidden, sought in every place, absent from none. Whoever knows himself finds in his own need the first thread of the road back.

And He is the Last. The road that went out from the solitude of the First returns to Him, because the treasure created the mirrors in order to be known, and being known is where the road was always going. The prayer of the first section said the four fences, and the journey ends inside the fourth. Nothing before Him, nothing after Him, nothing above Him, nothing nearer than He, and the light that was the first of His gifts is the light the last day finds still burning.
