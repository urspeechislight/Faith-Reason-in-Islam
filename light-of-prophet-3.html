Hadith of the Light

Part 3: The Cosmic Stack and the Throne

Bihar al-Anwar, Volume 15, pages 29 to 33. Continued from Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇.

Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇ continued:

When God Almighty created the earth, it rocked and swayed. Nothing held it steady. So God created an angel of immense stature and set the earth upon his shoulders. But the angel had nothing to stand on, and the earth still rocked.

So God created a rock, green like a emerald, wider than the seven heavens and the seven earths. He set the angel upon the rock. But the rock had nothing beneath it, and the earth still rocked.

So God created a bull, mighty beyond measure, with forty thousand horns and forty thousand legs. He set the rock upon the back of the bull. But the bull had nothing to stand on, and the earth still rocked.

So God created a whale, vast and shimmering, and set the bull upon the whale. The whale rested on the water. The water rested on the air. The air rested on the darkness.

And what is beneath the darkness? No created being knows. That is where the trail ends.

Then God Almighty spoke of His Throne. He said:

"My Throne is woven from two radiances. The first is the radiance of My Grace, by which I show mercy to whom I will. The second is the radiance of My Justice, by which I punish whom I will. Whoever comes to Me through Grace, I receive with Grace. Whoever comes to Me through Justice, I judge with Justice."

And from these two radiances God drew forth a chain of inner qualities. From Grace came mercy, generosity, patience, and forgiveness. From Justice came might, sovereignty, awe, and reckoning. Each quality was a pillar of the Throne, and each pillar held a portion of the light of Muhammad ﷺ within it.

Before Adam was ever shaped, the moral and intellectual substances of all creation were kneaded together and mixed into the clay of the Prophet ﷺ. Every good quality that would later appear in human souls was drawn from that same kneading. He was the first carrier of every virtue, and every virtue that exists in the world traces back to the clay of Muhammad ﷺ.

Then God commanded the light of Muhammad ﷺ to rest beneath the Throne. And it rested there for a length of time that only God knows, glorifying Him night and day without ceasing.

After that long rest, God commanded the light to descend. So it descended through the seventh heaven, and the angels of the seventh heaven gathered around it, marvelling at its beauty. They had never seen anything like it.

Then it descended through the sixth heaven, and the fifth, and the fourth, and the third, and the second, and the first. At each heaven the angels gathered and asked: "Our Lord, what is this light that passes through our midst?" And the answer came:

"This is the light of My beloved, Muhammad ﷺ. From him I created the heavens and the earths. From him I made Paradise and the Fire. From him I shaped the angels and the prophets. He is the reason for all that exists."

And the light descended until it reached the earth, where it was entrusted to Adam, the first of its bearers in the human line.

Commentary

The Cosmic Stack

The chain of supports (earth on angel, angel on rock, rock on bull, bull on whale, whale on water, water on air, air on darkness) is one of the most discussed passages in classical cosmology. The entities described carry animal names to make immense cosmic realities graspable. Many classical scholars treated these as symbolic descriptions of cosmic forces rather than literal animals.

The Darkness That Stops the Trail

The cosmology ends at darkness, and what lies beneath it is sealed off from any created mind. This is a humility clause built into the narration. The Imam takes the listener up to the edge of what can be known, then closes the door.

Why the Throne Appears Twice

The first passage, in Part 2, tells how the Throne came to exist from the Prophet's light. The second passage, here in Part 3, tells what the Throne is made of in terms of divine qualities: Grace and Justice. One answers "from what," the other answers "of what."

Kneaded Into the Clay of the Prophet

Before Adam, the moral and intellectual substances of creation are mixed into the very material of the Prophet's being. He is the original carrier of every good quality, and everything good that appears later in human souls is drawn from that same kneading.
