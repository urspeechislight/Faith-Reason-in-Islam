A Hadith Series

The Light of the Prophet

The journey of the prophetic light from before creation to the birth of Muhammad ﷺ, narrated by Imam Ali ﵇ and Imam al-Sadiq ﵇ in Bihar al-Anwar Volume 15.

A Cosmological Hadith

Before the Throne, before the Pen, before the heavens and the earth, before Adam and the angels, God created a single light. This narration, preserved in Bihar al-Anwar Volume 15 (pages 27 to 57), traces that light from its first moment through twelve veils, twenty seas, the cosmic architecture of the universe, the creation of Adam, and the long chain of generations that carried it down to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. It is a cosmological hadith: a text about the structure of existence and the place of the Prophet within it.

The narration opens with Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇ addressing the gathering at Kufa, and continues in later sections through Imam al-Sadiq ﵇. The commentary that follows each section explains the theology, the symbolism, and the way the text has been read across the centuries.

The Series

Part 1: Before All Things

God creates the light of Muhammad ﷺ four hundred and twenty-four thousand years before the rest of creation. The light passes through twelve veils, glorifying God under each divine name.

Part 2: Twenty Seas and Cosmic Architecture

The light descends through twenty seas of virtue. From it scatter the 124,000 prophets. A jewel is split in two, and from its halves God builds the Throne, the water, the heavens, and the earth.

Part 3: The Cosmic Stack and the Throne

The earth, the angel, the rock, the bull, the whale, the darkness. The Throne is woven from two radiances. The light of the Prophet rests beneath the Throne and descends through the seven heavens.

Part 4: The Creation of Adam

Gabriel is sent for clay. Iblis interferes at the first step. The body is built from the seven waters and the virtues. The spirit enters, the first sneeze, the first word spoken.

Part 5: The Light in Adam and the First Marriage

Eve is created. The wedding in Paradise with a dowry of blessings on Muhammad ﷺ. Five lights placed on five fingers. Seth is conceived.

Part 6: The Succession to Seth

Adam takes the pledge. All of Paradise falls silent. The silk, the pen, and seventy thousand angels bear witness to the first written succession.

Part 7: The Light Through the Generations to Hashim

The genealogy from Seth to Hashim. How the light becomes visible. The pure womb pledge. Hashim's character and his offices in Mecca.

The Full Genealogy

All forty-two generations from Adam ﵇ to Muhammad ﷺ, with notes on the chain of transmission.
