Hadith of the Light

Part 6: The Succession to Seth

Bihar al-Anwar, Volume 15, pages 46 to 49. Continued from Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇.

← Faith & Reason in Islam Commentary

Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇ continued:

When Adam felt the weight of his years and knew that his time on the earth was drawing near its close, he gathered his children and his children's children around him. They came from every corner where they had settled, and they stood before their father, the first of the human race.

Adam raised his head to speak. And at that moment, every sound in Paradise stopped.

The angels ceased their glorification. The wings that had been beating since creation folded and went still. The rivers of Paradise stopped flowing. The trees stopped rustling. The wind held its breath. Not a single voice was raised, not a single whisper was heard. The whole of creation leaned in, because the transfer of the prophetic light from one bearer to the next was about to take place, and the universe would not miss a single word of it.

Adam spoke. He told his children of the trust that God had placed in him: the light of Muhammad ﷺ, the five lights of the five, the deposit that had been given to him before the heavens and the earth were made. He told them that this light could only pass to one, the one whom God had chosen, and that the chosen one was Seth.

"O my son Seth, God has chosen you to carry what I have carried. Guard the light. Keep it in purity. Pass it only to the one whom God designates. The line of this light will run through your descendants until it reaches the one for whom the heavens were made. Do not let it fall into unworthy hands."

Then Adam called for a piece of silk, white and luminous, finer than anything woven on the earth. And he called for a pen, made of light. He wrote upon the silk the pledge of succession, the wasiyya, recording that the trust of the prophetic light had passed from Adam to Seth, by the command of God, with His seal upon it.

And God sent down seventy thousand angels to bear witness to the writing. They descended in ranks, their wings spread wide, and they stood around Adam and Seth as the pledge was recorded. Each angel placed its mark upon the silk, confirming what had been written.

Then God Almighty set His own seal upon the document. And the seal glowed with a light that no created eye could bear to look at directly.

When the pledge was complete, Adam placed the silk in the hands of Seth. And with it, the light passed. The five lights that had rested in Adam's body entered into Seth, and he became their new bearer.

Adam lived for a time after that, and then God took his spirit. The earth that had given him clay received him back. The first man returned to the dust from which he had been made, but the light he had carried did not die with him. It lived on in Seth, and from Seth it would travel, father to son, chosen bearer to chosen bearer, across the whole length of human history.

And the silk, with its writing and its seals, remained with Seth as the proof of his authority. It would pass from heir to heir alongside the light itself, a document that recorded what God had ordained and the angels had witnessed.

Commentary

All of Paradise Falls Silent

When Adam raises his head to speak at the succession ceremony, every sound in Paradise stops. The angels stop their glorification. The wings fold. The rivers stop running. The transmission of the prophetic light from one bearer to the next is treated as a cosmic event the whole universe leans in to witness.

The First Written Succession Document

Adam writing the pledge to Seth, with angelic witnesses and a divine seal, establishes the practice of recording succession in writing. Authority is not just spoken. It is documented, witnessed, and sealed. The document itself (the wasiyya) would pass from heir to heir alongside the light, serving as tangible proof of the chain of designation.
