What the council converged on, and what shipped

  • Flipped negation ("the selection verses say the opposite") — rebuilt in batch 1. Verified by all round-2 and round-3 seats.
  • Foreknowledge — staged in batch 1 (causal answer), closed in batch 3 (certain vs must; B stays open; the counterfactual stated outright: had the servant chosen B, God would have known B).
  • Created desires vs agent causation — joined in batch 1 through the motive-versus-necessitating-cause argument (fire produces burning; a desire is obeyed at one hour and refused at the next).
  • Decree verses — 37:95-96 met with al-Murtada's tafsir (batch 1); 2:6-7 and 35:8 quoted in their own wording with their self-discharge read in order (batch 3); 16:93 read as the parable's leaving-alone (batch 2).
  • The sealing mechanism — the Kafi rana narration of Imam al-Baqir (report 2430) on Qur'an 83:14 added in batch 2: the covering is earned, repentance erases it.
  • qada — grounded in Lisan al-Arab vol.15 p.186 (judgment and command versus completion and creation), the Merv choice made explicit.
  • Attribution fix — the Bihar 5:24 report's speaker corrected from al-Jawad to Imam al-Hadi (Abu al-Hasan al-Thalith, verified against Bihar vol.50 and al-Tusi's Rijal).
  • Citation integrity — 13:11, 18:29, 91:7-10, 2:6-7, 35:8 re-grounded to Uthmani API bytes with byte-exact asserts on the final file; the unverifiable al-Qubaysi and al-Insan cites removed.
