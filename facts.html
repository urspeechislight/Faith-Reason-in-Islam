And Those Who Have Not Menstruated: The Imami Reading of Qur'an 65:4

The Readings Weighed

ReadingBasisQualifierDetail
The clause reaches down to the pre-pubescent girlHeld by the majority of exegetes, and inside the school by al-Murtada, who read "have not menstruated" as "have not reached it" and gave menopausal and pre-pubescent alike a three-month ʿidda in every case, and by Ibn Zuhra, who called it the ruling caution requires; Al Asfur (d.1216 AH) records it as following the opponents, against the school's soundest viewreported↗
The three months engage only inside doubt; where the boundary is passed or not yet reached, there is no ʿiddaMu'awiya ibn Hukaym's answer to Ibn Sama'a, who had cited the verse for three months: God said "if you are in doubt," and the girl who has not reached the boundary "has no waiting period"; al-Kulayni (d.329 AH), al-Kafi, Furu', vol.11, p543; also al-Tusi, Tahdhib al-Ahkam, vol.08, p138, and al-Istibsar, vol.03, p338reported↗
The conjunct inherits the whole conditional frame: "and those who have not menstruated, likewise, if you are in doubt, three months"Abd al-Qahir al-Jurjani (d.471 AH), Dala'il al-I'jaz, p224: the wāw asserts nothing beyond co-sharing in the ruling; Ibn Aqil (d.769 AH), Sharh Alfiyyat Ibn Malik, vol.01, p246: the clause is conjoined to "and those who have despaired," its elided ruling restored as kadhālik, "likewise"stated↗
The verse's doubt is an adult question: a cycle that has run past a month, not the small child whose age has not comeImam Ja'far al-Sadiq, via al-Halabi, glossed the doubt as "what exceeds a month," the state of the woman who does not menstruate (al-Kafi, cited in al-Mizan, vol.19, p321); al-Irwani states the argument: doubt is inconceivable except for one at the age of those who menstruate (Durus Tamhidiyya fi al-Fiqh al-Istidlali, vol.02, p424)stated↗
"Those who have not menstruated while being at the age of women who menstruate," one adult population with the menopausal classAllamah Tabataba'i (d.1402 AH), al-Mizan fi Tafsir al-Qur'an, vol.19, p316: the clause is an ʿaṭf on "and those who have despaired" and shares its framestated↗
