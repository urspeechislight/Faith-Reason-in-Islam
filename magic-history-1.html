How Sorcery Began

The Shia narrations trace sorcery to the satans who eavesdropped on heaven and fed mixed lies to the soothsayers, until God sent two angels with the antidote and shut the heavens at the Prophet's ﷺ mission. From al-Qummi, al-Ayyashi, al-Mashhadi, al-Bahrani, al-Majlisi, and al-Hurr al-Amili.

The satans used to climb toward heaven and steal whatever words they could hear. They mixed lies into the stolen words and fed them to the soothsayers of the earth, who wrote them down and taught them to the people. After Noah ﵇ the craft had spread so far that sorcerers and deceivers were everywhere, and God answered it from heaven itself. That answer stands at Babylon, and it is the next part of this history.

A Roof Kept from the Satans

The Qur'an calls the sky a kept roof, and al-Qummi's gloss on the verse names what the keeping guards against.

وقوله : (وجعلنا السماء سقفا محفوظا) يعني من الشياطين اي لا يسترقون السمع

"His words, "We made the sky a protected roof," mean that it is protected from devils, so they cannot listen secretly."

- Ali ibn Ibrahim al-Qummi, Tafsir al-Qummi, vol.2, p.70

So heaven's own speech needed a guard. The satans wanted that speech because a true word stolen and bent carries further than a plain lie. Al-Qummi carries the whole story of that guarding under the verses of Surat al-Hijr, and the story runs from the night the Prophet ﷺ was born to the day Iblis himself came to look.

ولقد جعلنا في السماء بروجا) قال منازل الشمس والقمر (وزيناها للناظرين) بالكواكب (وحفظناها من كل شيطان رجيم إلا من استرق السمع فاتبعه شهاب مبين) قال لم تزل الشياطين تصعد إلى السماء وتتجسس حتى ولد النبي ﵌ وروي عن آمنة أم النبي ﵌ انها قالت لما حملت برسول الله ﵌ لم اشعر بالحمل ولم يصبني ما يصيب النساء من ثقل الحمل ورأيت في نومي كأن آتيا اتاني فقال لي قد حملت بخير الأنام ثم وضعته يتقي (قابض ك) الأرض بيديه وركبتيه ورفع رأسه إلى السماء وخرج مني نور أضاء ما بين السماء إلى الأرض ورميت الشياطين بالنجوم وحجبوا من السماء ورأت قريش الشهب تتحرك وتزول وتسير في السماء ففزعوا وقالوا هذا قيام الساعة واجتمعوا إلى الوليد بن المغيرة وكان شيخا كبيرا مجربا فسألوه عن ذلك فقال انظروا إلى هذه النجوم التي يهتدى بها في ظلمات البر والبحر فان كانت قد زالت فهي الساعة وان كانت هذه ثابتة فهو لامر قد حدث وكان بمكة رجل يهودي يقال له يوسف فلما رأى النجوم تتحرك وتسير في السماء خرج إلى نادي قريش فقال : يا معشر قريش ! "Al-Qummi says, "We placed in the sky great signs" means the stations of the sun and the moon. "And We adorned it for the beholders" means with the stars. "And We guarded it from every rejected satan, except whoever steals a hearing, and then a clear flame pursues him." Al-Qummi said, the satans never stopped climbing to the sky and spying until the Prophet was born. It is related from Amina, the mother of the Prophet, that she said, when I carried the Messenger of Allah I did not feel the pregnancy, and no weight of pregnancy touched me as it touches women. I saw in my sleep as if someone came to me and said to me, you have carried the best of mankind. Then I gave birth to him, and he braced the earth with his hands and his knees, and he raised his head toward the sky, and a light came out of me that lit everything between the heaven and the earth. The satans were pelted with stars and barred from the sky. Quraysh saw the shooting stars moving, shifting, and traveling in the sky. They panicked and said, this is the rising of the Hour. They gathered before al-Walid ibn al-Mughira, who was an old and experienced man, and they asked him about it. Al-Walid said, look at these stars by which people find their way in the darkness of land and sea. If they have shifted, then it is the Hour, and if they stand firm, then something has happened. There was a Jewish man in Mecca named Yusuf. When he saw the stars moving and traveling in the sky, he went out to the gathering of Quraysh and said, O company of Quraysh, has a child been born among you tonight? They said, no. Yusuf said, you are mistaken. By the Torah, the last of the prophets and the best of them has been born on this night, the one we find in our books, that when that prophet is born the satans are pelted with stars and barred from the sky. Every man among them went back to his house and asked his household. They said, a son has been born to Abd Allah ibn Abd al-Muttalib. The Jew said, show him to me. They walked with him to the door of Amina and said to her, bring out your son so this Jew may look at him. She brought him out in his swaddling clothes. He looked into his eyes and uncovered his shoulder and saw a black mark with hair on it, and he fell to the ground unconscious. They laughed at him. Yusuf said, do you laugh, O company of Quraysh? This is the prophet of the sword. He will destroy you, and prophethood has left the Children of Israel forever. The people scattered, spreading the news of the Jew. When the satans were pelted with the stars and they could not tell why, they gathered before Iblis and said, we have been barred from the sky, and we have been pelted with shooting stars. Iblis said, search, for something has happened in the world. They went and returned and said, we saw nothing. Iblis said, I will see to it myself. He ranged between the East and the West until he reached the Sanctuary, and he found it surrounded by the angels, with Gabriel at the gate of the Sanctuary holding a spear. Iblis wanted to enter, and Gabriel shouted at him and said, get back, accursed one. He came from the direction of Hira and turned himself into the shape of a small bird. Then Iblis said, O Gabriel, one word I ask you. Gabriel said, and what is it? Iblis said, what is this, and why have you all gathered in the world? Gabriel said, this is the prophet of this community. He has been born, and he is the last of the prophets and the best of them. Iblis said, is there a share for me in him? Gabriel said, no. Iblis said, then in his community? Gabriel said, yes. Iblis said, I am content."

- Ali ibn Ibrahim al-Qummi, Tafsir al-Qummi, vol.1, p.399-400

The heavens shut on the night of the birth, and the shut door is the wall this history keeps meeting. The satans still held the stock of words they had already stolen, and the soothsayers still held their trade. ▼ commentary

The Stolen Words and the Soothsayers

Al-Mashhadi records how the theft worked and where the stolen words went.

كانوا يسترقون السّمع ، ويضمّون إلى ما سمعوا أكاذيب ، ويلقونها إلى الكهنة ، وهم يدوّنونها ، ويعلَّمون النّاس . وفشى ذلك في عهد سليمان ، حتّى قيل : إنّ الجنّ يعلم الغيب . وإن ملك سليمان تمّ بهذا العلم . وإنّه تسخّر به الإنس والجنّ والرّيح له .

"They listened secretly, added lies to what they heard, and passed it to the soothsayers. The soothsayers wrote it down and taught it to the people. This spread during Solomon's time, until people said that the jinn knew the unseen. They said Solomon's kingdom rested on this knowledge, and that by it humankind, the jinn, and the wind were subdued to him."

- al-Mashhadi, Tafsir Kanz al-Daqa'iq, vol.2, p.99

The soothsayers became the craft's teachers, and the trade reached ordinary people. Al-Ayyashi records a question al-Baqir ﵇ was asked about the Qur'an's words on men who sought refuge with the jinn, and the practice behind those words.

في تفسير علي بن إبراهيم باسناده إلى زرارة قال : سألت أبا جعفر ﵇ عن قول الله وانه كان رجال من الانس يعوذون برجال من الجن فزادوهم رهقا قال : كان الرجل ينطلق إلى الكاهن الذي يوحى إليه الشيطان فيقول : قل للشيطان فلان قد عاذبك .

"In the tafsir of Ali ibn Ibrahim, with his chain to Zurara. Zurara said, I asked Abu Jafar about Allah's words, "Men from humankind sought refuge with men from the jinn, and they increased them in burden." He answered, "A man would go to a fortune-teller inspired by a devil and say, Tell the devil that so-and-so has sought refuge with you.""

- al-Baqir, Tafsir al-Ayyashi, carried in Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.5, p.436

The refuge-seeker went to the soothsayer because the soothsayer had a line to the listening satans. And the satans themselves kept walking among people. Abd al-Salam asked al-Sadiq ﵇ how a man can guard himself when the danger is unseen, and received the answer that the listener comes in a body.

في تفسير العياشي عن بكر بن محمد الأزدي عن عمه عبد السلام عن أبي عبد الله ﵇ قال : قال يا عبد السلام : احذر الناس ونفسك ، فقلت : بأبي أنت وأمي اما الناس فقد أقدر على أن أحذرهم ، فاما نفسي فكيف ؟ قال : إن الخبيث المسترق السمع يجيئك فيسترق ثم يخرج في صورة آدمي ، فقال عبد السلام : فقلت : بأبي وأمي هذا ما لا حيلة له قال : هو ذاك (2) .

"In the tafsir of al-Ayyashi, from Bakr ibn Muhammad al-Azdi, from his uncle Abd al-Salam, from Abu Abd Allah. Abu Abd Allah said, Abd al-Salam, beware of people and of yourself. Abd al-Salam replied, "May my father and mother be your ransom. I can beware of people, but how can I guard myself?" Abu Abd Allah answered, "The wicked one who listens secretly comes to you, listens, then appears in human form." Abd al-Salam said, "May my father and mother be your ransom. There is no way to prevent this." Abu Abd Allah replied, "That is how it is.""

- al-Sadiq , Tafsir al-Ayyashi, carried in Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.3, p.4

Iblis Makes the First Idols

Before the Flood, Iblis built a different trap. He did not steal words from heaven; he made images that would stand in for the dead.

علل الشرائع : أبي ، عن سعد ، عن ابن عيسى ، عن محمد البرقي ، عن حماد بن عيسى ، عن حريز ، (1) عن جعفر بن محمد ﵇ في قول الله ﷿ : وقالوا لا تذرن آلهتكم ولا تذرن ود أولا سواعا ولا يغوث ويعوق ونسرا ، قال : كانوا يعبدون الله ﷿ فماتوا فضج قومهم وشق ذلك عليهم ، فجاءهم إبليس لعنه الله فقال لهم : أتخذ لكم أصناما على صورهم فتنظرون إليهم وتأنسون بهم وتعبدون الله ، فأعد لهم أصناما على مثالهم فكانوا يعبدون الله ﷿ ، وينظرون إلى تلك الأصنام ، فلما جاءهم الشتاء والأمطار أدخلوا الأصنام البيوت فلم يزالوا يعبدون الله ﷿ حتى هلك ذلك القرن ونشأ أولادهم ، فقالوا : إن آباءنا كانوا يعبدون هؤلاء ، فعبدوهم من دون الله ﷿ ، فذلك قول الله تبارك وتعالى : " ولا تذرن ود أولا سواعا " الآية .

"Ilal al-Shara'i reports, from my father, from Sa'd, from Ibn Isa, from Muhammad al-Barqi, from Hammad ibn Isa, from Hariz, from Jafar ibn Muhammad, on God's words, "And they said, do not leave your gods, and do not leave Wadd, or Suwa, or Yaghuth, or Yauq, or Nasr." Jafar said, they used to worship God. Then they died, and their people cried out in grief, and their deaths weighed on them. Iblis, may God curse him, came to them and said, "Shall I make idols in their likeness, so you can look at them, find comfort in them, and worship God?" He made idols in their image for them. They used to worship God while looking at those idols. When winter and rain came, they brought the idols inside their homes. They went on worshipping God until that generation passed away and their children grew up. The children said, "Our fathers used to worship these." So they worshipped them apart from God. That is the word of God, the Blessed and Exalted, "Do not leave Wadd or Suwa," to the end of the verse."

- al-Sadiq, Ilal al-Shara'i, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.3, p.252

He took a cloth and worn garments, threw them into the sacred enclosure, and set them on fire at night. In the morning, the people found that the house, the idol, and the guards had burned. The broken idol lay scattered. They grieved and intended to kill Yauq. He said to them, "If you kill your leader, your affairs will fall apart." So they held back. Soon Yauq died and left behind a son named Nasr. Iblis came to them and said, "I have heard of your leader's death. I will make an image of Yauq for you from something that will never wear away." They said, "Do it." He took gold and heated it until it became like water. He made a clay form shaped like Yauq, poured the gold into it, and set it up in their monastery. This caused Nasr great pain. He could not enter that monastery. He withdrew with a small group of his brothers, and they worshipped Nasr. The others worshipped the idol. Nasr died. Idris received prophethood. He learned that the people worshipped a body shaped like Yauq, and that Nasr was worshipped apart from God. He marched toward them with those who were with him until he came down upon their city of Nasr and routed them in it. He killed whom he killed, and whoever fled scattered through the lands. He ordered the idol carried away and thrown into the sea. Every group among them then made itself an idol and gave it one of those names. Generation after generation, they knew nothing but those names. Then the prophethood of Noah appeared, and he called them to worship God alone and to abandon the idols they had been worshipping. Some of them said, "Do not leave your gods. Do not leave Wadd, or Suwa, or Yaghuth, or Yauq, or Nasr.""

- al-Baqir, Qisas al-Anbiya, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.3, p.252-254

Commentary

The Stolen Words

Source: Tafsir Kanz al-Daqa'iq, vol.2, p.99; Tafsir al-Qummi, vol.2, p.70 and vol.1, p.399-400; Tafsir al-Ayyashi in Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.5, p.436 and vol.3, p.4

Claim: sorcery began as words stolen from heaven, mixed with lies, and taught by the soothsayers

Speaker: al-Mashhadi; al-Qummi; al-Baqir ﵇

The school does not begin the history of magic with a rebel angel or a forgotten empire. It begins with theft and counterfeiting, a true word stolen and diluted with lies until the mixture could be sold. This matters for everything that follows, because it makes the sorcerer's capital always second-hand. The soothsayer never speaks first. He repeats what a satan claims to have overheard, and the Qur'an guarded the sky itself against the claim.

The Eye, the One Power Left

The First Idols

Source: Bihar al-Anwar vol.3 p.252-254

Claim: Iblis created the first idols as images of righteous dead men, and each generation worshipped the image itself

Speaker: al-Sadiq ﵇; al-Baqir ﵇

The first sorcery needed no spell. Iblis offered the grieving a picture of their dead, and the picture became the god. Five idols across five generations, each one further from the worship the first generation still practiced. The craft was already working before the eavesdropping began, and the two traps together, the stolen word and the standing image, are the twin roots of everything this history follows.

Source: Tibb al-A'imma via al-Baqir ﵇, in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.26

Claim: sorcerers were granted power over nothing except the envied eye

Speaker: al-Baqir ﵇

The old power that survived is the eye, and it survives because it never needed the eavesdropping. It works through human envy itself, from person to person. The listener who comes in a body, from the same part, stands outside any fence of sight, and the closing of the heavens, which this history dates at the Prophet's ﷺ mission, was the answer to him. This ruling shapes the protections the Imams ﵈ practiced, most of which shelter the sufferer from the eye. None of them duels with a sorcerer. Those protections fill a later part of this series, and the story now runs on to the first great contest between the stolen craft and a prophet's staff.

Where the Craft Settled

The craft settled at Babylon first, where God's answer to it had stood from the ages after Noah ﵇. From Babylon it passed to Egypt, to the court of a king who claimed divinity and kept sorcerers the way kings keep soldiers. Pharaoh had learned the trade himself, and when God sent Moses ﵇ to him, the two crafts met. That meeting is Part 2 of this series.
