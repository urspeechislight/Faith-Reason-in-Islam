Hadith of the Light

Part 5: The Light in Adam and the First Marriage

Bihar al-Anwar, Volume 15, pages 41 to 46. Continued from Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇.

← Faith & Reason in Islam Commentary

Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇ continued:

When Adam opened his eyes in Paradise, he found himself alone. No creature like him walked beside him. No voice answered his. God, who knows what His servant needs before His servant asks, created a companion for him.

From the same clay from which Adam had been shaped, from the very same handful, God created Eve. The narration calls her "from his side," meaning from his source, from the same substance. She was not an afterthought or a lesser copy. She was made from the same primordial clay, kneaded with the same waters, carrying the same light within her.

And God married them in Paradise. The ceremony was simple and the dowry was unlike any dowry before or since. God said to Adam:

"O Adam, the dowry for your marriage is this: that you send blessings upon Muhammad ﷺ and the family of Muhammad three times."

Adam did as he was commanded. He sent blessings upon Muhammad ﷺ three times, and the marriage was sealed. From that day, the blessing upon the Prophet ﷺ and his family became the most precious thing a believer could offer, more precious than gold or silver. It was the dowry of the first marriage in the history of the human race.

Then God Almighty placed five lights upon the right hand of Adam. One light on the thumb: the light of Muhammad ﷺ. One light on the index finger: the light of Ali ﵇. One light on the middle finger: the light of Fatima ﵍. One light on the ring finger: the light of Hasan ﵇. One light on the little finger: the light of Husayn ﵇.

Adam looked at his hand and saw it glowing with five distinct lights, each one a different colour. He asked: "My Lord, what are these?"

"These are the five for whose sake I created the heavens and the earth. Muhammad ﷺ is the most beloved of My creation. Ali ﵇ is his brother and his successor. Fatima ﵍ is the daughter of My beloved and the mother of his descendants. Hasan ﵇ and Husayn ﵇ are the two shoots of Paradise, the leaders of the youth of Paradise. Guard these lights, O Adam, for they are the trust I have placed in you."

And Adam raised his hand to his face and pressed it against his forehead, and the five lights sank into his flesh and entered his being. They were his to carry and his to pass on. Every generation that came from him would bear these lights until they reached their final resting place in the wombs that God had prepared.

Adam and Eve dwelt in Paradise. They ate from its fruits and drank from its rivers and lived beneath its shade. And in time, Eve conceived. She bore twin children: a son named Cain and a daughter named Iqlima. Then she conceived again, and bore another set of twins: a son named Abel and a daughter named Labuda.

But the light was not in Cain. The light passed through Eve and rested in the womb, waiting. When Abel was killed and his line ended, the light turned to the one whom God had chosen from the beginning.

Eve conceived a third time. This child was Seth. And into Seth the light of the Prophet ﷺ passed from Adam. Seth was the first inheritor, the first link in the chain of light that would run through the generations, carried from womb to womb, from father to chosen son, across the whole span of human history until it reached the one for whom it had all been made.

Adam placed his hand on the head of Seth and said: "This is my successor. This is the bearer of the trust. This is the one who carries the light."

Commentary

Eve and the "Bent Rib"

The phrase dil'ihi al-a'waj reads on the surface like the well-known narration about Eve being formed from Adam's rib. The dominant Imami position, however, takes a different view. Several narrations in Bihar al-Anwar itself reject the rib account explicitly. The Imami reading is that Eve was created independently from the same clay. The phrase should be read figuratively: "from his side," meaning from the same source or kind.

The Five Fingers and the Five Lights

Each finger carries the light of one of the five: the Prophet ﷺ, Ali ﵇, Fatima ﵍, Hasan ﵇, and Husayn ﵇. The hand is a single unit made of five distinct lights that belong together. This is the doctrine of the khamsa, the Five of the Cloak, given an origin story in Adam's own body.

Why Cain and Abel Are Not Mentioned

The light narration moves directly from Adam and Eve to Seth because the light was always destined for Seth once Abel was killed. The Cain and Abel story is told fully in Bihar al-Anwar Volume 11. The deeper question is always the same: who has been chosen by God, and what happens when human beings refuse that choice.
