The Book Buried under Solomon's Throne

Solomon ﵇ died leaning on his staff while the jinn worked on for a year. Iblis forged a book of magic in his minister's name and buried it under the throne, and the libel that grew from it called Solomon a sorcerer until the Qur'an cleared him. From al-Qummi, al-Burhan, Majma al-Bayan, and Kanz al-Daqa'iq.

Solomon's ﵇ kingdom is the hinge of this history. The satans fed the soothsayers stolen words, and in his era the trade grew so strong that people said his kingdom itself stood on it. His death, the forged book that followed it, and the verse that cleared him are one connected event in the Shia narrations, and the accusation born there has never stopped traveling.

A King Who Leaned on His Staff

Al-Qummi's narration from al-Baqir ﵇ opens inside the glass house Solomon ﵇ built with the labor of jinn and people.

فإنه حدثني أبي عن ابن أبي عمير عن أبان بن عثمان عن أبي بصير عن أبي جعفر ﵇ قال إن سليمان بن داود امر الجن والإنس فبنوا له بيتا من قوارير قال فبينما هو متكئ على عصاه ينظر إلى الشياطين كيف يعملون وينظرون إليه إذا حانت منه التفاتة فإذا هو برجل معه في القبة ، ففزع منه وقال من أنت ؟ قال انا الذي لا اقبل الرشى ولا اهاب الملوك ، انا ملك الموت ، فقبضه وهو متكئ على عصاه فمكثوا سنة يبنون وينظرون إليه ويدانون له ويعملون حتى بعث الله الأرضة فأكلت منساته وهي العصا فلما خر تبينت الانس ان لو كان الجن يعلمون الغيب ما لبثوا سنة في العذاب المهين فالجن تشكر الأرضة بما عملت بعصا سليمان ، قال فلا تكاد تراها في مكان الا وجد عندها ماء وطين فلما هلك سليمان وضع إبليس السحر وكتبه في كتاب ثم طواه وكتب على ظهره " هذا ما وضع آصف بن برخيا للملك سليمان بن داود من ذخاير كنوز العلم من أراد كذا وكذا فليفعل كذا وكذا " ثم دفنه تحت السرير ثم استثاره لهم فقرأه فقال الكافرون ما كان سليمان ﵇ يغلبنا الا بهذا وقال المؤمنون بل هو عبد الله ونبيه فقال الله جل ذكره " واتبعوا ما تتلوا الشياطين على ملك سليمان وما كفر سليمان ولكن الشياطين كفروا يعلمون الناس السحر وما انزل على الملكين ببابل هاروت وماروت " إلى قوله (فيتعلمون منهما ما يفرقون به بين المرء وزوجه وما هم بضارين به من أحد الا بإذن الله)

"Solomon son of David ordered the jinn and people to build him a house of glass. While he leaned on his staff and watched the devils work, they watched him. He turned and saw a man inside the dome. Solomon feared him and asked, "Who are you?" The man answered, "I accept no bribes and fear no kings. I am the Angel of Death." He took Solomon's soul while Solomon still leaned on his staff. For a year, they kept building. They watched him, drew near to him, and worked. Then Allah sent the termite, which ate his staff. When Solomon fell, the people learned that the jinn did not know the unseen. Otherwise, they would not have remained for a year in humiliating torment. The jinn thank the termite for what it did to Solomon's staff. Abu Jafar said, in every place you see it, you find water and clay beside it. After Solomon died, Iblis prepared magic and wrote it in a book. He folded it and wrote on its cover, "This is what Asif ibn Barkhiya prepared for King Solomon son of David from the stored treasures of knowledge. Whoever wants such and such should do such and such." Then he buried it beneath the throne. He brought it out for them, and they read it. The disbelievers said, "Solomon defeated us only through this." The believers said, "He was Allah's servant and prophet." God, mighty in His mention, said, "And they followed what the satans recited against Solomon's rule, and Solomon did not disbelieve, but the satans disbelieved, teaching the people sorcery and what was sent down upon the two angels at Babylon, Harut and Marut," to His words, "so that they learn from them what divides between a man and his wife, and they harm no one by it except by God's leave."

- al-Baqir , Tafsir al-Qummi, vol.1, p.80-81

Al-Tabarsi records the wider tradition behind the same event, in the account attributed to al-Suddi.

واختلف في السبب الذي لأجله أضافت اليهود السحر إلى سليمان ﵇ ، فقيل : إن سليمان كان قد جمع كتب السحرة ، ووضعها في خزانته . وقيل : كتمها تحت كرسيه لئلا يطلع عليها الناس ، ولا يعلموا بها . فلما مات سليمان استخرجت السحرة تلك الكتب ، وقالوا : إنما تم ملك سليمان بالسحر ، وبه سخر الإنس والجن والطير ، وزينوا السحر في أعين الناس بالنسبة إلى سليمان ﵇ ، وشاع ذلك في اليهود وقبلوه لعداوتهم لسليمان ، عن السدي .

"Scholars differed over why the Jews linked magic to Solomon. Some said that Solomon collected the books of magicians and placed them in his storehouse. Others said that he hid them beneath his throne so that people would not discover or learn about them. After Solomon died, magicians brought out those books and said, "Solomon's kingdom came through magic. Through it, he controlled people, jinn, and birds." They made magic attractive to people by linking it to Solomon. This claim spread among the Jews, and they accepted it because of their hostility toward Solomon."

- al-Tabarsi, Majma al-Bayan, vol.1, p.328

One Accusation, Two Ages

The tafsir attributed to Imam al-Askari ﵈ names the people who used the libel and the reason they needed it.

وذلك أن اليهود الملحدين والنواصب المشاركين لهم في إلحادهم لما سمعوا من رسول الله (﵌) فضائل علي بن أبي طالب (﵇) ، وشاهدوا منه ومن علي (﵉) المعجزات التي أظهرها الله تعالى لهم على أيديهما ، أفضى بعض اليهود والنصاب إلى بعض ، وقالوا : ما محمد إلا طالب الدنيا بحيل ومخاريق وسحر ونيرنجات تعلمها ، وعلم عليا بعضها ، فهو يريد أن يتملك علينا في حياته ، ويعقد الملك لعلي بعده ، وليس ما يقوله عن الله بشيء ، إنما هو قوله ، فيعقد علينا وعلى ضعفاء عباد الله بالسحر والنيرنجات التي يستعملها . وأوفر الناس كان حظا من هذا السحر سليمان بن داود ، الذي ملك بسحره الدنيا كلها من الجن والإنس والشياطين ، ونحن إذا تعلمنا بعض ما كان تعلمه سليمان بن داود ، تمكنا من إظهار مثل ما يظهره محمد وعلي ، وادعينا لأنفسنا بما يجعله محمد لعلي ، وقد استغنينا عن الانقياد لعلي . فحينئذ ذم الله تعالى الجميع من اليهود والنواصب ، فقال الله ﷿ : * (نَبَذَ فَرِيقٌ مِنَ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْكِتابَ كِتابَ اللَّه) * الآمر بولاية محمد وعلي * (وَراءَ ظُهُورِهِمْ) * (2) فلم يعملوا به * (واتَّبَعُوا ما تَتْلُوا) * كفرة * (الشَّياطِينُ) * من السحر والنيرنجات * (عَلى مُلْكِ سُلَيْمانَ) * الذين يزعمون أن سليمان به ملك ، ونحن أيضا به نظهر العجائب حتى ينقاد لنا الناس ، ونستغني عن الانقياد لعلي . قالوا : وكان سليمان كافرا ساحرا ماهرا ، بسحره ملك ما ملك ، وقدر على ما قدر ، فرد الله تعالى عليهم ، وقال : * (وما كَفَرَ سُلَيْمانُ) * ولا استعمل السحر ، كما قال هؤلاء الكافرون * (ولكِنَّ الشَّياطِينَ كَفَرُوا يُعَلِّمُونَ النَّاسَ السِّحْرَ) * أي بتعليمهم الناس السحر الذي نسبوه إلى سليمان كفروا

"The atheist Jews and the Nasibis, people hostile to the Prophet's family, shared the same disbelief. They heard from the Messenger of Allah about the virtues of Ali ibn Abi Talib. They saw miracles that Allah showed through Muhammad and Ali. Some Jews and Nasibis spoke privately to one another. They said, "Muhammad seeks worldly power through tricks, illusions, magic, and secret arts that he learned. He taught some of them to Ali. He wants to rule over us during his life and give the kingdom to Ali after him. His words about Allah have no truth. They are his own words. He controls us and Allah's weak servants through the magic and secret arts he uses." They said, "Solomon son of David received the greatest share of this magic. Through magic, he ruled the whole world of jinn, people, and devils. If we learn some of what Solomon learned, we can show what Muhammad and Ali show. We can claim for ourselves what Muhammad gives to Ali. We will no longer need to obey Ali." At that point God blamed them all, the Jews and the sectarians who shared their unbelief. God said, "A party of those who were given the Book cast the Book of God," the Book that commands the loyalty of Muhammad and Ali, "behind their backs," and they did not act by it, "and they followed what the satans recited," the disbelieving satans, of sorcery and tricks, "against Solomon's rule," those who claim that by it Solomon ruled, and that we too, by it, can show wonders like theirs until the people yield to us, and we are free of needing to follow Ali. They said, "Solomon was an unbelieving and skilled magician. Through his magic, he gained what he gained and became able to do what he did." Allah answered them, "Solomon did not disbelieve." He did not use magic, as those disbelievers claimed. "But the satans disbelieved, teaching the people sorcery," means that by teaching the people the sorcery they attributed to Solomon, they disbelieved."

- Tafsir al-Imam al-Askari , carried in al-Burhan, vol.1, p.353

Al-Mashhadi closes the door that the libel tried to open.

تكذيب لمن زعم ذلك . وعبّر عن السّحر ، بالكفر ، ليدلّ على أنّه كفر . وأنّ من كان نبيّا ، كان معصوما عنه . « ولكِنَّ الشَّياطِينَ كَفَرُوا » باستعماله . وقيل (3) : بما نسبوا إلى سليمان من السّحر . وقيل (4) : عبّر عن السّحر ، بالكفر . وقرأ ابن عامر وحمزة والكسائي (5) : ولكن ( بالتّخفيف ) ، ورفع الشّياطين . « يُعَلِّمُونَ النَّاسَ السِّحْرَ » إغواء وإضلالا . والجملة حال عن الضّمير في « كفروا . »

"This denies the claim of those who said it. It calls magic disbelief to show that magic is disbelief, and that whoever is a prophet is protected from it. "But the satans disbelieved," means by using it. It is said, by what they attributed to Solomon of sorcery. It is said, God called the sorcery disbelief. Ibn Amir, Hamza, and al-Kisai read, "but," with lightening, raising the satans. "Teaching the people sorcery," means leading astray and misleading, and the sentence describes the state of the pronoun in "they disbelieved." It calls magic disbelief to show that magic is disbelief. A prophet is protected from it."

- al-Mashhadi, Tafsir Kanz al-Daqa'iq, vol.2, p.99

Commentary

The Year the Jinn Served a Dead King

Source: Tafsir al-Qummi vol.1 p.80-81

Claim: the jinn served a dead king for a year, which proves they did not know the unseen

Speaker: al-Baqir ﵇

The story begins as a proof against the sorcerers' own suppliers. If the satans could overhear heaven's secrets, they would have known the king in the glass house was dead. Instead they worked on under his empty gaze until a termite ate the staff. The eavesdropping trade of Part 1 is refuted here by its own silence, and the narration keeps a small kindness in the record, that the jinn still thank the termite.

The Signature on the Forgery

Source: Tafsir al-Qummi vol.1 p.81; Majma al-Bayan vol.1 p.328

Claim: Iblis wrote the book of magic and signed it in Asif ibn Barkhiya's name for King Solomon

Speaker: al-Baqir ﵇; al-Tabarsi from al-Suddi

The forgery was built to be found. It carried the name of Solomon's ﵇ own minister, it sat under the throne where treasure would sit, and it promised power to whoever worked its formulas. When the book was dug up, the kingdom's enemies read the signature and said the throne had rested on sorcery all along. The believers answered with a sentence of creed, that Solomon ﵇ was God's servant and prophet, and the Qur'an set that answer into revelation. Every later forgery of power in this history follows this one's pattern, a true name, a buried object, and a promise.

The Word That Came Back

Source: al-Burhan vol.1 p.353; Kanz al-Daqa'iq vol.2 p.99

Claim: the same accusers called Muhammad ﷺ and Ali ﵇ workers of the same craft, and prophethood is protected from sorcery

Speaker: Tafsir al-Imam al-Askari ﵈; al-Mashhadi

The Askari tafsir strips the libel of its disguise. The people who called Solomon ﵇ a sorcerer said the same of Muhammad ﷺ, that his miracles were learned tricks, and that he taught some of them to Ali ﵇ in order to hand him a kingdom. The motive named is the refusal to follow Ali ﵇, and the magic-claim is the instrument. Al-Mashhadi's line answers every age of the claim at once, that whoever is a prophet is protected from sorcery. The word will come back again at the moon-splitting in Part 5, and the school's verdict on it is recorded there.

Where the Story Runs Next

The Qur'an cleared Solomon ﵇ in the same verse that named Babylon and two angels, and the story of those two angels stands earlier in this history, in the part before this one. The verse that ends the libel opens the road forward. The word sorcerer now travels to the Prophet ﷺ himself, and that is the next part.
