Where the council agrees. The page's strongest moves survived every round: the admission-against-interest reading of Ibn Sīrīn's report, the bundle-vs-single-strand answer to back-projection, the Qays obituary calendar-marker, and the qualifier-graded Facts table. Round 3 (full-file reads) found zero flipped negations remaining and confirmed every earlier fix landed.

Where the council clashed. Round 2's low comprehensiveness and escape scores traced mainly to a retrieval artifact: four of five advisors read a truncated URL fetch and reported missing tabs. The one full reader scored 1.5-2 points higher across dimensions. Round 3, reading the local file, vindicated that diagnosis.

Blind spots the council caught. Three real defects survived the artifact: (1) the page applied bottleneck criticism to the Ibn Sīrīn report but never to the al-Nakhaʿī report that carries the floor; (2) the verdict overstated the Shia evidence, which dates a schism, not isnād-scrutiny; (3) a factual slip gave Basra one pivot where the quote names two. All three were fixed in the final commit, plus a polarity flip in Tab 4 ("agreed on theology") and the Schacht 720 CE boundary phrasing.

Recommendation. Ship in the current state. The remaining sub-9 ceiling is structural: the council wants documentary evidence (papyri, Ṣaḥīfat Ḥammām, Schoeler on orality), which the site's corpus-grounded standard deliberately excludes as unverifiable. Adding it would trade the page's discipline for reach.

One thing to do first, if continuing: a fourth round would need new corpus-grounded material to move DTE above 7: the strongest candidate is a full isnād-grading of the two floor chains by classical rijāl criteria (Jābir b. Nūḥ, al-Aʿmash, ʿĀṣim al-Aḥwal, Ismāʿīl b. Zakariyyāʾ), pulled from the corpus and added to Tab 5.
