They Called Him a Sorcerer

The word sorcerer followed every prophet in this history, from Pharaoh's court to the moon over Mecca to a well in Medina. At the well the school weighs the spell story and records its own verdicts upon it. From al-Rawandi's al-Khara'ij, Madinat al-Ma'ajiz, al-Jaza'iri's al-Nur al-Mubin, Bihar al-Anwar, and Majma al-Bayan.

The word that cleared Solomon ﵇ never retired. Pharaoh said it of Moses ﵇, and the enemies of Solomon ﵇ said it of him. This part follows the same word forward through the corpus, to Jesus, to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ at a split moon, and to the visions that affirmed Ali ﵇ in the mosque of Quba. Then the word becomes an object. A spell in a well at Medina is the corpus's most famous sorcery story, and the school's handling of it is the pattern this whole series has followed.

The Birth and the Barred Heavens

The era of the Prophet ﷺ opened with heaven itself closing to the listeners of Part 1. Ali ﵇ saw the night it happened.

في كتاب الاحتجاج للطبرسي متصل بآخر ما نقلنا عنه سابقا أعني يستمعون القرآن فأقبل إليه الجن والنبي ﵌ ببطن النخل فاعتذروا بأنهم " ظنوا كما ظننتم ان لن يبعث الله أحدا " ولقد اقبل إليه أحد وسبعون ألفا منهم ، فبايعوه على الصوم والصلاة والزكاة والحج والجهاد ونصح المسلمين ، فاعتذروا بأنهم " قالوا على الله شططا " . 23 - في تفسير علي بن إبراهيم باسناده إلى زرارة قال : سألت أبا جعفر ﵇ عن قول الله وانه كان رجال من الانس يعوذون برجال من الجن فزادوهم رهقا قال : كان الرجل ينطلق إلى الكاهن الذي يوحى إليه الشيطان فيقول : قل للشيطان فلان قد عاذبك . أقول : قد سبق قريبا عن كتاب الاحتجاج قول أمير المؤمنين ﵇ فأقبل إليه الجن والنبي ﵌ ببطن النخل فاعتذر بأنهم ظنوا كما ظننتم ان لن يبعث الله أحدا . قال عز من قائل : وانا لمسنا السماء فوجدناها ملئت حرسا شديدا وشهبا 24 - في كتاب الاحتجاج للطبرسي ﵀ حديث طويل عن أمير المؤمنين ﵇ يذكر فيه مناقب الرسول ﵌ وفيه : ولقد رأيت الملائكة ليلة ولد تصعد وتنزل وتسبح وتقدس وتضطرب النجوم وتتساقط علامة لميلاده ، ولقد هم إبليس بالظعن في السماء لما رأى من الأعاجيب في تلك الليلة ، وكان له مقعد في السماء الثالثة والشياطين يسترقون السمع ، فلما رأوا العجائب أرادوا أن يسترقوا السمع فإذا هم قد حجبوا عن السماوات كلها ; ورموا بالشهب جلالة لنبوة محمد ﵌ (1)

"In the book al-Ihtijaj of al-Tabarsi there is a long report from the Commander of the Faithful on the merits of the Messenger of Allah. In it, on the night he was born, I saw the angels rising and descending, praising Allah and declaring Him holy. The stars shook and fell as a sign of his birth. Iblis prepared to leave heaven when he saw the wonders of that night. He had a place in the third heaven, and the devils used to listen secretly. The same proof was set before the Jews of Medina in a long narration of the Prophet's ﷺ signs, and they could read its premise in their own books.

في قرب الإسناد للحميري باسناده إلى موسى بن جعفر ﵇ حديث طويل يذكر فيه آيات الرسول ﵌ يقول فيه مخاطبا لنفر من اليهود : اما أول ذلك فإنكم أنتم تقرؤن ان الجن كانوا يسترقون السمع قبل مبعثه فمنعت في أوان رسالته بالرجوم وانقضاض النجوم وبطلان الكهنة والسحرة .

"In Qurb al-Isnad of al-Humayri, by his chain to Musa ibn Jafar, there is a long report naming the signs of the Messenger of Allah. In it, speaking to a group of Jews, Musa ibn Jafar said, first, you yourselves read that the jinn listened secretly before he was sent. When his message began, meteors and falling stars stopped them. Soothsayers and magicians lost their power.""

- Musa ibn Ja'far , Qurb al-Isnad, carried in Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.3, p.4

The Word at Large

The word had already hunted prophets of an older age, and the corpus keeps its older victims in view before the Prophet's ﷺ own story. Al-Jaza'iri records what the Jews said of Jesus when he passed by them.

فقال بعضهم قد جاءكم الساحر ابن الساحرة والفاعل بن الفاعلة فقذفوه بأمه فسمع ذلك عيسى ( ع ) فقال اللهم أنت ربي خلقتني ولم أتهم من تلقاء نفسي اللهم العن من سبني وسب والدتي

"Some of them said, "The sorcerer, son of a sorceress, has come to you. The fornicator, son of a fornicator." They slandered him through his mother. Jesus heard this and said, "Allah, You are my Lord. You created me, and I have brought no charge against myself. Allah, curse whoever insults me and insults my mother.""

- al-Jaza'iri, al-Nur al-Mubin, p.420

Al-Rawandi's al-Khara'ij carries the word as it met the Prophet ﷺ in Mecca, first over the news his caravan brought back.

"Among them is what is related from Ali. Three years after his mission began, he was carried by night to Jerusalem, and raised from there to heaven on the night of the ascent. When morning came, he told Quraysh of his ascent, and their ignorant ones said, what a lying tale this is. One of them said, O Abu al-Qasim, how shall we know that you are truthful? Then over the sign the Quraysh themselves had demanded, in the sky they said sorcery cannot reach.

"Among them is that the Prophet was sitting at night in the Hijr while Quraysh sat talking in their gatherings. Some said to one another, "Muhammad's case has exhausted us. They went to him and said, "Muhammad, if what we see from you is free from sorcery, show us a sign in heaven. We know that sorcery does not last in heaven as it lasts on earth." The Spell in the Well

The corpus's most famous sorcery story begins with Jibril's warning, carried in the medicine book of the Imams from Ali ﵇.

16 طب الأئمة : عن محمد بن جعفر البرسي ، عن أحمد بن يحيى الأرمني عن محمد بن سنان ، عن المفضل بن عمر ، عن أبي عبد الله ﵇ قال : قال أمير المؤمنين ﵇ : إن جبرئيل أتى النبي ﵌ وقال : يا محمد ، قال لبيك يا جبرئيل قال : إن فلانا اليهودي سحرك ، وجعل السحر في بئر بني فلان ، فابعث إليه - يعني إلى البئر - أوثق الناس عندك ، وأعظمهم في عينك ، وهو عديل نفسك ، حتى يأتيك بالسحر . وقال : فبعث النبي ﵌ علي بن أبي طالب ﵇ وقال : انطلق إلى بئر " ذروان " فإن فيها سحرا سحرني به لبيد بن أعصم اليهودي ، فائتني به . قال علي عليه السلام : فانطلقت في حاجة رسول الله ﵌ فهبطت فإذا ماء البئر قد صار كأنه ماء الحناء من السحر ، فطلبته مستعجلا حتى انتهيت إلى أسفل القليب (4) ولم أظفر به . قال الذين معي : ما فيه شئ فاصعد ، فقلت : لا والله ، ما كذبت (1) ولا كذبت ، وما يقيني به مثل يقينكم يعني رسول الله ﵌ ثم طلبت طلبا بلطف ، فاستخرجت حقا ، فأتيت النبي ﵌ فقال : افتحه ، ففتحته فإذا في الحق قطعة كرب النخل ، في وتر عليها إحدى وعشرون عقدة . وكان جبرئيل ﵇ أنزل يومئذ المعوذتين على النبي ﵌ فقال النبي ﵌ : يا علي اقرأهما على الوتر ، فجعل أمير المؤمنين كلما قرأ آية انحلت عقدة حتى فرغ منها وكشف الله ﷿ عن نبيه ما سحر به وعافاه . ويروى أن جبرئيل وميكائيل ﵉ أتيا إلى النبي ﵌ فجلس أحدهما عن يمينه والآخر عن شماله ، فقال جبرئيل لميكائيل : ما وجع الرجل ؟ فقال ميكائيل هو مطبوب ، فقال جبرئيل ﵇ : ومن طبه ؟ قال : لبيد بن أعصم اليهودي ، ثم ذكر الحديث إلى آخره (2)

"Tibb al-A'imma relates, from Muhammad ibn Jafar al-Barsi, from Ahmad ibn Yahya al-Armani, from Muhammad ibn Sinan, from al-Mufaddal ibn Amr, from Abu Abd Allah, who said, the Commander of the Faithful said, Gabriel came to the Prophet and said, "Muhammad." He replied, "I am here, Gabriel." Gabriel said, "A certain Jewish man has cast a spell on you. He placed it in the well of a certain clan. Send to it, meaning the well, the person you trust most, who holds the highest place in your eyes and is equal to yourself. Let him bring you the spell." The Prophet sent Ali ibn Abi Talib and said, "Go to the well of Dharwan. Labid ibn al-A'sam the Jew placed a spell there against me. Bring it to me." Ali said, "I went to carry out the Messenger of Allah's order. I went down and found that the well's water had become like henna water because of the spell. I searched quickly until I reached the bottom of the well, yet I did not find it. Those with me said, 'There is nothing there. Come back up.' I said, 'By Allah, I have spoken no lie and have been told no lie, and my certainty about it is unlike yours,' meaning about the Messenger of Allah. Then I searched with care and took out a small box. I brought it to the Prophet, and the Prophet said, "Open it." I opened it and found a piece of palm fiber in the box, and in its hollow a palm-frond rope with twenty-one knots on it. That day Gabriel had brought down the two protective suras upon the Prophet. The Prophet said, "Ali, recite them over the rope." Each time the Commander of the Faithful recited a verse, one knot came undone. When he finished, Allah removed the spell from His Prophet and restored his health. It is reported that Gabriel and Michael came to the Prophet. One sat on his right and the other on his left. Gabriel asked Michael, "What illness has this man?" Michael answered, "He has been bewitched." Gabriel asked, "Who cast the spell on him?" Michael answered, "Labid ibn al-Asam, the Jew." Then the hadith continues to its end."

- Ali , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.25-26

The Word against the House

After the Prophet's ﷺ death the word was aimed at his own House. Madinat al-Ma'ajiz carries the whole scene at Quba, from Umar's rebuke of Abu Bakr to the order at the mosque.

فقال له عمر : ويلك ما أقل عقلك ، فوالله ما أنت فيه الساعة إلا من [بعض] (4) سحر ابن أبي كبشة قد نسيت [سحر] (5) بني هاشم [ومن أين يرجع محمد ولا يرجع من مات ، إنما أنت فيه أعظم من سحر بني هاشم ف‍] (6) تقلد هذا السربال ومر (7) فيه . ورواه الراوندي : عن معاوية بن عمار الدهني ببعض التغيير اليسير . ثم قال بعد ذلك : وروى الثقاة ، عن أبي عبد الله - ﵇ - مثل ذلك إلى أن جاء مذعورا إلى صاحبه فأخبره بالخبر ، فتضاحك منه ، وقال : أنسيت بني هاشم ؟ (8) 686 - ومن الكتاب المذكور أيضا : محمد بن الحسين بن أبي الخطاب ، عن الحكم بن مسكين (1) ، عن أبي سعيد المكاري (2) ، عن أبي عبد الله - ﵇ - قال : إن أمير المؤمنين - ﵇ - لقى أبا بكر ، فقال له : أما أمرك رسول الله - ﵌ - أن تطيع لي ؟ فقال : لا ، ولو أمرني لفعلت . فقال : سبحان الله ، أما أمرك رسول الله - ﵌ - أن تطيع لي ؟ فقال : لا ، ولو أمرني لفعلت . قال : فامض بنا إلى رسول الله - ﵌ - ، فانطلق به إلى مسجد قبا فإذا رسول الله - ﵌ - يصلي . فلما انصرف قال له علي : يا رسول الله ، إني قلت لأبي بكر : أما أمرك رسول الله أن تطيعني ؟ فقال : لا . فقال رسول الله - ﵌ - : قد أمرتك فأطعه . قال : فخرج ولقى عمر وهو ذعر ، فقام عمر وقال له : ما بالك (3) ؟ فقال له : قال رسول الله كذا وكذا . فقال [له] (4) عمر : تبا لامة ولوك أمرهم ، أما تعرف سحر بني هاشم ؟ (5

"Umar said to him, "Woe to you. How little sense you have. By Allah, what you are in now comes from the sorcery of Ibn Abi Kabsha. Have you forgotten the sorcery of Banu Hashim? Where can Muhammad return from when the dead do not return? What you are in is greater than the sorcery of Banu Hashim. Put on this garment and go on in it." Al-Bahrani says, al-Rawandi related it from Muawiya ibn Ammar al-Dahhan with slight changes. Then he said after that, the trustworthy ones related from Abu Abd Allah something like it, until he came terrified to his companion and told him the news, and his companion laughed at him and said, have you forgotten the family of Hashim? From the same book also, Muhammad ibn al-Husayn ibn Abi al-Khattab, from al-Hakam ibn Maskin, from Abu Said al-Makari, from Abu Abd Allah, who said, the Commander of the Faithful met Abu Bakr and said to him, did the Messenger of Allah not order you to obey me? Abu Bakr said, no, and had he ordered me I would have done it. Ali said, glory be to God, did the Messenger of Allah not order you to obey me? Abu Bakr said, no, and had he ordered me I would have done it. Ali said, then come with me to the Messenger of Allah. He took him to the mosque of Quba, and there was the Messenger of Allah praying. When he finished, Ali said to him, O Messenger of Allah, I said to Abu Bakr, did the Messenger of Allah order you to obey me? and he said no. The Messenger of Allah said, I did order you, so obey him. He went out and met Umar while he was frightened. Umar stood and said to him, what is the matter with you? He said to him, the Messenger of Allah said such and such. Umar said to him, ruin to a community that chews over their affair. Do you not know the sorcery of the family of Hashim?"

- Madinat al-Ma'ajiz, vol.3, p.8-9

And Ibn Shahrashub's recension carries it to the grave of the Prophet ﷺ himself.

هذا سحر من سحر بني هاشم ، دم على ما أنت عليه ، واخطط مكانك ، ولم يزل به حتى صده عن المراد

"This comes from the sorcery of Banu Hashim. Stay as you are and mark your place. He kept at him until he turned him away from what he wanted."

- Madinat al-Ma'ajiz, vol.3, p.11, from Ibn Shahrashub

The Story Weighed

The corpus does not carry the story at one strength. The Da'a'im al-Islam recension holds its most extreme form.

دعائم الاسلام : عن جعفر بن محمد ، عن أبيه ، عن آبائه ، عن علي ﵇ مثله - إلى قوله - وجعلاه في مراقي البئر بالمدينة ، فأقام رسول الله ﵌ لا يسمع ولا يبصر ولا يفهم ولا يتكلم ولا يأكل ولا يشرب ، فنزل عليه جبرئيل ﵇ بمعوذات - وساق نحوه إلى قوله - فقطعت يده فكوي منها فمات .

"Da'a'im al-Islam relates, from Jafar ibn Muhammad, from his father, from his fathers, from Ali, the like of it, to the point where it says, they placed it in the upper part of a well in Medina. The Messenger of Allah remained unable to hear, see, understand, speak, eat, or drink. Gabriel came down to him with two protective suras, and the account runs to the point where it says that his hand was cut off, cauterized from it, and he died."

- Da'a'im al-Islam, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.25

Abu Basir asked al-Sadiq ﵇ how far the spell was said to reach, and the answer is recorded beside the story.

فقال أبو بصير لأبي عبد الله ﵇ : وما كاد أو عسى أن يبلغ من سحره ؟ قال أبو عبد الله الصادق ﵇ : بلى كان النبي ﵌ يرى أنه يجامع وليس يجامع وكان يريد الباب ولا يبصره ، حتى يلمسه بيده ، والسحر حق وما يسلط السحر إلا على العين والفرج ،

"Abu Basir said to Abu Abd Allah, and what did it come to, or could it come to, of his sorcery? Abu Abd Allah al-Sadiq answered, "It reached the point where the Prophet thought he was having intercourse when he was not, and he would go toward a door and fail to see it until he touched it with his hand. Sorcery is real, and sorcery was only given power over the eye and the private part."

- al-Sadiq , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.89, p.368

Al-Majlisi's own note on the page sets the school's position against the story's strongest form.

أقول : قد مر الكلام في تأثير السحر في الأنبياء والأئمة ﵈ وأن المشهور عدمه

"I have already discussed whether sorcery affects prophets and Imams. The well-known view denies that it does."

- al-Majlisi, Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.25

Al-Tabarsi went further, and his verdict names the reports themselves.

فأما ما روي من الأخبار أن النبي ﵌ سحر ، فكان يرى أنه فعل ما لم يفعله ، وأنه لم يفعل ما فعله ، فأخبار مفتعلة لا يلتفت إليها . وقد قال الله ، ﷾ ، حكاية عن الكفار : (إن تتبعون إلا رجلا مسحورا) فلو كان للسحر عمل فيه ، لكان الكفار صادقين في مقالهم ، حاشا النبي ﵌ ، من كل صفة نقص ، تنفر عن قبول قوله ، فإنه حجة الله على خليقته ، وصفوته على بريته

"Reports claim that the Prophet was bewitched, so he thought he had done things he had not done and had not done things he had done. These reports were invented and deserve no attention. Allah reports the words of the disbelievers, "You follow only a bewitched man." If magic had affected him, the disbelievers would have spoken truthfully. May the Prophet be kept free from every defect that would turn people away from accepting his words. He is Allah’s proof against His creation and His chosen servant among them."

- al-Tabarsi, Majma al-Bayan, vol.1, p.334

His pragmatic disproof of the whole trade closes the weighing.

ولو أن الساحر والمعزم قدرا على نفع أو ضر ، وعلما الغيب ، لقدرا على إزالة . الممالك ، واستخراج الكنوز من معادنها ، والغلبة على البلدان بقتل الملوك ، من غير أن ينالهم مكروه وضرر . فلما رأيناهم أسوأ الناس حالا ، وأكثرهم مكيدة واحتيالا ، علمنا أنهم لا يقدرون على شئ من ذلك

"If sorcerers and spell-casters could bring benefit or harm, and knew the unseen, they could overthrow kingdoms, draw treasures from their mines, and conquer lands by killing kings without suffering harm. We see that they live in the worst conditions and rely most on tricks and schemes. We therefore know that they cannot do any of this."

- al-Tabarsi, Majma al-Bayan, vol.1, p.334

Commentary

The Heavens Shut at the Mission

Source: Qurb al-Isnad via al-Rida ﵇, in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.75; Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.3, p.4

Claim: at the Prophet's ﷺ mission the eavesdropping was barred and soothsaying and sorcery lost their power

Speaker: al-Rida ﵇; Musa ibn Ja'far ﵇

The cutoff is a dated event in this history, not a gradual fading. The stonings began with the mission, and the soothsayers went silent because their supply line was cut. The narration to the Jews of Medina makes the point sharper, since it rests on what the Jews themselves accepted in scripture. A craft built on stolen speech dies when the theft becomes impossible.

The Word That Traveled

Source: al-Nur al-Mubin p.420; al-Khara'ij vol.1 p.145-147

Claim: the accusation of sorcery was made against Jesus, and against the Prophet ﷺ over his news, the split moon, and the eaten parchment

Speaker: al-Jaza'iri; al-Rawandi

The mi'raj caravan report, the split moon, and the termite-eaten parchment are three different kinds of sign, a fulfilled prediction, a public miracle in the demanded sky, and a silent verdict on a covenant. All three received the same answer, the word sorcery, and the Qur'an itself preserved that answer against the moon, that they said persistent sorcery. The word never improved its argument across the centuries. It is the whole argument, and Part 2 recorded where it was first refuted, by craftsmen who knew the craft from inside.

Sorcery and Government

Source: Madinat al-Ma'ajiz vol.3 p.8-11

Claim: the visions commanding the yield of the Prophet's ﷺ right were answered with the sorcery of Banu Hashim

Speaker: the Kafi-family chains; Basa'ir al-Darajat; Ibn Shahrashub

The recensions agree on the shape. A vision of the Prophet ﷺ commands the first caliph to yield what he had taken, panic follows, and a counselor dismisses the vision as the House's sorcery. The word that failed against the moon works here as government, a reason not to obey what was seen. The corpus keeps these reports among the miracles of the Prophet ﷺ, and the sorcery-claim against the House stands recorded inside the miracle itself.

The Spell Story in the Corpus

Source: Tibb al-A'imma in Bihar al-Anwar vol.60 p.25-26 and vol.92 p.127-128

Claim: a Jew named Labid ibn al-A'sam bewitched the Prophet ﷺ, the spell sat knotted in a well, and the two shelter suras undid it verse by verse

Speaker: Ali ﵇ via al-Mufaddal and the Tibb chains

The birth vision closed the era this series opened with. The heavens were shut before the ministry began, and the spell story arrives inside that shut world, which is exactly the collision the verdicts address, that the sorcery which lost its power at the mission cannot be the same thing as this spell, and the well-known position and the forged-verdict are the corpus holding that line. The story's details are vivid and mutually close. The well water turned the color of henna, Ali ﵇ searched to the bottom against his companions' doubts, and the container held a palm-frond cord with twenty-one knots. The two suras came down that day, and a knot untied at each verse until the spell was gone. The sorcerer is named, Labid ibn al-A'sam, though the name belongs to the report tradition, and the well name Dharwan marks this recension, while a parallel one kept the name Azwan. Even the extent of the spell is preserved in the record, in Abu Basir's question and its uncomfortable answer.

The School's Answer

Source: Bihar al-Anwar vol.60 p.25 and vol.92 p.128; Majma al-Bayan vol.1 p.334

Claim: the well-known position denies that sorcery reaches prophets and Imams, and al-Tabarsi calls the bewitched-Prophet reports forged

Speaker: al-Majlisi; al-Tabarsi; the school

The school's mind meets the story on three fronts. Al-Majlisi notes on the page itself that the known position among the scholars is that sorcery has no hold on prophets and Imams. Al-Tabarsi rules that the reports making the Prophet ﷺ imagine deeds he did not do are invented, because otherwise the word of the Quraysh, that he was a bewitched man, would stand true, and the Prophet ﷺ is God's proof, kept from every defect. His pragmatic disproof then turns the trade's own claim against itself, that sorcerers holding the unseen and the power to seize kingdoms would not live as the worst-off schemers on earth. The disproof aims at the trade's grand claim, not at the bounded eye, which the corpus affirms and the shelter answers. The story remains in the corpus, read and taught, beside the verdicts upon it. That is this series' whole method, stated once more at the place it matters most.

What the Accusation Left

The word sorcerer, spent against every prophet, left two things behind. It left the community a warning about what sorcery does to those who practice it and to those they work it on. The Imams meet that craft in combat next, and the costs follow them. And it left the believer a shield, the two suras that came down at the well and the protections taught around them, which is the part after.
