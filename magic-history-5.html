They Called Him a Sorcerer

The word sorcerer followed every prophet in this history, from Pharaoh's court to the moon over Mecca to a well in Medina, and at the well the school weighs the spell story and records its own verdicts upon it. From al-Rawandi's al-Khara'ij, Madinat al-Ma'ajiz, al-Jaza'iri's al-Nur al-Mubin, Bihar al-Anwar, and Majma al-Bayan.

The word that cleared Solomon ﵇ never retired. Pharaoh said it of Moses ﵇, and the enemies of Solomon ﵇ said it of him, and this part follows the same word forward through the corpus, to Jesus, to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ at a split moon, and to the visions that affirmed Ali ﵇ in the mosque of Quba. Then the word becomes an object. A spell in a well at Medina is the corpus's most famous sorcery story, and the school's handling of it is the pattern this whole series has followed.

The Birth and the Barred Heavens

The era of the Prophet ﷺ opened with heaven itself closing to the listeners of Part 1. Ali ﵇ saw the night it happened.

ولقد رأيت الملائكة ليلة ولد تصعد وتنزل وتسبح وتقدس وتضطرب النجوم وتتساقط علامة لميلاده ، ولقد هم إبليس بالظعن في السماء لما رأى من الأعاجيب في تلك الليلة ، وكان له مقعد في السماء الثالثة والشياطين يسترقون السمع ، فلما رأوا العجائب أرادوا أن يسترقوا السمع فإذا هم قد حجبوا عن السماوات كلها ; ورموا بالشهب جلالة لنبوة

"On the night he was born, I saw the angels rising and descending, praising Allah and declaring Him holy. The stars shook and fell as a sign of his birth. Iblis prepared to leave heaven when he saw the wonders of that night. He had a place in the third heaven, and the devils used to listen secretly. When they saw these wonders, they tried to listen secretly, but they found every heaven barred to them. Shooting stars struck them out of reverence for his prophethood."

- Ali , al-Ihtijaj, carried in Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.5, p.436

The closing held from that night to the mission, and Qurb al-Isnad carries the report of the end of the listening.

عن الحسن بن ظريف عن معمر عن الرضا عن أبيه ﵈ قال : إن الجن كانوا يسترقون السمع قبل مبعث النبي ﵌ فمنعت في أوان رسالته بالرجوم ، وانقضاض النجوم ، وبطلان الكهنة والسحر

"al-Rida reported from his father: "Before the Prophet was sent, the jinn listened secretly. When his message began, meteors and falling stars stopped them. Soothsaying and magic lost their power.""

- al-Rida , Qurb al-Isnad, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.75

The same proof was set before the Jews of Medina in a long narration of the Prophet's ﷺ signs, and they could read its premise in their own books.

مخاطبا لنفر من اليهود : اما أول ذلك فإنكم أنتم تقرؤن ان الجن كانوا يسترقون السمع قبل مبعثه فمنعت في أوان رسالته بالرجوم وانقضاض النجوم وبطلان الكهنة والسحرة

"Speaking to a group of Jews, Musa ibn Ja'far said, "First, you yourselves read that the jinn listened secretly before he was sent. When his message began, meteors and falling stars stopped them. Soothsayers and magicians lost their power.""

- Musa ibn Ja'far , Qurb al-Isnad, carried in Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.3, p.4

The Word at Large

The word had already hunted prophets of an older age, and the corpus keeps its older victims in view before the Prophet's ﷺ own story. Al-Jaza'iri records what the Jews said of Jesus when he passed by them.

فقال بعضهم قد جاءكم الساحر ابن الساحرة والفاعل بن الفاعلة فقذفوه بأمه فسمع ذلك عيسى ( ع ) فقال اللهم أنت ربي خلقتني ولم أتهم من تلقاء نفسي اللهم العن من سبني وسب والدتي

"Some of them said, "The sorcerer, son of a sorceress, has come to you. The fornicator, son of a fornicator." They slandered him through his mother. Jesus heard this and said, "Allah, You are my Lord. You created me, and I have brought no charge against myself. Allah, curse whoever insults me and insults my mother.""

- al-Jaza'iri, al-Nur al-Mubin, p.420

Al-Rawandi's al-Khara'ij carries the word as it met the Prophet ﷺ in Mecca, first over the news his caravan brought back.

فلما كان اليوم الثالث خرجوا إلى باب مكة لينظروا صدق ما أخبر به محمد ﵌ قبل طلوع الشمس ، فهم كذلك إذا طلعت العير عليهم بطلوع الشمس ، في أولها الجمل الأحمر فتعجبوا من ذلك ، وسألوا الذين كانوا مع العير فقالوا مثل ما قال محمد ﵌ في إخباره عنهم . فقالوا : هذا أيضا من سحر محمد

"On the third day, they went to the gate of Mecca before sunrise. They wanted to see whether what Muhammad had told them was true. While they waited, the caravan appeared with the sunrise. A red camel led it. They were amazed. They asked those with the caravan, and they gave the same account Muhammad had given. They said, "This too is Muhammad's sorcery.""

- al-Rawandi, al-Khara'ij wa-l-Jara'ih, vol.1, p.145, no.228

Then over the sign the Quraysh themselves had demanded, in the sky they said sorcery cannot reach.

فقال بعضهم لبعض : قد أعيانا أمر محمد ﵌ ، فما ندري ما نقول فيه . فقال بعضهم : قوموا بنا جميعا إليه نسأله أن يرينا آية من السماء ، فإن السحر قد يكون في الأرض ولا يكون في السماء ، فصاروا إليه . فقالوا : يا محمد إن لم يكن هذا الذي نرى منك سحرا ، فأرنا آية في السماء فإنا نعلم أن السحر لا يستمر في السماء كما يستمر في الأرض . فقال لهم : ألستم ترون هذا القمر في تمامه لأربع عشرة ؟ فقالوا : بلى . قال : أفتحبون أن تكون الآية من قبله وجهته ؟ قالوا : قد أحببنا ذلك . فأشار إليه بإصبعه فانشق بنصفين ، فوقع نصفه على ظهر الكعبة ، ونصفه الآخر على جبل أبي قبيس ، وهم ينظرون إليه . فقال بعضهم : فرده إلى مكانه . فأومى بيده إلى النصف الذي كان على ظهر الكعبة وبيده الأخرى إلى النصف الذي كان على جبل أبي قبيس ، فطارا جميعا فالتقيا في الهواء فصارا واحدا ، واستقر القمر في مكانه ، على ما كان . فقالوا : قوموا فقد استمر سحر محمد في السماء والأرض . فأنزل الله تعالى : " اقتربت الساعة وانشق القمر * وإن يروا آية يعرضوا ويقولوا سحر مستمر

"Some said to one another, "Muhammad's case has exhausted us. We do not know what to say about him." Some said, "Let us all go to him and ask him to show us a sign from heaven. Sorcery may happen on earth, yet it does not happen in heaven." They went to him and said, "Muhammad, if what we see from you is free from sorcery, show us a sign in heaven. We know that sorcery does not last in heaven as it lasts on earth." He asked them, "Do you see this moon when it is full on its fourteenth night?" They answered, "Yes." He said, "Would you like its face and direction to be the sign?" They answered, "We would." He pointed at it with his finger, and it split into two halves. One half fell upon the roof of the Kaaba. The other fell upon Mount Abu Qubays as they watched. Some said, "Return it to its place." He gestured with one hand toward the half on the Kaaba and with the other toward the half on Mount Abu Qubays. Both halves flew together and met in the air. They became one, and the moon settled where it had been. They said, "Leave, for Muhammad's sorcery has continued in heaven and on earth." Allah Most High sent down: "The Hour has drawn near, and the moon has split. When they see a sign, they turn away and say, 'Continuing sorcery.'""

- al-Rawandi, al-Khara'ij wa-l-Jara'ih, vol.1, p.145-146, no.229

And over the parchment of the boycott, when a creature of God ate their covenant and left the name of God.

قال : أخبرني أن الله قد بعث على صحيفتكم دابة فلحست ما فيها غير اسم الله فحطوها فإن كان الامر بخلاف ما قال سلمته إليكم . ففتحوها فلم يجدوا فيها شيئا غير اسم الله . فتفرقوا وهم يقولون : سحر سحر

"He said, "Allah has sent a creature against your document. It has licked away everything in it except Allah's name. Bring it down. If the matter differs from what he said, I will hand him over to you." They opened it and found nothing in it except Allah's name. They left, saying, "Sorcery, sorcery.""

- al-Rawandi, al-Khara'ij wa-l-Jara'ih, vol.1, p.147, no.230

The Spell in the Well

The corpus's most famous sorcery story begins with Jibril's warning, carried in the medicine book of the Imams from Ali ﵇.

إن جبرئيل ﵇ أتى النبي ﵌ وقال له : يا محمد ، قال : لبيك يا جبرئيل ، قال : إن فلا نا اليهودي سحرك وجعل السحر في بئر بني فلان ، فابعث إليه يعني إلى البئر أو ثق الناس عندك ، وأعظمهم في عينك ، وهو عديل نفسك ، حتى يأتيك بالسحر

"Gabriel came to the Prophet and said, "Muhammad." He replied, "I am here, Gabriel." Gabriel said, "A certain Jewish man has cast a spell on you. He placed it in the well of a certain clan. Send to it, meaning the well, the person you trust most, who holds the highest place in your eyes and is equal to yourself. Let him bring you the spell.""

- Ali , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.92, p.127

The Prophet ﷺ sent Ali ﵇ to the well, and Bihar carries two recensions of what he found. The first names the well Dharwan.

قال : فبعث النبي ﵌ علي بن أبي طالب ﵇ وقال : انطلق إلى بئر " ذروان " فإن فيها سحرا سحرني به لبيد بن أعصم اليهودي ، فائتني به . قال علي عليه السلام : فانطلقت في حاجة رسول الله ﵌ فهبطت فإذا ماء البئر قد صار كأنه ماء الحناء من السحر ، فطلبته مستعجلا حتى انتهيت إلى أسفل القليب (4) ولم أظفر به . قال الذين معي : ما فيه شئ فاصعد ، فقلت : لا والله ، ما كذبت (1) ولا كذبت ، وما يقيني به مثل يقينكم

"The Prophet sent Ali ibn Abi Talib and said, "Go to the well of Dharwan. Labid ibn al-A'sam the Jew placed a spell there against me. Bring it to me." Ali said, "I went to carry out the Messenger of Allah's order. I went down and found that the well's water had become like henna water because of the spell. I searched quickly until I reached the bottom of the well, yet I did not find it. Those with me said, 'There is nothing there. Come back up.' I said, 'By Allah, I have spoken the truth and have been told the truth. My certainty about it is unlike yours.'""

- Ali , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.25-26

The second names it Azwan.

قال : فبعث النبي ﵌ علي بن أبي طالب ﵇ وقال : انطلق إلى بئر أزوان (2) فان فيها سحرا سحرني به لبيد بن أعصم اليهودي فأتني به . قال علي ﵇ : فانطلقت في حاجة رسول الله ﵌ فهبطت فإذا ماء البئر قد صار كأنه ماء الحنا (4) من السحر ، فطلبته مستعجلا حتى انتهيت إلى أسفل القليب فلم أظفر به ، قال الذين معي : ما فيه شئ فاصعد ، فقلت : لا والله ما كذبت وما كذبت وما نفسي به مثل أنفسكم

"The Prophet sent Ali ibn Abi Talib and said, "Go to the well of Azwan. Labid ibn al-A'sam the Jew placed a spell there against me. Bring it to me." Ali said, "I went to carry out the Messenger of Allah's order. I went down and found that the well's water had become like henna water because of the spell. I searched quickly until I reached the bottom of the well, yet I did not find it. Those with me said, 'There is nothing there. Come back up.' I said, 'By Allah, I have spoken the truth and have been told the truth. I will not act as you do.'""

- Ali , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.92, p.127

What Ali ﵇ drew out of the well carries the whole episode, the knotted cord and the two suras that untied it.

ثم طلبت طلبا بلطف ، فاستخرجت حقا ، فأتيت النبي ﵌ فقال : افتحه ، ففتحته فإذا في الحق قطعة كرب النخل ، في وتر عليها إحدى وعشرون عقدة . وكان جبرئيل ﵇ أنزل يومئذ المعوذتين على النبي ﵌ فقال النبي ﵌ : يا علي اقرأهما على الوتر ، فجعل أمير المؤمنين كلما قرأ آية انحلت عقدة حتى فرغ منها وكشف الله ﷿ عن نبيه ما سحر به وعافاه

"Then I searched carefully and took out a small box. I brought it to the Prophet, and he said, "Open it." I opened it and found a piece of palm fiber in the box. It held a string with twenty-one knots. That day Gabriel brought down the two chapters seeking refuge in Allah to the Prophet. The Prophet said, "Ali, recite them over the string." Each time the Commander of the Faithful recited a verse, one knot came undone. When he finished, Allah removed the spell from His Prophet and restored his health."

- Ali , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.26

And the two angels at the bedside named the sorcerer in the report's other telling.

ويروى أن جبرئيل وميكائيل ﵉ أتيا إلى النبي ﵌ فجلس أحدهما عن يمينه والآخر عن شماله ، فقال جبرئيل لميكائيل : ما وجع الرجل ؟ فقال ميكائيل هو مطبوب ، فقال جبرئيل ﵇ : ومن طبه ؟ قال : لبيد بن أعصم اليهودي

"It is reported that Gabriel and Michael came to the Prophet. One sat on his right and the other on his left. Gabriel asked Michael, "What illness has this man?" Michael answered, "He has been bewitched." Gabriel asked, "Who cast the spell on him?" Michael answered, "Labid ibn al-Asam, the Jew.""

- Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.92, p.128

The Word against the House

After the Prophet's ﷺ death the word was aimed at his own House. Madinat al-Ma'ajiz carries the scene at Quba, in the recension of the Kafi family.

فقال له عمر : ويلك ما أقل عقلك ، فوالله ما أنت فيه الساعة إلا من [بعض] (4) سحر ابن أبي كبشة قد نسيت [سحر] (5) بني هاشم [ومن أين يرجع محمد ولا يرجع من مات ، إنما أنت فيه أعظم من سحر بني هاشم ف‍] (6) تقلد هذا السربال ومر

"Umar said to him, "Woe to you. How little sense you have. By Allah, what you are in now comes from the sorcery of Ibn Abi Kabsha. Have you forgotten the sorcery of Banu Hashim? Where can Muhammad return from when the dead do not return? What you are in is greater than the sorcery of Banu Hashim. Put on this garment and go on.""

- Madinat al-Ma'ajiz, vol.3, p.8

The same scene returns in the recension of Basa'ir al-Darajat.

تبا لامة ولوك أمرهم ، أما تعرف سحر بني هاشم

"Curse the community that lets him rule them. Do you not know the sorcery of Banu Hashim?"

- Madinat al-Ma'ajiz, vol.3, p.9, from Basa'ir al-Darajat

And Ibn Shahrashub's recension carries it to the grave of the Prophet ﷺ himself.

هذا سحر من سحر بني هاشم ، دم على ما أنت عليه ، واخطط مكانك ، ولم يزل به حتى صده عن المراد

"This comes from the sorcery of Banu Hashim. Stay as you are and mark your place. He kept at him until he turned him away from what he wanted."

- Madinat al-Ma'ajiz, vol.3, p.11, from Ibn Shahrashub

The Story Weighed

The corpus does not carry the story at one strength. The Da'a'im al-Islam recension holds its most extreme form.

وجعلاه في مراقي البئر بالمدينة ، فأقام رسول الله ﵌ لا يسمع ولا يبصر ولا يفهم ولا يتكلم ولا يأكل ولا يشرب ، فنزل عليه جبرئيل ﵇ بمعوذات - وساق نحوه إلى قوله - فقطعت يده فكوي منها فمات

"They placed it in the upper part of a well in Medina. The Messenger of Allah remained unable to hear, see, understand, speak, eat, or drink. Gabriel came down to him with two protective suras, and the account continues until it says: His hand was cut off, cauterized, and he died."

- Da'a'im al-Islam, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.25

Abu Basir asked al-Sadiq ﵇ how far the spell was said to reach, and the answer is recorded beside the story.

وما كاد - أو عسى - أن يبلغ من سحره ؟ قال أبو عبد الله الصادق : بلى كان النبي ﵌ يرى أنه يجامع وليس بجامع ، وكان يريد الباب ولا يبصره

"How far did the magic reach? Abu Abd Allah al-Sadiq answered: "It reached the point where the Prophet thought he was having intercourse when he was not. He would go toward a door and fail to see it.""

- al-Sadiq , Tibb al-A'imma, carried in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.92, p.128

Al-Majlisi's own note on the page sets the school's position against the story's strongest form.

أقول : قد مر الكلام في تأثير السحر في الأنبياء والأئمة ﵈ وأن المشهور عدمه

"I have already discussed whether sorcery affects prophets and Imams. The well-known view denies that it does."

- al-Majlisi, Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.25

Al-Tabarsi went further, and his verdict names the reports themselves.

فأما ما روي من الأخبار أن النبي ﵌ سحر ، فكان يرى أنه فعل ما لم يفعله ، وأنه لم يفعل ما فعله ، فأخبار مفتعلة لا يلتفت إليها . وقد قال الله ، ﷾ ، حكاية عن الكفار : (إن تتبعون إلا رجلا مسحورا) فلو كان للسحر عمل فيه ، لكان الكفار صادقين في مقالهم ، حاشا النبي ﵌ ، من كل صفة نقص ، تنفر عن قبول قوله ، فإنه حجة الله على خليقته ، وصفوته على بريته

"Reports claim that the Prophet was bewitched, so he thought he had done things he had not done and had not done things he had done. These reports were invented and deserve no attention. Allah reports the words of the disbelievers: "You follow only a bewitched man." If magic had affected him, the disbelievers would have spoken truthfully. May the Prophet be kept free from every defect that would turn people away from accepting his words. He is Allah’s proof against His creation and His chosen servant among them."

- al-Tabarsi, Majma al-Bayan, vol.1, p.334

His pragmatic disproof of the whole trade closes the weighing.

ولو أن الساحر والمعزم قدرا على نفع أو ضر ، وعلما الغيب ، لقدرا على إزالة . الممالك ، واستخراج الكنوز من معادنها ، والغلبة على البلدان بقتل الملوك ، من غير أن ينالهم مكروه وضرر . فلما رأيناهم أسوأ الناس حالا ، وأكثرهم مكيدة واحتيالا ، علمنا أنهم لا يقدرون على شئ من ذلك

"If sorcerers and spell-casters could bring benefit or harm, and knew the unseen, they could overthrow kingdoms, draw treasures from their mines, and conquer lands by killing kings without suffering harm. We see that they live in the worst conditions and rely most on tricks and schemes. We therefore know that they cannot do any of this."

- al-Tabarsi, Majma al-Bayan, vol.1, p.334

Commentary

The Heavens Shut at the Mission

Source: Qurb al-Isnad via al-Rida ﵇, in Bihar al-Anwar, vol.60, p.75; Nur al-Thaqalayn, vol.3, p.4

Claim: at the Prophet's ﷺ mission the eavesdropping was barred and soothsaying and sorcery lost their power

Speaker: al-Rida ﵇; Musa ibn Ja'far ﵇

The cutoff is a dated event in this history, not a gradual fading. The stonings began with the mission, and the soothsayers went silent because their supply line was cut. The narration to the Jews of Medina makes the point sharper, since it rests on what the Jews themselves accepted in scripture. A craft built on stolen speech dies when the theft becomes impossible.

The Word That Traveled

Source: al-Nur al-Mubin p.420; al-Khara'ij vol.1 p.145-147

Claim: the accusation of sorcery was made against Jesus, and against the Prophet ﷺ over his news, the split moon, and the eaten parchment

Speaker: al-Jaza'iri; al-Rawandi

The mi'raj caravan report, the split moon, and the termite-eaten parchment are three different kinds of sign, a fulfilled prediction, a public miracle in the demanded sky, and a silent verdict on a covenant. All three received the same answer, the word sorcery, and the Qur'an itself preserved that answer against the moon, that they said persistent sorcery. The word never improved its argument across the centuries. It is the whole argument, and Part 2 recorded where it was first refuted, by craftsmen who knew the craft from inside.

Sorcery and Government

Source: Madinat al-Ma'ajiz vol.3 p.8-11

Claim: the visions commanding the yield of the Prophet's ﷺ right were answered with the sorcery of Banu Hashim

Speaker: the Kafi-family chains; Basa'ir al-Darajat; Ibn Shahrashub

The recensions agree on the shape. A vision of the Prophet ﷺ commands the first caliph to yield what he had taken, panic follows, and a counselor dismisses the vision as the House's sorcery. The word that failed against the moon works here as government, a reason not to obey what was seen. The corpus keeps these reports among the miracles of the Prophet ﷺ, and the sorcery-claim against the House stands recorded inside the miracle itself.

The Spell Story in the Corpus

Source: Tibb al-A'imma in Bihar al-Anwar vol.60 p.25-26 and vol.92 p.127-128

Claim: a Jew named Labid ibn al-A'sam bewitched the Prophet ﷺ, the spell sat knotted in a well, and the two shelter suras undid it verse by verse

Speaker: Ali ﵇ via al-Mufaddal and the Tibb chains

The birth vision closed the era this series opened with. The heavens were shut before the ministry began, and the spell story arrives inside that shut world, which is exactly the collision the verdicts address, that the sorcery which lost its power at the mission cannot be the same thing as this spell, and the well-known position and the forged-verdict are the corpus holding that line. The story's details are vivid and mutually close. The well water turned the color of henna, Ali ﵇ searched to the bottom against his companions' doubts, and the container held a palm-frond cord with twenty-one knots. The two suras came down that day, and a knot untied at each verse until the spell was gone. The sorcerer is named, Labid ibn al-A'sam, though the name belongs to the report tradition, and the two well names, Dharwan and Azwan, mark the two recensions. Even the extent of the spell is preserved in the record, in Abu Basir's question and its uncomfortable answer.

The School's Answer

Source: Bihar al-Anwar vol.60 p.25 and vol.92 p.128; Majma al-Bayan vol.1 p.334

Claim: the well-known position denies that sorcery reaches prophets and Imams, and al-Tabarsi calls the bewitched-Prophet reports forged

Speaker: al-Majlisi; al-Tabarsi; the school

The school's mind meets the story on three fronts. Al-Majlisi notes on the page itself that the known position among the scholars is that sorcery has no hold on prophets and Imams. Al-Tabarsi rules that the reports making the Prophet ﷺ imagine deeds he did not do are invented, because otherwise the word of the Quraysh, that he was a bewitched man, would stand true, and the Prophet ﷺ is God's proof, kept from every defect. His pragmatic disproof then turns the trade's own claim against itself, that sorcerers holding the unseen and the power to seize kingdoms would not live as the worst-off schemers on earth. The disproof aims at the trade's grand claim, not at the bounded eye, which the corpus affirms and the shelter answers. The story remains in the corpus, read and taught, beside the verdicts upon it. That is this series' whole method, stated once more at the place it matters most.

What the Accusation Left

The word sorcerer, spent against every prophet, left two things behind. It left the community a warning about what sorcery does to those who practice it and to those they work it on. The Imams meet that craft in combat next, and the costs follow them. And it left the believer a shield, the two suras that came down at the well and the protections taught around them, which is the part after.
