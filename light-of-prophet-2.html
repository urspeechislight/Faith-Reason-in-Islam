Hadith of the Light

Part 2: Twenty Seas and Cosmic Architecture

Bihar al-Anwar, Volume 15, page 28. Continued from Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇.

Imam Ali ibn Abi Talib ﵇ continued:

Then God Almighty created twenty seas of light from the light of Muhammad ﷺ. In each sea was knowledge that no one knows except God Himself. Then He said to the light of Muhammad ﷺ: Descend into the Sea of Glory. So it descended.

The seas, one after another: the Sea of Glory, the Sea of Patience, the Sea of Humility, the Sea of Lowliness, the Sea of Contentment, the Sea of Faithfulness, the Sea of Forbearance, the Sea of God-consciousness, the Sea of Awe, the Sea of Turning Back, the Sea of Action, the Sea of Increase, the Sea of Guidance, the Sea of Purity, the Sea of Modesty, and on through all twenty.

When his light came out of the last of the seas, God Almighty said:

"My beloved, master of My messengers, first of all that I have made, and last of My messengers. You are the intercessor on the Day of Gathering."

At this the light fell prostrate. Then it rose, and from it scattered droplets, one hundred and twenty-four thousand of them. From each droplet God created one of the prophets.

When all the lights were complete, they began to circle the light of Muhammad ﷺ the way pilgrims circle the Sacred House, glorifying God and praising Him, saying:

"Glory to the One who knows and is never ignorant. Glory to the One who is forbearing and never hasty. Glory to the One who is rich and never in need."

Then God called out to them:

"Do you know who I am?"

The light of Muhammad ﷺ answered before all the other lights, calling out:

"You are God. There is no god but You. Alone, with no partner. Lord of lords. King of kings."

And the answer came from the Truth Himself:

"You are My chosen one. You are My beloved. The best of My creation. Your community is the best community brought forth for mankind."

Then God created a single jewel from the light of Muhammad ﷺ and split it in two.

He looked at the first half with the eye of awe, and it turned to fresh water.

He looked at the second half with the eye of tender care, and from it He made the Throne, which settled upon the surface of the water.

From the light of the Throne He made the Footstool. From the light of the Footstool He made the Tablet. From the light of the Tablet He made the Pen.

Then He said to the Pen: Write My oneness.

The Pen stayed there for a thousand years, drunk from the words of God Almighty. When it came to itself, He said: Write. The Pen said: My Lord, what shall I write? He said: Write: There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah.

When the Pen heard the name of Muhammad ﷺ, it fell prostrate and said:

"Glory to the One, the Subduer. Glory to the Great, the Greatest."

Then it lifted its head from prostration and wrote: There is no god but Allah, Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah. Then it said:

"My Lord, who is this Muhammad whose name You have joined to Your own, and whose mention You have set beside Your mention?"

God Almighty said to it:

"O Pen, were it not for him, I would not have created you. I made the whole of creation only for his sake. He is a bringer of good news and a warner. A shining lamp. An intercessor. A beloved."

At this the Pen split open from the sweetness of mentioning Muhammad ﷺ. Then the Pen said:

"Peace be upon you, O Messenger of Allah."

God Almighty replied:

"And upon you peace from Me, and God's mercy and blessings."

And for this reason giving the greeting of peace became a recommended practice, and returning it became an obligation.

Then God said: Write My decree and what I have ordained, and everything I will create until the Day of Resurrection.

Then God created angels who send blessings on Muhammad and the family of Muhammad, and ask forgiveness for his community until the Day of Resurrection.

Then God created Paradise from the light of Muhammad ﷺ, and adorned it with four things: honour, majesty, generosity, and trustworthiness. He made it for His friends and those who obey Him.

Then He looked at the rest of the jewel with the eye of awe, and it melted.

From its vapour He made the heavens, and from its foam He made the earths.

Commentary

The Twenty Seas

This narration picks up where the veils left off and adds another set of pre-existential stations, this time imagined as oceans. Each sea is a moral or spiritual quality: patience, humility, contentment, modesty, and so on. The Prophet's light is no longer just learning the names of God. It is being soaked in the virtues themselves. If the veils built theology, the seas build ethics.

"First of All That I Have Created, and Last of My Messengers"

This phrase compresses the entire doctrine into two clauses. The Prophet is first in creation and last in the chain of messengers. He bookends time. He opens it and he closes it. In Imami theology this is connected to the famous hadith "I was a prophet while Adam was still between water and clay."

The 124,000 Prophets

This number is the traditional figure for the total prophets sent throughout history. The narration roots that number in a single image. Every prophet who ever walked the earth came from a single droplet that fell from the Prophet Muhammad's light when it rose from prostration. This is the basis for the Imami doctrine that the prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ is the source from which all other prophethoods flow.

Circling the Prophet's Light

The image of the prophets circling Muhammad's light the way pilgrims circle the Kaaba is striking. It frames the Hajj as a re-enactment of a primordial scene. When pilgrims walk around the Sacred House, they are mirroring something that happened before time. The Prophet's light is, in this reading, the original Kaaba, and the building in Mecca is its earthly reflection.

The Jewel Split in Two

The narration shifts from creating prophets to creating the cosmos. A jewel made from the Prophet's light is divided. One half becomes water. The other half becomes the Throne. God looks at each half differently. Awe melts one half into water, the foundation. Tender care shapes the other into the Throne, the seat of sovereignty.

The Pen Splits Open

The detail about the Pen splitting from the sweetness of saying Muhammad's name is the basis for the visible split in the tip of a writing pen. Traditional commentators read this as a literal etymology for why pens have that shape. Every pen, in this reading, bears the mark of that first moment of love.

Why Peace Is Greeted

The narration grounds the entire ritual of salam in a single exchange. When the Pen said "peace be upon you" to the Prophet, God Himself answered on his behalf. From that exchange, the rule was set: giving peace is a recommended practice (sunna), and answering it is an obligation (farida).

Paradise from His Light, the Heavens from the Rest

Paradise is created from the Prophet's pure light. The heavens and the earths are created from what was left of the jewel after it melted. This is a hierarchy of origin. What is closest to the Prophet's light becomes the highest reward. What is further from it becomes the physical universe we know.
